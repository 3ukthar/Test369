# Admission requirements, documents, legalisation and application mechanics for Indian bachelor's graduates applying to Austrian master's programmes (state as of 10 November 2026)

Research notes. All pages were accessed on 10 November 2026. Most university pages carry no "last updated" date, so the access date is the only date unless one is stated. "UNVERIFIED" marks anything not confirmed on an official page.

Access notes (which sources needed a real browser, which stayed unreachable) are at the end of the last section's Gaps.

## 1. Academic eligibility: what "subject-relevant bachelor's equivalent to 180 ECTS" means for Indian degrees (3-year vs 4-year, GPA floors, supplementary exams, work experience)

### Takeaway
Three-year Indian bachelor's degrees (B.Sc., B.Com., BA, BBA, BCA) are not rejected for length: the national ENIC NARIC recommendation and the University of Vienna, TU Graz, Uni Graz and JKU all accept "at least three years, full-time", but only with at least 60% aggregate and only from institutions in the right category (Section 1 or 2; Section 3 only if a state university and, at Vienna and TU Graz, with 80%). Since winter semester 2026/27 most public universities also make Indian applicants sit an in-person "competence check" at VFS Education Services in India before applying; TU Wien and WU do not list this requirement.

### Cited Findings

**Legal basis and national recommendation**
- § 64 Abs. 3 UG: the general university entrance qualification for a master's is proven by completing a subject-relevant bachelor's or another relevant degree of at least the same level; the Rectorate may prescribe supplementary examinations to offset substantial subject differences, to be passed by the end of the second semester of the master's. Version in force since 01.05.2024, legislation status considered 10.11.2026 — [jusline § 64 UG](https://www.jusline.at/gesetz/univg/paragraf/64)
- ENIC NARIC AUSTRIA, in agreement with the science ministry (BMFWF), publishes a 2026 "Empfehlung für den Universitäts-/Hochschulzugang" for Afghanistan, Bangladesh, India, Nepal and Sri Lanka. It is marked "Stand: März 2026" and "ab 1. März 2026 bis auf Weiteres maßgeblich" (authoritative from 1 March 2026 until further notice) — [OeAD / ENIC NARIC PDF](https://oead.at/fileadmin/Dokumente/oead.at/OeAD_Hauptmenueseiten/Studieren_Forschen_Lehren/ENIC_NARIC_PDFs/Asien__Auswahl___Universitaets-_und_Hochschulzugang_mit_Ausbildungsabschluessen.pdf)
- The India section of that recommendation (master's access, "Hilfsinstrument: CEP") says:
  - check the status of the university and the continuous affiliation of the college during the whole study period;
  - Section 1 and Section 2 bachelor's degrees (at least 3 study years, from 60% of maximum marks) reach bachelor level: direct access to a master's is possible;
  - Section 3 bachelor's degrees (at least 3 years, from 60%): at state institutions, direct access from 80%; between 60% and 80%, 2 supplementary exams or exams of up to 20 ECTS are to be imposed; at private institutions, no admission to a master's is possible (admission to a bachelor's with credit transfer is possible);
  - a 2-year bachelor's (from 70%) plus a 2-year master's (from 70%) can be upgraded for master's admission;
  - a 3-year bachelor's with 50–60% plus a 2-year master's (from 70%) can be upgraded for master's admission;
  - supplementary exams to test knowledge are "generally possible".
  — [OeAD / ENIC NARIC PDF](https://oead.at/fileadmin/Dokumente/oead.at/OeAD_Hauptmenueseiten/Studieren_Forschen_Lehren/ENIC_NARIC_PDFs/Asien__Auswahl___Universitaets-_und_Hochschulzugang_mit_Ausbildungsabschluessen.pdf)
- The same recommendation gives the percentage formula (sum of marks obtained ÷ sum of maximum marks × 100) for transcripts that show no official percentage, and says universities may hold an (online) entrance exam, demand additional language proof, and ask master's applicants for the school-leaving certificate as well as the bachelor's — [OeAD / ENIC NARIC PDF](https://oead.at/fileadmin/Dokumente/oead.at/OeAD_Hauptmenueseiten/Studieren_Forschen_Lehren/ENIC_NARIC_PDFs/Asien__Auswahl___Universitaets-_und_Hochschulzugang_mit_Ausbildungsabschluessen.pdf)
- ENIC NARIC AUSTRIA states it cannot give a pre-assessment of foreign study programmes and points to the German anabin database for checking institutions (no liability assumed) — [OeAD ENIC NARIC page](https://oead.at/en/study-research-teaching/coming-to-austria-information-and-services/recognition-and-certifications-enic-naric-austria)

**University-by-university treatment of Indian bachelor's degrees (they differ)**
- University of Vienna (master's, India): application "can be processed" if the bachelor's has a stipulated duration of at least three years full-time at a recognised university AND the university is Section 1 or 2 (per ENIC NARIC AUSTRIA Credential Evaluation) or a state university in Section 3 AND the applicant achieved at least 60% (Section 1 or 2) or 80% (Section 3) of maximum marks. "Each application must be reviewed individually." — [univie competence check page](https://studieren.univie.ac.at/en/applying-for-a-programme/international-students/country-specific-requirements-for-admission/competence-check-at-vfs-education-services)
- TU Graz (master's, India): at least three years full-time at a recognised university AND Section 1 or 2 AND at least 60%; OR a Section 3 state university AND at least 80%. — [TU Graz master's with international degree](https://www.tugraz.at/en/studying-and-teaching/studying-at-tu-graz/prospective-students/admission/masters-programme-without-admission-procedure-with-an-international-degree)
- University of Graz (master's, India): at least three years full-time at a recognised university in India AND Section 1 or 2 AND at least 60%. No Section 3 route is listed. "Meeting these requirements and passing the Competency Check do not guarantee admission." — [Uni Graz competency check](https://internationale-studierende.uni-graz.at/en/application-masters-studies/competency-check-master/)
- JKU Linz (master's, India): duration at least three years, at least 60% of maximum marks, "from a recognized state higher education institution", and same or closely related field. — [JKU master's registration](https://www.jku.at/en/degree-programs/prospective-students/register-to-enroll/masters-degree-programs/)
- University of Innsbruck: applications for a master's based on a bachelor's completed in India "will be reviewed individually"; no fixed criteria and no VFS check are listed. — [UIBK third-country information](https://www.uibk.ac.at/en/admission-department/admission/information-on-third-countries/)
- TU Wien: no India-specific criteria are published on the master's admission page or FAQ. General rule: bachelor's in a relevant subject from a recognised institution with at least 180 ECTS; the Dean of Academic Affairs assesses equivalence; differences of up to 30 ECTS are imposed as supplementary examinations, above 30 ECTS admission is not possible. — [TU Wien master's admission](https://www.tuwien.at/en/studies/admission/masters-programmes/other-inter-national-bachelors-degree)
- TU Wien FAQ, "Does my GPA allow admission?": degrees are "continuously re-evaluated" per ministry recommendations and eligibility "can only be determined during verification. Therefore, an application for admission is necessary." — [TU Wien FAQ](https://www.tuwien.at/en/tu-wien/organisation/central-divisions/admission-office/contact/faqs)
- WU Vienna (example: MSc Marketing): programme of at least 180 ECTS (three academic years), with 30 ECTS in business administration/economics and 8 ECTS in mathematics/statistics/quantitative methods. — [WU application guide, Marketing, international degree, step 1](https://www.wu.ac.at/en/programs/application-and-admission/your-journey-starts-here/0/19/20/2/1?cHash=603d4d5db021bb4873ab46c530a05af4)
- WU requires the transcript to show workload per course and the legally required programme duration and total credits in the same unit; evaluation is "NOT possible" if overall workload and per-course workload are in different units or if the information is not in an official stamped and signed document. — [WU information for evaluation](https://www.wu.ac.at/en/programs/application-and-admission/important-information-for-evaluation)
- UAS Technikum Wien (master's): bachelor's from a UAS in a relevant subject or an equivalent degree from a recognised foreign post-secondary institution (at least 180 ECTS); the programme director can require exams during the master's to establish full equivalence. — [FHTW admission requirements](https://www.technikum-wien.at/en/student-guide/requirements/)

**Supplementary examinations (Auflagen)**
- University of Vienna: admission letter outcomes are admission without supplementary exams, admission with subject-specific supplementary exams from the bachelor's (maximum 30 ECTS, completed within two semesters after admission), or rejection for missing pertinence. Which exams will be prescribed cannot be known before applying. — [univie master's admission](https://studieren.univie.ac.at/en/admission/master/external); [univie country-specific FAQ](https://studieren.univie.ac.at/en/applying-for-a-programme/international-students/country-specific-requirements-for-admission)
- TU Wien: supplementary courses must be completed within the first two semesters, and because all TU Wien bachelor's programmes are taught in German, "these supplementary courses too will be in German". — [TU Wien additional and supplementary examinations](https://www.tuwien.at/en/studies/admission/masters-programmes/other-inter-national-bachelors-degree/additional-and-supplementary-examinations)
- JKU: letters of admission may carry conditional requirements (additional courses during studies) and/or a supplementary German exam. — [JKU master's registration](https://www.jku.at/en/degree-programs/prospective-students/register-to-enroll/masters-degree-programs/)

**The VFS competence check (new, mandatory for Indian-educated applicants at most public universities)**
- University of Vienna: mandatory for all applicants who completed previous education in Bangladesh, India or Pakistan, for bachelor's/diploma and master's. In person at VFS Education Services locations. Fee "around € 160" paid to VFS (competence check and onSET). VFS checks identity (passport) and admission documents and assesses German (for German-taught programmes), English, Mathematics, Biology, Chemistry, History/Geography and Physics. VFS sends results directly to the university; the applicant uploads the confirmation of participation under "Further documents and proofs" in u:space. It does not replace the Admission Office's review or German proof. — [univie competence check page](https://studieren.univie.ac.at/en/applying-for-a-programme/international-students/country-specific-requirements-for-admission/competence-check-at-vfs-education-services)
- TU Graz: mandatory from winter semester 2026/27 for Bangladesh, India, Pakistan; must be taken "before submitting an application"; fee around € 100; the confirmation of participation is uploaded during registration. — [TU Graz competence check](https://www.tugraz.at/en/studying-and-teaching/studying-at-tu-graz/prospective-students/admission/competence-check-at-vfs-global)
- JKU Linz: mandatory for those starting in summer semester 2027 or later; fee around € 100; proof uploaded in MyAdmission. — [JKU competence check](https://www.jku.at/en/degree-programs/students/competence-check-at-vfs-global/)
- BOKU: mandatory from winter semester 2026/27 for Bangladesh, India, Pakistan and Nigeria; fee around € 100. — [BOKU country page](https://boku.ac.at/en/studienservices/themen/zulassung/international-educational-background/masters-programmes/admission-to-a-masters-programme-with-international-educational-background/country-of-education/pakistan-bangladesh-india-nigeria)
- University of Graz: the result must be received BEFORE applying; "Applications without a positive Competency Check result by the time of applying cannot be processed"; a minimum of 50% of maximum marks is required, otherwise "you will not be eligible for admission under any circumstances"; fee around € 100. — [Uni Graz competency check](https://internationale-studierende.uni-graz.at/en/application-masters-studies/competency-check-master/)
- The VFS registration form (title "VFS Education Services- Competency Test Registration") lists these intended universities: University of Vienna, University of Klagenfurt, BOKU University, TU Leoben, University of Salzburg, University of Graz, Technical University of Graz, Johannes Kepler University. TU Wien, WU and the University of Innsbruck are not on the list. Test types: "Competency check" or "Competency check + Onset German language test". — [VFS registration form](https://forms.office.com/r/GVtNDShBqm)
- Separately, VFS Education Services signed an agreement (media release dated 3 July 2025) with TU Leoben, TU Wien and TU Graz for Indian engineering graduates "from recognised colleges under the ANABIN list"; selection is based on grades, English proficiency, one-to-one assessments and document verification, with B.Tech certificates sent to austria@vfsedu.com. — [VFS media release](https://www.vfsglobal.com/en/PDF/media-releases/2025/VFS_Education_Services_collaborates_with_Austrias_leading_Technical_Universities.pdf)

**Work experience**
- WU's selection for MSc Marketing combines a document review ("CV, GMAT/GRE or GPA, motivation letter, experience abroad, work experience, etc.") and a video interview; a recommendation letter may come from a work supervisor. — [WU application guide, step 2](https://www.wu.ac.at/en/programs/application-and-admission/your-journey-starts-here/0/19/20/2/2?cHash=8d204e5699ce88327bd9d1999745e530)
- UAS Technikum Wien's master's procedure is an online subject-specific placement test plus an interview with the programme director; a CV with photo is required. — [FHTW admissions process](https://www.technikum-wien.at/en/student-guide/admissions-process/); [FHTW third-country document list](https://www.technikum-wien.at/en/ma-click-through-third-country/)
- MCI lists "relevant evidence of experience or further education" among general application documents and holds an online admission interview. — [MCI application and admission](https://www.mci.edu/en/study-at-mci/application-admission)

### Inferences
- A 4-year B.Tech/B.E. is treated under the same India rule as a 3-year degree (the floor is "at least three years"); the extra year gives no formal advantage in the published criteria. What matters is the institution's Section, state vs private status, aggregate percentage and subject match.
- The riskiest profile is a graduate of a private "Section 3" institution: the national recommendation says no master's admission is possible, and the University of Vienna, TU Graz and JKU wording (state university required for Section 3 / "recognized state higher education institution") matches that.
- A graduate with 50–60% has no direct route; the recommendation only opens one via an additional 2-year Indian master's with 70%+.
- "CEP" in the recommendation is probably the Australian Government's Country Education Profiles, which classify Indian institutions into Sections 1–3. UNVERIFIED: the Austrian document does not expand the abbreviation and no public Austrian list of Indian universities by Section was found.
- TU Wien and WU appear to be outside the competence-check scheme as of November 2026 (absent from the VFS form and no mention on their pages), but TU Wien is in the separate VFS "Focus India" channel. Whether TU Wien will adopt the check later is unknown.
- Work experience is not a formal eligibility criterion at public research universities; it counts in selection at WU and in interviews at UAS.

### Gaps
- No public list mapping individual Indian universities to Section 1/2/3 was found. Applicants cannot self-check their Section from Austrian sources; they would have to ask the admission office. This is the single biggest eligibility unknown.
- VFS test centres/cities in India, test dates, exact fee in INR, pass mark at universities other than Uni Graz (50%), and validity period of a result were not found on official pages.
- Whether CGPA-only transcripts (10-point scale) are converted by a set formula was not found beyond the generic percentage formula.
- AQ Austria publishes nothing India-specific that was found; it was not searched in depth.
- RIS (the official legal database) blocked automated access, so § 64 UG was read on jusline, a commercial mirror.

## 2. Document legalisation for India: apostille or full diplomatic legalisation, the exact chain, costs, time, and which documents

### Takeaway
Austria accepts the Indian MEA apostille; no step at the Austrian Embassy New Delhi is needed, and Austria never objected to India's accession (Germany did, and its objection still stands, so German guidance does not transfer). The Indian chain is state-level authentication, then MEA apostille through one of four outsourced agencies (MEA fee Rs 50 per apostille plus Rs 84 per document and Rs 3 per page agency charges); universities insist that the apostille certifies the issuing institution's signature, not a notary's copy certification.

### Cited Findings

**Austria's position**
- Austrian Embassy New Delhi: under the 1961 Hague Convention, Austria and India recognise each other's public documents; they need no prior legalisation by an Embassy or Consulate but must be authenticated by an apostille. In India the apostille is issued by the Ministry of External Affairs (mea.gov.in/apostille.htm). — [Austrian Embassy New Delhi, Legalization](https://www.bmeia.gv.at/en/austrian-embassy-new-delhi/service-for-citizens/legalization)
- The ministry-level "Beglaubigungsliste Hochschulwesen" (issued by the Federal Ministry for Women, Science and Research, agreed with the foreign ministry, footer "ENIC NARIC AUSTRIA | April 2026") lists "India (BGBl. III No. 154/2005)" under "Legalization in the form of the Apostille". — [Legalisation list, English, April 2026](https://oead.at/fileadmin/Dokumente/oead.at/OeAD_Hauptmenueseiten/Studieren_Forschen_Lehren/ENIC_NARIC_PDFs/2.4.13.6E_Beglaubigungsliste_ab_2024.pdf); German version dated October 2025: [Beglaubigungsliste Hochschulwesen](https://oead.at/fileadmin/Dokumente/oead.at/OeAD_Hauptmenueseiten/Studieren_Forschen_Lehren/ENIC_NARIC_PDFs/Beglaubigungsliste_Hochschulwesen.pdf)
- Same list: only the foreign ministry or specially empowered authorities in the issuing country can apostille; the country's embassy in Austria "is under no circumstances authorized to do it". E-apostilles are equivalent to paper apostilles. — [Legalisation list, April 2026](https://oead.at/fileadmin/Dokumente/oead.at/OeAD_Hauptmenueseiten/Studieren_Forschen_Lehren/ENIC_NARIC_PDFs/2.4.13.6E_Beglaubigungsliste_ab_2024.pdf)
- HCCH status table: India acceded 26-X-2004, entry into force 14-VII-2005, flagged as having objections. — [HCCH status table](https://www.hcch.net/en/instruments/conventions/status-table/?cid=41)
- HCCH notifications for India: objections were raised by Belgium (withdrawn 9 January 2008), Germany (21-04-2005, no withdrawal recorded), Finland (withdrawn 5 October 2009), the Netherlands (withdrawn 16 September 2008) and Spain (withdrawn 12 February 2008); Portugal's arrived late and has no legal effect. Austria is not listed as an objecting state. — [HCCH notifications, India](https://www.hcch.net/en/instruments/conventions/status-table/notifications/?csid=929&disp=type)
- Embassy of India, Vienna: apostilled Indian documents "are not required to be 're-authenticated' either by the Austrian Embassy/ Consulate in India"; the Indian Embassy itself does not apostille. — [Embassy of India Vienna](https://eoivienna.gov.in/pages/NTA,)

**University country lists (all put India under apostille)**
- University of Vienna: "India: Apostille"; and on the India page, "Your documents have to be legalised by apostille and must be translated into either German or English afterwards." — [univie legalisation](https://studieren.univie.ac.at/en/applying-for-a-programme/international-students/legalisation); [univie competence check page](https://studieren.univie.ac.at/en/applying-for-a-programme/international-students/country-specific-requirements-for-admission/competence-check-at-vfs-education-services)
- TU Wien: India is in the Apostille (Hague Convention) group; "an Apostille issued in the country which issued the original document is sufficient"; electronic apostilles are recognised. — [TU Wien legalisation](https://www.tuwien.at/en/studies/admission/masters-programmes/other-inter-national-bachelors-degree/legalisation-and-translations-of-documents)
- TU Graz: selecting India in the country list leads to "Contracting States of the Hague Convention" (apostille). — [TU Graz Hague states page](https://www.tugraz.at/index.php?id=7697&L=1)
- JKU: India is listed among Hague Convention states whose documents "must be verified with an APOSTILLE". — [JKU master's registration](https://www.jku.at/en/degree-programs/prospective-students/register-to-enroll/masters-degree-programs/)
- WU: India links to "Legalization and translation: Apostille". — [WU apostille page](https://www.wu.ac.at/en/programs/application-and-admission/legalize/apost)
- UAS Technikum Wien: India links to the apostille rule page. — [FHTW country page for India](https://www.technikum-wien.at/en/list-of-countries/legalization-2/)
- MCI: Hague Convention states need "legalization of documents in the form of an apostille or e-apostille". — [MCI internationals](https://www.mci4me.at/en/international/internationals-full-degree)

**What the apostille must certify (the most common formal trap)**
- University of Vienna: "The apostille must attest any signature(s) by the issuing educational institution (directorate, rectorate, or deanship) that the document bears and not the signature of the notary or translation agency." Do not detach the apostille. — [univie legalisation](https://studieren.univie.ac.at/en/applying-for-a-programme/international-students/legalisation)
- JKU: "The Apostille needs to confirm the signature on the original document not the signature of the notary." — [JKU master's registration](https://www.jku.at/en/degree-programs/prospective-students/register-to-enroll/masters-degree-programs/)
- TU Graz: it "can only accept documents on which the educational authority or the notary explicitly confirms that the signature of the person issuing the document is authentic. A certification which only confirms that the document is a faithful representation of the original is not sufficient". Documents of private institutions may count as private documents and must first be certified by the education authority or a notary before the apostille. — [TU Graz Hague states page](https://www.tugraz.at/index.php?id=7697&L=1)
- WU: identical rule; documents where the authority confirmed "only that the copy is identical to the original cannot be accepted". — [WU apostille page](https://www.wu.ac.at/en/programs/application-and-admission/legalize/apost)

**The Indian side of the chain (MEA)**
- MEA: apostille is done for personal documents (birth, death, marriage certificates, affidavits, powers of attorney) and educational documents (degree, diploma, matriculation and secondary certificates). — [MEA Attestation / Apostille](https://www.mea.gov.in/apostille-menu.htm)
- MEA procedure for documents not covered by e-Sanad: Step 1, authentication of documents by the Regional Authentication Centres (RACs, designated state/UT authorities); Step 2, deposit with an authorised outsourced service provider; Step 3, collect the apostilled document from the agency. — [MEA Attestation / Apostille](https://www.mea.gov.in/apostille-menu.htm)
- The four outsourced agencies named: M/s BLS International Services Ltd., M/s Superb Enterprises Pvt. Ltd., M/s IVS Global Services Private Ltd., M/s Alhind Tours & Travels Pvt. Ltd. (The same page elsewhere says "five" providers; the list names four.) — [MEA Attestation / Apostille](https://www.mea.gov.in/apostille-menu.htm)
- MEA accepts no documents directly from individuals (since July 2012); submit the original with a photocopy and a passport photocopy to an agency. "The Ministry does not legalize photocopies." "Applicants are advised not to rely on unauthorised persons/touts." — [MEA Attestation / Apostille](https://www.mea.gov.in/apostille-menu.htm)
- Since 1 January 2019 the service is decentralised to Branch Secretariats/RPOs in 16 cities: Ahmedabad, Bengaluru, Bhopal, Chennai, Chandigarh, Cochin, New Delhi, Guwahati, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Lucknow, Mumbai, Panaji, Jaipur, Patna, Thiruvananthapuram. — [MEA Attestation / Apostille](https://www.mea.gov.in/apostille-menu.htm)
- Fees: MEA Rs 50 per apostille sticker; outsourced agency service charge Rs 84 per document and scanning charge Rs 3 per page. Normal attestation (non-Hague countries) is free at MEA. — [MEA Attestation / Apostille](https://www.mea.gov.in/apostille-menu.htm)
- Timing: MEA gives no number of days, only "apply well in advance as Ministry needs some time to verify and process the documents". — [MEA Attestation / Apostille](https://www.mea.gov.in/apostille-menu.htm)
- MEA "legalises the documents on the basis of the signature of the designated signing authorities of the State Government/Union Territory" and "does not take responsibility of the contents". — [MEA Attestation / Apostille](https://www.mea.gov.in/apostille-menu.htm)
- The MEA list of RACs names, state by state, Home/General Administration and higher or technical education department officers. For Delhi it states: "SDM of Delhi has been authorised as pre-authenticated authority for all kind of documents (except commercial documents) (subject to acceptance by the concerned Foreign Mission) on behalf of all states for the time being". — [MEA RAC list PDF](https://www.mea.gov.in/images/RAC_Updated_DATA.pdf)
- e-Sanad: online verification and apostille for documents held in a digital depository (it started with CBSE documents from 2014 onwards); documents without a digital depository follow the physical procedure. — [MEA e-Sanad note](https://www.mea.gov.in/images/e_sanad_website.pdf)

**Which documents need the apostille**
- University of Vienna, master's: the diploma, the relevant transcripts of records and/or diploma supplement, and "where applicable, proof of the national university entrance examination". — [univie legalisation](https://studieren.univie.ac.at/en/applying-for-a-programme/international-students/legalisation)
- University of Vienna: you cannot apply without the required legalisation. If the degree certificate is not yet issued, a confirmation of completion from the institution plus final transcripts is accepted "as long as this also contains the legalisation required for your country". "Provisional transcripts cannot be accepted", and confirmations that you will complete cannot be accepted (except master's with selection procedure). — [univie country-specific FAQ](https://studieren.univie.ac.at/en/applying-for-a-programme/international-students/country-specific-requirements-for-admission)
- TU Wien: degree certificate and transcript/diploma supplement as "officially legalised copy". Language certificates do not require legalisation; transcripts of records do. "Documents without diplomatic certification have no validity and therefore cannot be processed." — [TU Wien master's admission](https://www.tuwien.at/en/studies/admission/masters-programmes/other-inter-national-bachelors-degree); [TU Wien FAQ](https://www.tuwien.at/en/tu-wien/organisation/central-divisions/admission-office/contact/faqs)
- Residence permit side: the Austrian Embassy New Delhi says applicants submit originals plus one set of photocopies, "All documents must be legalised", and documents not in German need a certified German translation. — [Austrian Embassy New Delhi, Studying in Austria](https://www.bmeia.gv.at/en/austrian-embassy-new-delhi/travels-to-austria/studying-in-austria)

**Originals, certified copies, scans: universities differ**
- University of Vienna: upload scans of all pages including seals, apostille and any translation as one PDF in u:space. — [univie legalisation](https://studieren.univie.ac.at/en/applying-for-a-programme/international-students/legalisation)
- TU Wien: send notary- or court-certified copies of the legalised documents, never originals; "Simple photocopies will not be accepted"; certified copies made in any country by a notary or competent authority are accepted. — [TU Wien legalisation](https://www.tuwien.at/en/studies/admission/masters-programmes/other-inter-national-bachelors-degree/legalisation-and-translations-of-documents)
- TU Graz: after the online application, "Send the complete application, including all original documents, by post". — [TU Graz master's with international degree](https://www.tugraz.at/en/studying-and-teaching/studying-at-tu-graz/prospective-students/admission/masters-programme-without-admission-procedure-with-an-international-degree)
- JKU: upload without legalisation first; only on request send either notarised copies of the duly legalised originals (not returned) or the legalised originals (returned by courier at your cost, by Austrian post at JKU's cost, or by pick-up). — [JKU master's registration](https://www.jku.at/en/degree-programs/prospective-students/register-to-enroll/masters-degree-programs/)
- WU: upload PDFs to apply; present originals and copies in person at enrolment. — [WU application guide, step 3](https://www.wu.ac.at/en/programs/application-and-admission/your-journey-starts-here/0/19/20/2/3?cHash=64a95e173c1441ebbd9aaea9286ffc5e)
- UAS Technikum Wien: certifications and translations should be uploaded at application; certifications can be submitted later "but no later than November 15th". — [FHTW legalisation, third country](https://www.technikum-wien.at/legalization-of-foreign-documents-ma-third-country/)

### Inferences
- Correct chain for an Indian degree for Austrian universities: (1) original degree certificate and consolidated/all-semester marksheets; (2) authentication by the designated state authority (the state education/"HRD" department route verifies with the issuing university); (3) MEA apostille via BLS, Superb, IVS or Alhind; (4) nothing at the Austrian Embassy; (5) no translation if the documents are in English; (6) scan every page front and back including the apostille sticker.
- The SDM (Delhi) route is an official MEA-recognised shortcut, but MEA itself qualifies it as "subject to acceptance by the concerned Foreign Mission", and it usually rests on a notary's attestation. Because Vienna, JKU, TU Graz and WU reject apostilles that only certify a notary's or a copy's authenticity, the state education department route is the safer choice for degree documents. Whether Austrian universities actually reject SDM-route apostilles is UNVERIFIED.
- Apostille the original, not a photocopy: MEA does not legalise photocopies, and Austrian universities want the apostille on the original or an attached sheet. For TU Wien, make notarised copies after the apostille is on the original.
- Germany's standing objection means apostilles are not valid between India and Germany. Advice aimed at Germany-bound students (embassy verification, APS) does not apply to Austria.
- For the university, only degree and transcripts need the apostille. Birth certificate and police clearance certificate matter for the residence permit, where the Embassy's blanket "all documents must be legalised" applies; by the same Hague logic that means apostille.

### Gaps
- State-level authentication fees and processing times (they vary by state), and realistic end-to-end duration, were not found on an official page. Consultancy claims of 4–6 weeks are UNVERIFIED.
- MEA's page is undated; the Rs 50 / Rs 84 / Rs 3 fee table is what the live page showed on 10 November 2026. University e-Sanad pages cite other figures (Rs 90 per apostille), which were not verified on MEA's site.
- Whether a provisional degree certificate is accepted elsewhere than Vienna (which accepts a legalised confirmation of completion but not provisional transcripts) was not checked per university.
- The Austrian foreign ministry's own country list page was reached, but India's entry was not located on it; the Embassy page and the ministry legalisation list were used instead.
- The exact list of personal documents the Embassy requires apostilled for the residence permit (birth certificate, PCC) was not confirmed here; it belongs to the visa research strand.

## 3. Translation rules: are English documents accepted, and when is a sworn German translation needed

### Takeaway
Every institution checked accepts documents issued in English without translation; a sworn translation is needed only for documents in another language (for example a degree certificate in Hindi or a regional language), and it must be made after the apostille and stay physically attached. The residence permit is different: the Embassy asks for certified German translations of documents not in German.

### Cited Findings
- University of Vienna: "We only accept documents in German or English." Other languages must be translated by "translators who are sworn in and certified by the courts"; the translation must be inseparably attached to the original, "Do not detach them – not even when scanning!" — [univie legalisation](https://studieren.univie.ac.at/en/applying-for-a-programme/international-students/legalisation)
- TU Wien: documents not in German or English need a translation by a court-certified interpreter, inextricably linked to the original; sworn or certified translators from any country are accepted; translate after legalisation because the legalisation notes must be translated too. "The translation alone is not sufficient." — [TU Wien legalisation](https://www.tuwien.at/en/studies/admission/masters-programmes/other-inter-national-bachelors-degree/legalisation-and-translations-of-documents); [TU Wien FAQ](https://www.tuwien.at/en/tu-wien/organisation/central-divisions/admission-office/contact/faqs)
- TU Graz: all documents (including curricula) must be in German or English; all stamps and seals must be translated, "The apostille itself does not have to be translated"; a translation made in the issuing country must itself be apostilled, one made by an Austrian court interpreter need not be. — [TU Graz Hague states page](https://www.tugraz.at/index.php?id=7697&L=1)
- WU: same two procedures (translation in Austria: apostille, then translation; translation in the issuing country: apostille, translation, then apostille of the translation). — [WU apostille page](https://www.wu.ac.at/en/programs/application-and-admission/legalize/apost)
- JKU: documents not in German or English must be translated by a court sworn translator after legalisation. — [JKU master's registration](https://www.jku.at/en/degree-programs/prospective-students/register-to-enroll/masters-degree-programs/)
- UAS Technikum Wien: translations by a sworn, judicially certified translator; translations made abroad are treated like foreign originals and need that country's legalisation. — [FHTW country page for India](https://www.technikum-wien.at/en/list-of-countries/legalization-2/)
- § 60 Abs. 2 UG requires translations of foreign-language documents by generally sworn and court-certified interpreters. — [jusline § 60 UG](https://www.jusline.at/gesetz/univg/paragraf/60)
- Residence permit: documents not in German must be submitted with a certified German translation. — [Austrian Embassy New Delhi, Studying in Austria](https://www.bmeia.gv.at/en/austrian-embassy-new-delhi/travels-to-austria/studying-in-austria)

### Inferences
- Most Indian university degree certificates and marksheets are in English or bilingual, so no translation is needed for admission. A certificate printed only in Hindi or a regional language needs a sworn translation, and if done in India the translation needs its own apostille at TU Graz, WU and FHTW.
- The German-translation requirement for the residence permit is stricter than the universities' rule, so an applicant may need German translations of the same English documents later.

### Gaps
- Whether the Embassy in practice accepts English-language documents for the residence permit without German translation was not verified; the official page says German.
- Who counts as a "sworn" translator in India (there is no court-sworn translator register comparable to Austria's) is not addressed on any page read.

## 4. Is the "special university entrance qualification" (proof of eligibility for the same master's at home) still required for master's applicants from India

### Takeaway
No public university checked asks Indian master's applicants for a home-country study-place or eligibility certificate; the requirement that survives is academic (Section, percentage, competence check). The term "besondere Universitätsreife" now means only the additional school-level exams for certain bachelor's fields.

### Cited Findings
- § 64 Abs. 3 UG addresses only the general university entrance qualification for master's, proven by the completed bachelor's. — [jusline § 64 UG](https://www.jusline.at/gesetz/univg/paragraf/64)
- § 65 UG as shown on the official legal database defines "besondere Universitätsreife" as the additional exams to the school-leaving exam laid down in the University Entrance Qualification Ordinance (UBVO 1998) for the studies named there. — [RIS, § 65 UG](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/Dokumente/Bundesnormen/NOR40261566/NOR40261566.html) (read via search result excerpt; the site blocked direct automated access)
- University of Vienna's required documents for master's admission: passport/ID, degree certificate and transcripts with legalisation, language proof per the Master Access Guide, and further curriculum-specific documents. No home-country eligibility certificate is listed. — [univie master's admission](https://studieren.univie.ac.at/en/admission/master/external)
- University of Vienna: "If you have already completed a bachelor's or diploma programme (minimum degree programme duration of three years or a minimum of 180 ECTS), you do not need to provide proof of having passed the university entrance examination." — [univie country-specific requirements](https://studieren.univie.ac.at/en/applying-for-a-programme/international-students/country-specific-requirements-for-admission)
- TU Wien's master's document list (application form, degree certificate, transcript/diploma supplement with ECTS index and contact hours, language proof, passport copy) contains no such certificate. — [TU Wien master's admission](https://www.tuwien.at/en/studies/admission/masters-programmes/other-inter-national-bachelors-degree)
- UAS Technikum Wien does ask master's applicants for proof of the general university entrance qualification (school-leaving certificate) "for educational statistical reasons", even with a completed degree. — [FHTW third-country document list](https://www.technikum-wien.at/en/ma-click-through-third-country/)
- ENIC NARIC recommendation: for master's admission universities may also demand the applicant's school-leaving certificate. — [OeAD / ENIC NARIC PDF](https://oead.at/fileadmin/Dokumente/oead.at/OeAD_Hauptmenueseiten/Studieren_Forschen_Lehren/ENIC_NARIC_PDFs/Asien__Auswahl___Universitaets-_und_Hochschulzugang_mit_Ausbildungsabschluessen.pdf)

### Inferences
- An Indian applicant should keep Class 10 and Class 12 marksheets ready (FHTW requires the latter; others may ask), but does not need a letter from an Indian university confirming eligibility for a master's there.

### Gaps
- The date and amending act that removed the old "proof of study place in the home country" requirement were not verified on a primary source.
- The JKU and TU Graz document lists were read and contain no such item, but neither page states explicitly that it is not required.

## 5. Language requirements: English proof and scores, Medium of Instruction waivers, German level at application vs enrolment, and the VWU route

### Takeaway
English requirements differ sharply: B2 at the University of Vienna (IELTS 6.5 / TOEFL 94) and TU Wien (IELTS 6.5 / TOEFL 87), C1 at TU Graz (IELTS 7.0 / TOEFL 95) and WU (IELTS 7.0 / TOEFL 100). An English-medium Indian degree or Medium of Instruction letter is not accepted at Vienna, TU Wien, TU Graz, JKU or WU, because the exemption is limited to degrees from a closed list of countries that excludes India. For German-taught master's, A2 is needed at application and C1 before enrolment, with the university preparation programme (VWU/VGUH) available to master's applicants only for the German exam.

### Cited Findings

**English**
- University of Vienna: most English-taught master's need B2 at application. Accepted, not older than three years: TOEFL iBT 94 or higher; IELTS Academic overall 6.5; Cambridge FCE or CAE at result level B2; a B2 exam from a university language centre in the EU/EEA, Switzerland, UK, USA, Australia, New Zealand or Canada. A degree counts only if full-time, at least two years, taught entirely in English, at a university in those countries. C1 programmes (e.g. English Language and Linguistics) need TOEFL 110, IELTS 8 (at least 7.5 per component), CAE grade A or CPE grade C. — [univie English proficiency](https://studieren.univie.ac.at/en/applying-for-a-programme/language-proficiency/english-language-proficiency)
- TU Wien: B2 for English-taught master's. Accepted if issued within the last two years: TOEFL iBT (incl. Home Edition) at least 87, or 4.5 on the new scale valid since 21 January 2026; IELTS Academic (incl. Online) at least 6.5; Cambridge C1 Advanced at least 160; B2 First grade C or better (at least 160). "Not accepted": an English-taught degree from an institution outside the EU/EEA, Switzerland, UK, USA, Canada, Australia, New Zealand; nationality alone. Without a qualifying certificate "you cannot apply for admission." — [TU Wien competence in English](https://www.tuwien.at/en/studies/admission/masters-programmes/other-inter-national-bachelors-degree/competence-in-english)
- TU Graz: C1 for master's taught in English; certificate no more than two years old at application: TOEFL iBT 95; IELTS Academic 7.0; Cambridge CAE min. 180 points/grade C; Cambridge Proficiency; telc English C1; Pearson PTE Academic 76. A degree in English of at least two years counts only from an EU/EEA state, Australia, Canada, New Zealand, Singapore, South Africa, Switzerland, the UK or the USA. — [TU Graz English proof](https://www.tugraz.at/en/studying-and-teaching/studying-at-tu-graz/prospective-students/admission/proof-of-english-language-competence)
- JKU: B2 or C1 depending on programme. B2: TOEFL iBT 88 (min. 22 per skill) or 4.5 on certificates from January 2026; IELTS Academic 6.5 (6.5 per skill); PTE Academic 70. C1: TOEFL 100 (min. 25 per skill) or 5; IELTS 7.0 (7.0 per skill); PTE 80. Certificates not older than two years. English-taught study (at least 4 semesters) counts only at institutions in the EU, EEA, Switzerland, UK, USA, Australia, New Zealand or Canada. Duolingo is not mentioned. — [JKU English proficiency](https://www.jku.at/en/degree-programs/students/start-your-studies/proof-of-language-proficiency/language-proficiency-in-english/)
- WU (MSc Marketing): proof must be uploaded at application or the application is rejected. TOEFL iBT minimum 100 for tests before 21 January 2026, minimum 5 for tests on or after that date (MyBest scores excluded); IELTS Academic 7.0; Oxford Test of English Advanced at least 150; Cambridge CAE or CPE; or a three-year bachelor's taught entirely in English in an EU/EEA country, Switzerland, UK, USA, Australia, New Zealand or Canada; or English as native language. Course transcripts or confirmations of language skills are not accepted. — [WU application guide, step 1](https://www.wu.ac.at/en/programs/application-and-admission/your-journey-starts-here/0/19/20/2/1?cHash=603d4d5db021bb4873ab46c530a05af4)
- UAS Technikum Wien: B2 English for English-taught programmes; "Applicants with school education or university degree completed in the required languages need no proof of language skills", checked via the transcript; skills may also be tested in the interview. Language-proof PDFs on the page are dated 09.06.2026. — [FHTW admission requirements](https://www.technikum-wien.at/en/student-guide/requirements/); [FHTW third-country document list](https://www.technikum-wien.at/en/ma-click-through-third-country/)

**German**
- University of Vienna, German-taught master's: A2 (or higher) at application, C1 to be admitted. A2 certificates (not older than two years): ÖSD A2, Goethe A2, telc A2, DSD I, ÖIF A2, Austrian university language centre A2, TestDaF TDN 3 in all parts. C1 certificates (not older than three years): ÖSD C1, Goethe C1, telc C1 Hochschule, DSH2, DSD II, TestDaF TDN 4 in all parts, University of Vienna Language Center C1/2, ÖIF C1. "Applications without a proof of German language proficiency at level A2 will be rejected without exception." English-taught master's need no German proof. — [univie German proficiency](https://studieren.univie.ac.at/en/applying-for-a-programme/language-proficiency/german-language-proficiency)
- University of Vienna, India-specific: applicants educated in Bangladesh, India or Pakistan must take the onSET German placement test at VFS Education Services for programmes requiring A2 at application; it does not replace the German certificate. — [univie German proficiency](https://studieren.univie.ac.at/en/applying-for-a-programme/language-proficiency/german-language-proficiency); [univie competence check page](https://studieren.univie.ac.at/en/applying-for-a-programme/international-students/country-specific-requirements-for-admission/competence-check-at-vfs-education-services)
- TU Wien: A2 at application (ÖSD, Goethe, telc, DSD I, ÖIF, EU/EEA university language centre; valid two years); C1 for admission (ÖSD C1, Goethe C1, telc C1 Hochschule, DSH2, DSD II, TestDaF TDN 4 in all parts, University of Vienna Language Center C1/2; valid two years). — [TU Wien competence in German](https://www.tuwien.at/en/studies/admission/masters-programmes/other-inter-national-bachelors-degree/competence-in-german)
- TU Graz: C1 for German-taught programmes; with A2 at application the applicant gets a provisional notice of admission requiring the supplementary German exam at the University Preparation Programme Graz and enrols as a non-degree student. Without A2 the application "must be rejected". — [TU Graz German proof](https://www.tugraz.at/en/studying-and-teaching/studying-at-tu-graz/prospective-students/admission/proof-of-german-language-competence)
- JKU: A2 certificate (not older than two years) at application for German-taught programmes; not required for programmes offered entirely in English. — [JKU master's registration](https://www.jku.at/en/degree-programs/prospective-students/register-to-enroll/masters-degree-programs/)
- UAS Technikum Wien: German-taught programmes need B2 in both German and English. — [FHTW admission requirements](https://www.technikum-wien.at/en/student-guide/requirements/)

**University preparation programme (VWU / VGUH)**
- University of Vienna: "You cannot apply directly for the VWU." Master's applicants can attend only if they applied for a German-taught or bilingual master's and the supplementary German exam is prescribed in the admission letter. It is not available for master's taught in English. Subject-specific supplementary exams are taken later inside the master's, not at the VWU. Places are limited with no legal entitlement. — [univie VWU](https://studieren.univie.ac.at/en/applying-for-a-programme/vwu/i-want-to-start-the-vwu); [univie German proficiency](https://studieren.univie.ac.at/en/applying-for-a-programme/language-proficiency/german-language-proficiency)
- Once admitted to the VWU, only the VWU's own supplementary German exam is accepted; switching to another certificate "can have serious consequences for your residence permit" (Vienna) and "could lead to loss of your student visa" (TU Wien). — [univie German proficiency](https://studieren.univie.ac.at/en/applying-for-a-programme/language-proficiency/german-language-proficiency); [TU Wien competence in German](https://www.tuwien.at/en/studies/admission/masters-programmes/other-inter-national-bachelors-degree/competence-in-german)
- TU Wien VWU registration deadlines: 13 August (winter semester) and 19 January (summer semester). — [TU Wien master's admission](https://www.tuwien.at/en/studies/admission/masters-programmes/other-inter-national-bachelors-degree)

### Inferences
- An Indian applicant should plan on IELTS Academic or TOEFL iBT for every public university. A Medium of Instruction letter is useful only where the rule is not country-restricted (FHTW's wording allows it; MCI was not verified).
- IELTS Academic 7.0 with no band below 7.0 clears every institution checked except Vienna's few C1 programmes; 6.5 is enough only for Vienna (B2 programmes), TU Wien and JKU B2 programmes.
- Duolingo is not on the accepted list at Vienna, TU Wien, TU Graz, JKU or WU, so it should be treated as not accepted there.
- TOEFL scores are now reported on a new 1–6 scale for tests from 21 January 2026; thresholds are 4.5 (B2 at TU Wien and JKU) and 5 (C1 at WU and JKU).
- Even on an English-taught master's at TU Wien, any supplementary bachelor-level courses would be in German, which is a practical risk for applicants with subject gaps.

### Gaps
- MCI's English score thresholds and whether it accepts Duolingo or English-medium degrees were not found on the pages read.
- The two FHTW language-proof PDFs (dated 09.06.2026) were not opened, so its accepted certificates and scores are not listed.
- VWU/VGUH course fees and duration limits were not collected here.
- University of Vienna's page still lists TOEFL only on the old scale (94 points); its new-scale equivalent was not stated.

## 6. Other typical documents: CV, motivation letter, recommendations, passport, GRE/GMAT, portfolio, syllabus

Public research universities ask for little beyond passport, degree, transcripts and language proof; selective programmes (WU, UAS) add CV, motivation letter, recommendation and test scores. - Indian marksheets often show marks but not hours or credits per course. - GRE/GMAT is a WU (business) requirement, not a general Austrian one; none of the technical universities' general pages ask for it.

### Gaps
- Which other WU programmes require GMAT/GRE was checked only for Marketing.
- Proof of class ranking is not requested on any page read.
- Programme-specific extras in the University of Vienna's Master Access Guides were not reviewed programme by programme.

## 7. Step-by-step application mechanics at representative institutions (non-EU master's applicants)

### Takeaway
There is no central portal and the mechanics differ on every point that matters: Vienna is fully online, TU Wien is paper-only by post with certified copies, TU Graz is online plus originals by post, JKU is online with documents posted on request, WU is an online competitive tool with a deposit and in-person original check. For the next intakes, the closest live deadlines are Vienna's summer 2027 window (16 November 2026 to 7 January 2027), TU Graz and TU Wien summer 2027 (to 15 January 2027) and WU's 2027/28 rounds (8 January and 8 March 2027); JKU's summer 2027 non-EU window closed on 31 October 2026.

### Cited Findings

**University of Vienna (u:space, fully online)**
- Steps: check the Master Access Guide; for India take the VFS competence check first; create a u:account, register as prospective student and upload passport; then Studies → Apply for admission, upload documents and language proof, complete statistical data, submit. Status must show "submitted". — [univie master's admission](https://studieren.univie.ac.at/en/admission/master/external)
- Application periods, master's without selection procedure, non-EU/EEA citizens: summer semester 2027, 16 November 2026 to 7 January 2027; winter semester 2027/28, 31 May to 28 June 2027. Admission periods: 11 January to 31 March 2027 and 12 July to 31 October 2027. Master's with selection procedure: 1 March to 6 April 2027, once per year. — [univie deadlines](https://studieren.univie.ac.at/en/applying-for-a-programme/deadlines)
- Processing "may take several weeks"; no status information is given. The admission letter is sent by e-mail and stored in u:space → My documents. It is valid for two semesters unless the law or curriculum changes. Receiving it "does not mean you are admitted". — [univie master's admission](https://studieren.univie.ac.at/en/admission/master/external)
- Completing admission: log in to the Servicedesk within the admission period and state that you wish to start; the university "reserves the right to invite you to an online appointment"; after preliminary admission pay the tuition/Students' Union fee; admission is complete when the payment is registered in u:space; then order the u:card. — [univie completing admission](https://studieren.univie.ac.at/en/completing-the-admission-procedure/ma)

**TU Wien (paper application; no online submission)**
- "Can I submit my application online? No." Applications go by post or in person to the Admission Office, Karlsplatz 13; "Applications sent in electronically will not be considered." — [TU Wien FAQ](https://www.tuwien.at/en/tu-wien/organisation/central-divisions/admission-office/contact/faqs); [TU Wien master's admission](https://www.tuwien.at/en/studies/admission/masters-programmes/other-inter-national-bachelors-degree)
- Non-EU/EEA application periods: winter semester 16 January to 15 July; summer semester 16 July to 15 January. Maximum two applications per person. — [TU Wien master's admission](https://www.tuwien.at/en/studies/admission/masters-programmes/other-inter-national-bachelors-degree)
- Send certified copies only, no originals. Express courier shipments from non-EU countries are accepted only if marked that TU Wien incurs no costs (e.g. "Kein Zoll"). — [TU Wien FAQ](https://www.tuwien.at/en/tu-wien/organisation/central-divisions/admission-office/contact/faqs)
- Processing averages 10–12 weeks; no confirmation of receipt and no status updates. All correspondence, including the admission letter or rejection, is sent by post to the address on the form. No provisional or conditional letters are issued. Missing documents or legalisations not supplied in time move the application to the next semester. — [TU Wien FAQ](https://www.tuwien.at/en/tu-wien/organisation/central-divisions/admission-office/contact/faqs)

### Inferences
- Working back from an October 2027 start: VFS competence check and apostilles need to be done before the application windows (JKU 6 February to 31 March 2027; Vienna 31 May to 28 June 2027; TU Graz 1 May to 15 August 2027; TU Wien by 15 July 2027, realistically by March–April given 10–12 weeks' processing plus about three months for the residence permit).
- TU Graz is the only institution checked that wants original documents posted to Austria; applying there and elsewhere in the same cycle means the originals are unavailable for a while, so apply to TU Graz early or keep extra apostilled sets (for example a second original transcript).

## 8. Common rejection reasons and pitfalls for Indian applicants, including agents and consultancies

### Takeaway
The formal killers are: skipping or timing the VFS competence check wrongly, wrong institution category or under 60%, a missing or wrongly-based apostille, applying before the degree is complete, missing language proof at submission, and letting an agent submit the application, which Austrian law makes a ground for rejection.

### Cited Findings
- Agents: § 60 Abs. 6 UG makes representation of foreign applicants by persons not admitted to professional party representation in Austria (or not legally entitled to represent) inadmissible; such applications are to be rejected. Version in force since 01.09.2025. — [jusline § 60 UG](https://www.jusline.at/gesetz/univg/paragraf/60)
- TU Graz: "applications may only be submitted by the applicants themselves or by a person who can represent parties professionally in Austria. Applications submitted in any other way (e.g. via an agency) will be rejected." — [TU Graz master's with international degree](https://www.tugraz.at/en/studying-and-teaching/studying-at-tu-graz/prospective-students/admission/masters-programme-without-admission-procedure-with-an-international-degree)
- JKU: the application "must be submitted in person and is free of charge. Applications submitted by an agency or placement organization are unlawful and will not be processed." — [JKU master's registration](https://www.jku.at/en/degree-programs/prospective-students/register-to-enroll/masters-degree-programs/)
- MEA India: do not rely on "unauthorised persons/touts" for apostille services; only the named outsourced agencies accept documents. — [MEA Attestation / Apostille](https://www.mea.gov.in/apostille-menu.htm)
- Document doubts: under § 60 Abs. 3a UG the Rectorate may have documents or knowledge verified where authenticity is doubted and may charge a deposit of at most € 500, refunded if authenticity is confirmed and admission follows. — [jusline § 60 UG](https://www.jusline.at/gesetz/univg/paragraf/60)
- Not meeting the country criteria: University of Vienna, if the documents do not fulfil the requirements "you will not be admitted – regardless of the outcome of the competence check"; "Only take the competence check and onSET if you meet all admission requirements." — [univie competence check page](https://studieren.univie.ac.at/en/applying-for-a-programme/international-students/country-specific-requirements-for-admission/competence-check-at-vfs-education-services)
- Competence check timing: Uni Graz cannot process applications without a positive result at the time of applying. — [Uni Graz competency check](https://internationale-studierende.uni-graz.at/en/application-masters-studies/competency-check-master/)
- Missing legalisation: Vienna, "Can I apply without the required legalisation? No, this is not possible." TU Wien, documents without legalisation "cannot be processed"; photocopies with originals shown later are not allowed. — [univie country-specific FAQ](https://studieren.univie.ac.at/en/applying-for-a-programme/international-students/country-specific-requirements-for-admission); [TU Wien FAQ](https://www.tuwien.at/en/tu-wien/organisation/central-divisions/admission-office/contact/faqs)
- Damaged documents: TU Wien will not accept documents whose staples, seals, stickers or threads have been removed or tampered with. — [TU Wien legalisation](https://www.tuwien.at/en/studies/admission/masters-programmes/other-inter-national-bachelors-degree/legalisation-and-translations-of-documents)
- Incomplete degree: Vienna accepts applications only after the degree is entirely completed; provisional transcripts are not accepted. — [univie country-specific FAQ](https://studieren.univie.ac.at/en/applying-for-a-programme/international-students/country-specific-requirements-for-admission)
- Missing language proof: WU rejects applications without the English proof uploaded; Vienna rejects German-taught applications without A2 "without exception"; TU Wien, without a B2 certificate "you cannot apply". — [WU application guide, step 1](https://www.wu.ac.at/en/programs/application-and-admission/your-journey-starts-here/0/19/20/2/1?cHash=603d4d5db021bb4873ab46c530a05af4); [univie German proficiency](https://studieren.univie.ac.at/en/applying-for-a-programme/language-proficiency/german-language-proficiency); [TU Wien competence in English](https://www.tuwien.at/en/studies/admission/masters-programmes/other-inter-national-bachelors-degree/competence-in-english)
- Subject gap: TU Wien refuses admission if differences exceed 30 ECTS. — [TU Wien FAQ](https://www.tuwien.at/en/tu-wien/organisation/central-divisions/admission-office/contact/faqs)
- Late or slow processing: TU Graz warns it "may not be possible to be admitted in the semester applied for" and takes no responsibility for visa appointments; TU Wien processes strictly chronologically and will not expedite for visa appointments. — [TU Graz master's with international degree](https://www.tugraz.at/en/studying-and-teaching/studying-at-tu-graz/prospective-students/admission/masters-programme-without-admission-procedure-with-an-international-degree); [TU Wien FAQ](https://www.tuwien.at/en/tu-wien/organisation/central-divisions/admission-office/contact/faqs)
- Bank transfer shortfalls: TU Wien notes foreign transfers may lose about € 12 in bank fees, and enrolment is not registered until the full amount arrives. — [TU Wien FAQ](https://www.tuwien.at/en/tu-wien/organisation/central-divisions/admission-office/contact/faqs)

### Inferences
- An education consultant may advise, but the account, the submission and the signature must be the applicant's own; an application visibly filed by a consultancy can be rejected on that ground alone at public universities. VFS Education Services is a different case: it is the universities' contracted test provider (and, for the three technical universities, their announced implementing partner), not a private agent chosen by the applicant.
- Applications to public universities are free (JKU states so; no fee appears at Vienna, TU Wien or TU Graz). A consultancy charging a "university application fee" for these is charging for its own service.
- Relying on German-market advice is a specific trap for Indians: the German route (no apostille, embassy verification) is the opposite of the Austrian one.

### Gaps
- No official Austrian warning naming fake agents or fraudulent admission letters targeting Indian students was found on the Embassy New Delhi site or the national portals; the universities' statutory "no agencies" notices are the only official statements located.
- No official statistics on rejection reasons for Indian applicants were found; the list above is assembled from stated rules, not from outcome data.
- Reddit and consultancy claims (for example that TU Graz needs legalised documents only after acceptance) contradict the official pages and were not relied on.
