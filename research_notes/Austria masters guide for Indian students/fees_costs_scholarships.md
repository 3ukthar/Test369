# Fees, living costs and scholarships for an Indian (third-country) master's student in Austria

Research date: 10 November 2026. All pages were opened on that date. "AY" = academic year. Every euro figure below was read on the official page cited unless it is explicitly tagged UNVERIFIED. Exchange rate used throughout: ECB euro reference rate of 9 November 2026 (latest published on the research date), EUR 1 = INR 105.1435 — [ECB](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/policy_and_exchange_rates/euro_reference_exchange_rates/html/eurofxref-graph-inr.en.html).

Terminology: "third-country national" = citizen of a country outside the EU/EEA/Switzerland (India is one). "ÖH fee" = compulsory students' union fee incl. accident/liability insurance. "§ 64 NAG" = the ordinary "Residence Permit – Student" that an Indian degree student holds. "PersGV" = Personengruppenverordnung, the regulation listing groups of foreigners treated like EU citizens for fees (long-term residents, diplomats' families etc. — a newly arriving Indian student is normally NOT in it).

## 1. Public universities: tuition per semester for third-country students, and the ÖH fee (2026/27, 2027/28)

### Takeaway
At every public university checked, an Indian student on a student residence permit pays EUR 726.72 tuition + EUR 26.20 ÖH fee = EUR 752.92 per semester (EUR 1,505.84 per year, about INR 1.58 lakh) from the first semester, regardless of how long the degree takes. The ÖH fee of EUR 26.20 is the 2026/27 figure; no 2027/28 ÖH amount is published yet, and no page announces any change to the EUR 726.72 for 2027/28.

### Cited Findings
- National overview (OeAD): "The student union membership fee ('ÖH-Beitrag'), which includes student accident insurance, is €26.20 per semester and is compulsory for all students." and "For all other students from third countries (who hold a Residence Permit–Student), a fee of €726.72 per semester is generally required." Page carries no academic-year label. — [studyinaustria.at, Tuition fees](https://studyinaustria.at/en/tuition)
- University of Vienna: "Amount non-EU/EEA — Students' Union fee: 26.20 Euro; Tuition fee: 726.72 Euro; Total: 752.92 Euro per semester"; "You pay the tuition fee from the first semester onwards. The degree programme duration does not affect the amount of the fee." No AY label on the amounts; sister page lists application periods for "Summer semester 2027" and "Winter semester 2027/28", so the page is current. — [Uni Wien, Amount of the tuition fee/Students' Union fee](https://studieren.univie.ac.at/en/studying-and-learning/financial-matters/amount-of-the-tuition-fee/students-union-fee)
- TU Wien: "The tuition fee is regular € 363.36 per semester (or € 726.72 per semester for non-EU nationals)"; other non-EU nationals "have to pay a tuition fee of 726.72 € as well as the ÖH fee from the first semester". No AY label; TU Wien's ÖH page gives no amount, only that it is due by 31 October / 31 March each semester. — [TU Wien, Tuition fee](https://www.tuwien.at/en/studies/admission/students-union-fee-and-tuition-fee/tuition-fee); [TU Wien, ÖH fee](https://www.tuwien.at/en/studies/admission/students-union-fee-and-tuition-fee/oeh-fee-students-union-fee)
- TU Graz: "Austrian Student Union fee (ÖH fee) per semester: currently 26.20 Euro"; "Non-EU foreign nationals who are not exempt from paying tuition fees pay 726,72 Euro per semester." Page links forms "for the winter semester 2026/27". — [TU Graz, Tuition Fees and the Austrian Student Union Fee](https://www.tugraz.at/en/studying-and-teaching/studying-at-tu-graz/prospective-students/financial-matters/tuition-fees-and-the-austrian-student-union-fee)
- JKU Linz: "The Austrian Student Union fee is € 26.20 (last update: winter term 2026/27)"; third-country nationals € 726.72 per semester. — [JKU, Pay Austrian Student Union Fees and Tuition](https://www.jku.at/en/degree-programs/students/start-your-studies/pay-austrian-student-union-fees-and-tuition/); German page: "ÖH-Beitrag in der Höhe von EUR 26,20 (Stand WS 26/27)" — [JKU Studienbeitrag](https://www.jku.at/studienbeitrag)
- University of Innsbruck: "ÖH-fee in the academic year 2026/2027: € 26,20"; third-country nationals with a residence permit under § 64 NAG "have to pay a tuition fee of € 726.72 (plus ÖH-fee) from the first semester." — [Uni Innsbruck, Tuition fee and financial support](https://www.uibk.ac.at/en/admission-department/admission/tuition-fee-and-financial-support/)
- WU Vienna: ÖH membership "costs € 26.20"; "The tuition fee for Non-EU and Non-EEA Citizens is € 726.72 per semester." No AY label. — [WU, Tuition Fees / Students' Union (ÖH) Dues](https://www.wu.ac.at/en/students/getting-organized/tuition-fees-students-union-oeh-dues)
- University of Graz: third-country students "generally have to pay each semester the tuition fee of € 726,72 plus the student union fee (ÖH fee)". No AY label. — [Uni Graz Academic Affairs, International Degree Students](https://studienabteilung.uni-graz.at/en/tuition-fee/degree-programme-students/international-degree-students/). The Uni Graz Welcome Center page still shows rounded older figures ("approx. 750€ tuition fees per semester", ÖH "approx 25€") — [Uni Graz Welcome Center](https://welcome.uni-graz.at/en/study-international/costs/)
- BOKU Vienna: ÖH "costs € 26,20"; non-EU/EEA tuition € 726.72 per semester. — [BOKU, How much tuition will I have to pay?](https://boku.ac.at/en/studienservices/themen/studienbeitraege-foerderungen/hoehe-des-studienbeitrages)
- 2027/28: FH Wiener Neustadt, which publishes both years side by side, lists the ÖH fee as "EUR 26.20 (2026/27)" and for "Academic year 2027/28 … Student union fee per semester: tbd". — [FHWN, Costs](https://www.fhwn.ac.at/en/information-for/prospective-students/costs)
- Payment deadlines: the fee must be paid each semester within the continuation period (TU Wien: until 31 October for winter, 31 March for summer); non-payment ends enrolment. — [TU Wien, ÖH fee](https://www.tuwien.at/en/studies/admission/students-union-fee-and-tuition-fee/oeh-fee-students-union-fee)
- Bank charges: TU Wien warns "Transfers from foreign bank accounts may be subject to a transaction fee of €12", retained by the bank. — [TU Wien Admission Office FAQs](https://www.tuwien.at/en/tu-wien/organisation/central-divisions/admission-office/contact/faqs)

### Inferences
- A two-year (4-semester) master's at any public university costs 4 × 752.92 = EUR 3,011.68 in tuition + ÖH (about INR 3.17 lakh), plus the same per extra semester if the student overruns. Unlike EU students, third-country students get no tuition-free period.
- EUR 726.72 is identical on all nine public-university pages, i.e. it is a statutory amount, not a per-university decision. The ÖH fee is indexed yearly (EUR 25.20 in 2025/26 per the Vetmeduni/ÖH Uni Wien search snippets; EUR 26.20 in 2026/27), so expect roughly EUR 27 in 2027/28 — an estimate, not a published figure.

### Gaps
- No official page states the 2027/28 ÖH fee yet (normally fixed in spring/summer 2027).
- Most university pages do not attach an academic year to the EUR 726.72; only the ÖH figure is year-labelled.
- The legal text (Universities Act § 91 / Studienbeitragsverordnung) could not be opened on ris.bka.gv.at (bot security check), so the statutory basis is cited via the universities' own pages only.

## 2. Is India eligible for any tuition exemption, reduction or refund at public universities?

### Takeaway
No. India is not on the country list (annex to the Studienbeitragsverordnung — the least-developed-countries list) that gives exemption from tuition, and it is not on the separate university refund lists (South-East Europe/Turkey/Ukraine/Belarus) at TU Graz and Uni Graz. An Indian student on a "Student" residence permit pays the full EUR 726.72 every semester unless a personal ground applies (e.g. a different residence title, an exchange/partnership agreement, or a scholarship that carries a waiver).

### Cited Findings
- University of Vienna, "Exemption 1: Students from designated developing countries" — full list: Afghanistan, Angola, Bangladesh, Benin, Bhutan, Burkina Faso, Burundi, Cambodia, Central African Republic, Chad, Comores, Congo (Democratic Republic), Djibouti, Eritrea, Ethiopia, Gambia, Guinea, Guinea-Bissau, Haiti, Kiribati, Lao PDR, Lesotho, Liberia, Madagascar, Malawi, Mali, Mauritania, Mozambique, Myanmar, Nepal, Niger, Rwanda, São Tomé and Príncipe, Senegal, Sierra Leone, Solomon Islands, Somalia, South Sudan, Sudan, Tanzania, Timor-Leste, Togo, Tuvalu, Uganda, Yemen, Zambia. India is not on it. — [Uni Wien, Amount of the tuition fee](https://studieren.univie.ac.at/en/studying-and-learning/financial-matters/amount-of-the-tuition-fee/students-union-fee)
- TU Wien: "Degree students who are citizens of a country listed in Annex 1 of the Decree on Tuition Fee are generally exempt from paying tuition fees. Only the ÖH fee is to be paid" — same list (plus Vanuatu); "If you have the nationality of another non-EU-country … you have to pay a tuition fee of 726.72 € as well as the ÖH fee from the first semester!" — [TU Wien, Tuition fee](https://www.tuwien.at/en/studies/admission/students-union-fee-and-tuition-fee/tuition-fee)
- TU Graz prints the same exemption list (no India) and adds a refund scheme only for "Students from South-East Europe": Albania, Belarus, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Kosovo, North Macedonia, Moldova, Montenegro, Serbia, Turkey, Ukraine. — [TU Graz, Tuition Fees](https://www.tugraz.at/en/studying-and-teaching/studying-at-tu-graz/prospective-students/financial-matters/tuition-fees-and-the-austrian-student-union-fee)
- Uni Graz: countries in the appendix of the Tuition Fee Regulation "which are the poorest countries on Earth – only have to pay the student union fee"; all other third countries pay € 726.72. — [Uni Graz, International Degree Students](https://studienabteilung.uni-graz.at/en/tuition-fee/degree-programme-students/international-degree-students/)
- JKU: citizens of countries in the "annex to section 4 para 2 (1) of the regulations regarding tuition fees are generally exempt"; other third-country nationals pay. — [JKU, tuition page](https://www.jku.at/en/degree-programs/students/start-your-studies/pay-austrian-student-union-fees-and-tuition/)
- Uni Wien's remission/reimbursement page lists no country-based ground other than Ukraine; grounds open to all students include receipt of Austrian federal study aid, disability of at least 50%, participation in a mobility programme, a required stay abroad. — [Uni Wien, Remission and reimbursement](https://studieren.univie.ac.at/en/studying-and-learning/financial-matters/tuition-fee/students-union-fee/remission-and-reimbursement)
- "Equal status" (paying like an EU citizen) is closed to holders of the ordinary student permit: "If you have a 'Aufenthaltsbewilligung - Student', equal status is not attainable. Unless you also fulfil one of the other requirements (e.g. residence title 'Daueraufenthalt - EU')." — [Uni Wien, Equal status with EU/EEA citizens](https://studieren.univie.ac.at/en/studying-and-learning/financial-matters/tuition-fee/students-union-fee/equal-status-with-eu/eea-citizens)
- Uni Graz: a "student" permit holder can only be exempted if they hold a school-leaving certificate from an Austrian school abroad or have had the centre of their life in Austria for at least 5 years before first admission. — [Uni Graz, International Degree Students](https://studienabteilung.uni-graz.at/en/tuition-fee/degree-programme-students/international-degree-students/)
- TU Wien lists as a waiver ground "A university partnership agreement of TU Wien with a university last attended by the degree foreign student, which includes a mutual waiver of the tuition fee." — [TU Wien, Tuition fee](https://www.tuwien.at/en/studies/admission/students-union-fee-and-tuition-fee/tuition-fee)
- OeAD summary: exemption or refund "is possible in special cases (e.g. for holders of certain scholarships, participants in exchange programmes and university partnerships, or students from specific countries)". — [studyinaustria.at, Tuition fees](https://studyinaustria.at/en/tuition)

### Inferences
- Indian neighbours Nepal, Bhutan, Bangladesh, Myanmar and Afghanistan are exempt; India is not. Older guides that say "students from developing countries get tuition refunded" do not apply to India under the lists published in November 2026.
- The only realistic routes to not paying EUR 726.72 for an Indian are (a) a scholarship with a fee waiver (e.g. Helmut Veith Stipend at TU Wien, see section 7), (b) enrolling through an Erasmus Mundus/joint programme, or (c) later changing residence status (e.g. family member, Red-White-Red Card) — none of which is available by default.

### Gaps
- The annex of the Studienbeitragsverordnung itself could not be read on RIS (security check); the country list is taken from three universities that reproduce it and agree with each other (TU Wien/TU Graz add Vanuatu, Uni Wien's list omits it).
- Not every public university's statute was checked for a discretionary refund scheme; Uni Wien, TU Wien, TU Graz, Uni Graz, JKU, Uni Innsbruck, WU and BOKU show none that covers India.

UAS set their own third-country fees, and the spread is enormous: from EUR 0 tuition (FH Vorarlberg) through EUR 363–726.72 per semester (FH Salzburg, FH OÖ, FH Joanneum, Kufstein) and EUR 850–3,000 (FH Wiener Neustadt, FHWien der WKW, St. Pölten, FH Technikum Wien) to EUR 6,900–9,500 (IMC Krems) and EUR 8,250 (MCI Innsbruck) per semester. All add the EUR 26.20 ÖH fee. Each fee must be checked on the individual school's page.

## 4. Private universities: tuition range with examples

### Takeaway
Private universities charge roughly EUR 12,000–16,000 per year for a standard master's (EUR 6,000–8,000 per semester), with business-school flagships higher (CEU MS in Business Analytics/Finance EUR 15,000/year; Modul MBA EUR 11,000/semester; Webster MA/MBA about EUR 31,680 for the whole 36-credit degree). Fees are the same for all nationalities.

### Cited Findings
- Central European University (CEU), Vienna — table headed "tuition fees for the Academic Year 2025/26" (no 2026/27 or 2027/28 table published on the page): master's programmes EUR 12,000/year (EUR 6,000 per instalment); Legal Studies EUR 13,000; Master of Public Administration EUR 14,500; MS in Business Analytics and AI EUR 15,000; MS in Finance and AI EUR 15,000. Other required fees: ÖH EUR 52.40/year, Enrollment Fee EUR 200, one-time non-refundable Registration Fee EUR 500 (EUR 1,000 for MSc Social Data Science; EUR 3,000 for the two business MSc) which is "credited towards the 1st Tuition Fee Installment". — [CEU, Tuition and Other Fees](https://www.ceu.edu/admissions/tuition-fees)
- Modul University Vienna — "Fees as of 01 January 2026", per semester: MSc in Management EUR 8,000 (4 semesters); MSc in International Tourism Management EUR 7,000; MSc in Sustainable Development, Management and Policy EUR 7,000; MSc in Data Science for Sustainability EUR 7,000; MBA EUR 11,000 (3 semesters). "Tuition fees are adjusted bi-annually, in January and June according to the inflation index." Paying the whole programme upfront gives a 3% discount, a full year 1.5%. — [Modul, Tuition and Finances](https://www.modul.ac.at/study-at-mu/tuition-fees)
- Modul one-off administration fee for graduate programmes: EUR 500, "charged together with the first tuition fee installment and it is non-refundable" (Guidelines on Fees "as applicable from 18th September 2026"). — [Modul Guidelines on Fees (PDF)](https://aas.modul.ac.at/MU/MUDoc.pl?file=doc51.pdf&did=51)
- Webster Vienna Private University — "2026-2027 Tuition Rates": "MBA, MA in Marketing or MA in International Relations, excluding prerequisites (36 U.S. credit hours): 31,680 euros (estimated total fees…)"; MA in Psychology (48 credits) EUR 42,240; part-time EUR 880 per credit hour; ÖH EUR 26.20/semester; graduation fee EUR 100. — [Webster Vienna, Tuition and Fees](https://webster.ac.at/financialinformation/tuitionandfees.php)
- OeAD: at private universities "Tuition fees differ from one institution to the next. Please contact the relevant institution directly." — [studyinaustria.at](https://studyinaustria.at/en/tuition)

### Inferences
- Indicative first-year cost of tuition + compulsory fees: CEU standard master's about EUR 12,250 (12,000 + 200 + 52.40, 2025/26 table); Modul MSc about EUR 14,550–16,550 (incl. EUR 500 administration fee); Webster MA/MBA about EUR 15,900 if the 36 credits are spread over two years.
- CEU's page shows the 2026/27 ÖH amount (EUR 26.20/semester) beside a tuition table labelled 2025/26, so the tuition table may be un-updated rather than genuinely current.

### Gaps
- CEU tuition for 2026/27 and 2027/28 is not published on the page; treat EUR 12,000–15,000 as last-known (AY 2025/26).
- Other private universities (e.g. Sigmund Freud, Danube Private, Lauder Business School) were not checked.

## 5. Application fees, processing fees and deposits for non-EU master's applicants

### Takeaway
Public universities do not charge an application fee for master's admission (JKU and WU say so explicitly; Uni Wien's administration fee applies only to bachelor/diploma entrance procedures; TU Wien and TU Graz list none). The real upfront money is (a) deposits that many UAS and private universities take from third-country applicants, EUR 200–1,000 and usually credited or refunded, (b) MCI's demand for a full year (EUR 16,500) on admission, and (c) the EUR 218 residence-permit fee.

### Cited Findings
Public universities
- JKU Linz: "There is no fee to submit an application for admission. Applications submitted by an agency or placement organization are unlawful and will not be processed." — [JKU, Master's Degree Programs – register to enroll](https://www.jku.at/en/degree-programs/prospective-students/register-to-enroll/masters-degree-programs/); "Application submissions to the JKU Linz must always be submitted by the applicants themselves and are free of charge!" — [JKU, International Students](https://www.jku.at/en/degree-programs/international-students/international-students/)

(MSc Supply Chain Management FAQ) — [WU SCM FAQ](https://www.wu.ac.at/en/programs/masters-programs/supply-chain-management/frequently-asked-questions/). But WU requires a "security deposit in the amount of € 200" from every applicant who accepts an offer for an English-taught MSc; it is refunded after enrolment and payment of the tuition/ÖH fees, and forfeited if the student does not enrol. — [WU, Admission Security Deposit](https://www.wu.ac.at/en/programs/application-and-admission/deposit)

## 6. Monthly living costs: Vienna vs Graz / Linz / Innsbruck

### Takeaway
The official national guideline (OeAD, based on the 2025 student social survey) is about EUR 1,300 per month. University estimates put Vienna at EUR 1,200–1,600, Innsbruck at about EUR 1,200, Graz at about EUR 1,000–1,300 and Linz at EUR 800–1,250. Compulsory student health insurance is EUR 78.84 per month (2026). Students aged 26 or over — likely for someone with work experience — lose most youth transport discounts.

- The OeAD EUR 1,300 breakdown (accommodation + food + study/personal) does not list health insurance separately; WU lists it separately. Budgeting EUR 78.84 on top is the cautious reading.

## 7. Scholarships and funding realistically open to Indian master's students

### Takeaway
There is no Austrian government scholarship for a full master's degree: OeAD states it funds no whole degree programmes, and the Ernst Mach Grant is for short research stays by young researchers. Realistic money comes from (a) Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters with an Austrian partner, (b) a handful of competitive university schemes — TU Graz High Potentials (up to EUR 17,600, deadline 15 Feb 2027), TU Wien Helmut Veith Stipend for women in computer science (EUR 7,000/year + tuition waiver, deadline 30 Nov 2026), and (c) tuition discounts of 50–90% at expensive private/UAS schools (MCI, Modul, CEU), which reduce a high fee rather than pay living costs.

## 8. Total first-year budget estimate (low / typical / high), EUR and INR

### Takeaway
At a public university the first year (12 months) costs roughly EUR 13,500 (about INR 14.2 lakh) on a lean budget in Linz/Graz, EUR 18,300 (about INR 19.2 lakh) at the official national average, and EUR 21,900 (about INR 23.0 lakh) in Vienna at the upper university estimate — of which tuition and ÖH are only EUR 1,505.84. At premium UAS or private universities the same year costs EUR 29,000–36,000 (INR 30–38 lakh). These totals are this researcher's arithmetic from the cited figures, not an official estimate.

### Cited Findings
Inputs (all cited above)
- Tuition + ÖH, public university: EUR 752.92 per semester (AY 2026/27). — [Uni Wien](https://studieren.univie.ac.at/en/studying-and-learning/financial-matters/amount-of-the-tuition-fee/students-union-fee)
- Living costs: EUR 1,300/month national guideline. — [studyinaustria.at](https://studyinaustria.at/en/live-and-work/living-costs); Vienna EUR 1,200–1,600. — [WU](https://www.wu.ac.at/en/programs/international-and-exchange-students/international-students/welcome-to-vienna/estimated-living-costs); Linz EUR 800–1,250. — [JKU Fact Sheet](https://www.jku.at/fileadmin/gruppen/50/Exchange/JKU_Factsheet.pdf)
- Student health insurance EUR 78.84/month (2026) and residence-permit fee EUR 218 (2026). — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme)
- Exchange rate EUR 1 = INR 105.1435, ECB reference rate of 9 November 2026 (12-month range: 102.1440 on 20 Nov 2025 to 112.3170 on 20 May 2026; average 108.1537). — [ECB](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/policy_and_exchange_rates/euro_reference_exchange_rates/html/eurofxref-graph-inr.en.html)

- The "low" EUR 900/month sits between JKU's floor of EUR 800 and TU Graz's "a little less than 1000"; it assumes a dorm room of roughly EUR 300–400.
- Same "typical" living assumptions with other institution types (tuition + ÖH + compulsory one-off fees, first year, then total): FH Vorarlberg EUR 52.40 → EUR 16,816 (INR 17.7 lakh); FH Salzburg EUR 778.40 → EUR 17,542 (INR 18.4 lakh); FH Wiener Neustadt EUR 2,002.40 → EUR 18,766 (INR 19.7 lakh); FHWien der WKW EUR 2,052.40 → EUR 18,816 (INR 19.8 lakh); USTP EUR 3,052.40 → EUR 19,816 (INR 20.8 lakh); FH Technikum Wien EUR 6,052.40 → EUR 22,816 (INR 24.0 lakh); CEU standard master's about EUR 12,252 → EUR 29,016 (INR 30.5 lakh); IMC Krems business EUR 13,852.40 → EUR 30,616 (INR 32.2 lakh); Modul MSc EUR 14,552–16,552 → EUR 31,316–33,316 (INR 32.9–35.0 lakh); MCI EUR 16,552.40 → EUR 33,316 (INR 35.0 lakh); IMC Krems science & technology EUR 19,052.40 → EUR 35,816 (INR 37.7 lakh).
- Cash-flow point: the visa requires proof of 12 months' funds in advance (EUR 15,700.68 for age 24+, about INR 16.5 lakh; EUR 8,670.96 under 24), so the "typical" amount must largely exist in the student's own account before departure, in addition to any deposit paid to a UAS/private university.
- Currency risk: over the past 12 months the EUR/INR reference rate moved between 102.14 and 112.32, i.e. the typical EUR 18,270 budget would have ranged from about INR 18.7 lakh to INR 20.5 lakh.
- Not included in the totals because no official figure was found: flight to Austria, travel health insurance for entry, legalisation/translation of documents, dorm deposit (commonly one to two months' rent), winter clothing, and any German course.

### Gaps
- 2027 re-indexed values (health insurance, visa funds thresholds, ÖH fee 2027/28, Vienna transit) will raise each line by a few percent; they are not yet published except Vienna transit (+3.4% on average from 1 January 2027).
- One-off arrival costs are unquantified from official sources (see list above).
- The low/typical/high living figures are estimates published for all students or for exchange students; none is specific to Indian master's students or to students aged 24+ (whom the survey says spend more than average).
