## 1. Which title applies, where and how to apply from India (Embassy vs VFS, appointments, backlog)

### Takeaway
For any study stay longer than 6 months an Indian citizen needs the "Residence Permit – Student" (Aufenthaltsbewilligung "Student", § 64 NAG), not a visa; the first application must be filed in person at the Austrian Embassy New Delhi (not at VFS, not at an honorary consulate), the decision is awaited in India, and a Visa D is then issued only for entry to collect the card. Appointments for residence-permit applications are requested by e-mail to the consular section (new-delhi-ka@bmeia.gv.at); the embassy publishes no waiting time or backlog notice specific to student residence permits (only a general "high demand / longer waiting" notice for visa appointments).

### Cited Findings
- Visa vs permit: Visa C = up to 90 days in 180; Visa D = 91 days to max. 6 months; "A visa (travel visa C or visa D) can neither be applied for nor be renewed in Austria"; stays of more than 6 months require a residence permit, and "Most students will opt for a 'Residence Permit – Student'". — [Austrian Embassy New Delhi, "Studying in Austria"](https://www.bmeia.gv.at/en/austrian-embassy-new-delhi/travels-to-austria/studying-in-austria) (undated page)
- "The application for a residence permit must generally be submitted in person at the Austrian Embassy in New Delhi before travelling to Austria. The application will be forwarded to Austria by the representative authority and processed by the competent residence authority within Austria. Since you have to await the decision ... in your country of residence, you should apply at least three months before the intended entry to Austria." — [Embassy New Delhi, "Studying in Austria"](https://www.bmeia.gv.at/en/austrian-embassy-new-delhi/travels-to-austria/studying-in-austria)
- "Applications for residence permits (exceeding 6 months), Job seeker visa or visa as part of any other program offering work opportunities have to be applied at the Austrian embassy in New Delhi in person." — [Embassy New Delhi, "How to apply?"](https://www.bmeia.gv.at/en/austrian-embassy-new-delhi/travels-to-austria/entry-and-residence/how-to-apply) (undated; checklists on the page are dated Aug 2026)

### Inferences
- For an Indian citizen (visa-required nationality) the practical and safe route is the embassy route; filing in Austria on a Schengen visa is legally possible in individual cases but risky (must leave when the visa days run out; "hidden purpose" problem) and is not what the Embassy or OeAD recommend.
- Because every applicant in India must travel to New Delhi in person and appointments are arranged by e-mail (no public calendar), the appointment lead time is unknowable in advance — e-mail the consular section as soon as the admission letter arrives.

### Gaps
- No published waiting time / backlog figure for student residence-permit appointments at the Embassy New Delhi (checked embassy home, how-to-apply, studying, settlement, news pages on 10 Nov 2026). UNVERIFIED how many weeks the e-mail appointment currently takes.
- What exactly the embassy asks for in its reply e-mail (document order, number of copy sets beyond "one set of photocopies", payment mode) is not published.

## 2. Full document checklist for Indian applicants; apostille/legalisation and translation

### Takeaway
There is no India-specific residence-permit checklist PDF on the Embassy New Delhi site (its "Students" checklist PDF is only for Visa C/D up to 6 months); the binding list is the national one (OeAD/BMI/NAG-DV): application form, passport plus copy of all pages, ICAO photo, admission letter, police clearance certificate (max. 3 months old, apostilled), proof of funds, proof of accommodation costs (min. 3 months), health insurance, declaration of regular expenses. India–Austria is an Apostille relationship (no embassy legalisation), and originals plus one set of photocopies must be shown.

### Cited Findings
**Checklist (first application), per OeAD — "enclose ... photocopies of the following documents with your personally signed application form ... and additionally show only the originals":**
- Application form (BMI form "Antragsformular B", English version v2026-10-06) with the official explanation sheet "Stay Permit – Student" (Status 08/2026); the signature must be made "directly in front of the employee of the Austrian authority or Austrian embassy". — [BMI form (EN)](https://www.bmi.gv.at/312_en/60a/files/b/239-2026-antragsformular-b-aufenthaltstitel-drittstaatsangehoerige-en-v20261006-web-bf.pdf); [BMI explanation, Stay Permit – Student, Status 08/2026](https://www.bmi.gv.at/312_en/60a/files/b/explanation/19-aufenthaltsbewilligung-student-erklaerung-en-bf-20261006.pdf)
- Valid passport; for a first application additionally a "copy of the passport (all pages)". — [OeAD, Residence Permit – Student](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme)
- Photo: "Passport-sized photograph according to the ICAO criteria (colour photograph, size 3.5 x 4.5 cm) not older than 6 months". — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme)
- "letter of admission ('Zulassungsbescheid') of the Austrian higher education institution" (NAG-DV wording: "Aufnahmebestätigung der Universität, der Fachhochschule, der Privathochschule, der Privatuniversität ..."). — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme); [RIS, § 8 NAG-DV, in force 07.08.2026](https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/ii/2005/451/P8/NOR40278854)
- "Police clearance certificate from your country of residence (in individual cases also from your country of origin) not older than 3 months at the time of application (legalised and afterwards translated)". — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme). MA 35 adds: if you lived more than 6 months in another country before Austria you need the certificate from that country too. — [Stadt Wien / MA 35, Aufenthaltsbewilligung Student](https://www.wien.gv.at/amtswege/aufenthaltsbewilligung-student) (undated; fee note refers to 1 Jan 2026)
- Proof of sufficient funds for 12 months (amounts and forms: see section 3) and, on request, proof of the origin of the funds. — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme)
- "Declaration of regular expenses (loans, maintenance payments or other financial burdens)" (MA 35 form). — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme)
- "Accommodation in Austria for a minimum of 3 months (e.g. rental agreement, accommodation contract with a student hall of residence, accommodation agreement ['Wohnrechtsvereinbarung']) in connexion with proof of the required funds to cover the living costs (the calculation is based on the presented costs for accomodation)". — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme). Legally, students are exempt from proving a legal title to "accommodation according to local standards" (§ 64(1) Z 1 NAG excludes § 11(2) Z 2), "However the costs for accommodation will need to be taken into account when showing that the applicant has adequate means of subsistence." — [RIS, § 64 NAG](https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/i/2005/100/P64/NOR40278006); [migration.gv.at, Temporary residence](https://www.migration.gv.at/en/types-of-immigration/temporary-residence/) (undated; 2026 rates)
- "Proof of health insurance covering all risks (e.g. by means of registration for student insurance after entry) in Austria". For entry: travel health insurance "valid in Austria" with "insurance cover well over EUR 30,000 including guarantee to cover possible recovery"; "Travel health insurance is only sufficient for the period until a 'health insurance covering all risks' can be taken out after entry." A written statement that ÖGK student self-insurance will be taken out after entry "can be attached to the application form or stated in the application form under the point 'health insurance'". ÖGK student self-insurance premium: EUR 78.84/month [as of 2026]. Private alternative must have no waiting periods, cost caps or excluded risks, pay Austrian providers directly, and the insurer must confirm this in writing. — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme)
- Birth certificate: NOT on the OeAD student checklist. BMI explanation (08/2026): a birth/adoption certificate is needed only "If you want to prove that a person is related to you (for example: your child) ... In all other cases, you do not need to present a birth certificate." NAG-DV § 7(1) Z 3 lists it only "erforderlichenfalls" (where required). — [BMI explanation 08/2026](https://www.bmi.gv.at/312_en/60a/files/b/explanation/19-aufenthaltsbewilligung-student-erklaerung-en-bf-20261006.pdf); [RIS, § 7 NAG-DV (version in force since 01.10.2022)](https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/ii/2005/451/P7/NOR40246947). Conflicting lead: an FH JOANNEUM India hand-out lists, as "(Further) required documents by Austrian Embassy", "Birth certificate*" and "Police clearance certificate*" with "* Documents must be legalised (Apostille) and to be submitted together with a certified German translation" — [FH JOANNEUM, "Documents India" (PDF created Dec 2024, uploaded 2025/11; its funds figure EUR 672.64 is the outdated 2024 rate)](https://cdn3.fh-joanneum.at/media/2025/11/Documents-for-Admission-Visa-India.pdf)
- "The residence authorities may require the submission of further documents and confirmations in individual cases." — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme)
- At card collection in Austria you additionally need: confirmation of enrolment (study sheet / enrolment confirmation) and proof of full health insurance "if you have only presented a travel insurance so far" (BMI); OeAD FAQ: "your registration form [Meldezettel], a current bank statement and, at the latest, proof of all-risk health insurance". MA 35: "Wenn Sie die Aufenthaltsbewilligung abholen, müssen Sie nachweisen, dass das Geld für die gesamte Dauer des Aufenthalts zur Verfügung steht." — [BMI explanation 08/2026](https://www.bmi.gv.at/312_en/60a/files/b/explanation/19-aufenthaltsbewilligung-student-erklaerung-en-bf-20261006.pdf); [OeAD FAQ](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/translate-to-english-faq-zu-einreise-und-aufenthalt-fuer-drittstaatsangehoerige-studierende); [MA 35](https://www.wien.gv.at/amtswege/aufenthaltsbewilligung-student)

**Originals/copies, legalisation, translation:**
- Embassy New Delhi: "You have to submit the original documents and one set of photocopies. All documents have to be legalised and documents in languages other than German have to be submitted together with a certified German translation." — [Embassy, Studying in Austria](https://www.bmeia.gv.at/en/austrian-embassy-new-delhi/travels-to-austria/studying-in-austria)
- India = Apostille: "The 1961 Hague Convention stipulates that signatory countries (including both Austria and India) agrees to mutually recognize each other's 'public documents' ... such documents need no prior legalisation by an Embassy or Consulate of the other country. Yet, all these public documents must be authenticated by an Apostille." "Apostilles on Indian documents see Ministry of External Affairs, Government of India." — [Embassy New Delhi, Legalisation](https://www.bmeia.gv.at/en/austrian-embassy-new-delhi/service-for-citizens/legalization) (undated). Vienna's country list: "Indien: Apostille". — [Stadt Wien, Länderliste Apostille/diplomatische Beglaubigung](https://www.wien.gv.at/zusammenleben/beglaubigungen-ahs-info)
- Indian apostille procedure (MEA): apostille covers "personal documents like birth/death/marriage certificates, Affidavits, Power of Attorney, etc. and educational documents"; Step 1 authentication by the State/UT Regional Authentication Centre, Step 2 deposit with an authorised outsourced agency (BLS International, Superb Enterprises, IVS Global, Alhind Tours & Travels), Step 3 collect; "no document is directly accepted from individuals at the Ministry"; fees: "Rs 50 per apostille sticker" + agency "Rs 84 per document" + "Rs. 3 per page" scanning; e-Sanad is the online route for documents in a digital repository; services decentralised to 16 cities. — [MEA, Attestation / Apostille](https://www.mea.gov.in/apostille-menu) (undated)
- Indian police clearance certificate: applied for online on the Passport Seva portal ("Apply for Police Clearance Certificate"), fee paid online, appointment at a Passport Seva Kendra / Post Office PSK, then visit the PSK/RPO with originals. — [Passport Seva, Apply for PCC](https://www.passportindia.gov.in/psp/ApplyPCC) (portal banner: passport fees revised w.e.f. 1 July 2026 — check Fee Calculator)
- Order: "legalised and afterwards translated" (OeAD checklist); translations "into German language by a sworn and court-certified translator as the residence authority can ask for a German translation"; "It is recommended to submit all foreign documents in German language. Documents in English language are also accepted in some cases." — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme)
- English is normally accepted without translation by the Austrian side: "Urkunden und Unterlagen in englischer Sprache bedürfen in der Regel keiner Übersetzung" — [oesterreich.gv.at](https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/de/themen/menschen_aus_anderen_staaten/aufenthalt/3/2/1/1/Seite.120121) (updated 03.08.2026); MA 35: translation needed only "Wenn Ihre Unterlagen in einer anderen Sprache als Deutsch oder Englisch sind" — [MA 35](https://www.wien.gv.at/amtswege/aufenthaltsbewilligung-student); the embassy's general visa guidance likewise: "When submitting English language documents, a translation into German is not required." — [Embassy, How to apply?](https://www.bmeia.gv.at/en/austrian-embassy-new-delhi/travels-to-austria/entry-and-residence/how-to-apply). Law (§ 6 NAG-DV, in force 07.08.2026): documents are to be presented "im Original und in Kopie"; documents not in German "sind auf Verlangen der Behörde oder Berufsvertretungsbehörde zusätzlich in einer Übersetzung ins Deutsche vorzulegen" and "auf Verlangen der Behörde ... in beglaubigter Form" — i.e. translation and legalisation are on request of the authority/embassy. — [RIS, § 6 NAG-DV](https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/ii/2005/451/P6/NOR40278853)
- OeAD on timing: "Depending on the issuing country, it may take several months to legalise the documents required for an application." — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme); [OeAD info sheet Legalisation (April 2025)](https://oead.at/fileadmin/Dokumente/oead.at/OeAD_Stamm/Nach_Oesterreich/Einreise_und_Aufenthalt/Datenbank_Einreise_Aufenthalt/Infoblaetter/Englisch/Allgemeine_Info_-_Beglaubigung_en.pdf)
- The Embassy's only student checklist PDF ("Visa for Students, Research and Internship, Checklist – Updated August 2026") is for Visa C/D and itself says: "If applicant intends to stay longer than 6 (six) months, Residence Permit ('Aufenthaltstitel') has to be applied for." Its items (cover letter, round-trip flight reservation, travel medical insurance min. EUR 30,000, "Original bank statements of the last three (3) months", "ITR (with barcode) for last 2 years", copies of old visas, e-mail/mobile) are visa requirements, not residence-permit requirements. — [Embassy New Delhi checklist PDF, Aug 2026](https://www.bmeia.gv.at/fileadmin/user_upload/Vertretungen/New_Delhi/ENG/Visa_for_Students_Research_and_Internship_Checklist_updated_AUG_2026.pdf)

### Inferences
- Practical India document set: (1) PCC from Passport Seva issued within 3 months before the embassy appointment, apostilled by MEA; (2) birth certificate apostilled as a precaution (not legally required for a single student per BMI 08/2026, but the embassy text says "all documents have to be legalised" and a university hand-out says the embassy asks for it) — confirm in the embassy's appointment e-mail; (3) English-language Indian documents (PCC, bank statements) will usually not need German translation, but the Embassy's own student page still demands certified German translations for non-German documents, so ask the embassy or budget for sworn translations of PCC/birth certificate.
- Because the PCC must be ≤ 3 months old at application and the apostille takes extra time, the PCC should be the last document obtained, timed to the appointment date.

### Gaps
- No official embassy document states explicitly whether Embassy New Delhi insists on German translations of English-language Indian documents, or on the birth certificate, for student permits (official sources conflict as shown). UNVERIFIED.
- Whether the PCC must be the Passport-Office (PSK/RPO) PCC rather than a local-police certificate is not stated by any Austrian source; Austrian sources only say "police clearance certificate from your country of residence". UNVERIFIED (PSK PCC is the standard Indian document for foreign immigration).
- Current Passport Seva PCC fee: not confirmed on the page (search snippet of the official FAQ said Rs 750; fees revised 1 July 2026). UNVERIFIED.

## 3. Proof of funds: exact amounts 2026 (and 2027), rent rule, accepted forms of proof

### Takeaway
2026 (official): EUR 722.58/month if under 24 (EUR 8,670.96 for 12 months) and EUR 1,308.39/month from age 24 (EUR 15,700.68), shown for 12 months in advance, plus any accommodation cost above EUR 386.43/month and the health-insurance premium; 2027 amounts are not yet published — the law fixes a 3.3 % increase (factor 1.033), giving ESTIMATES of about EUR 746.43 and EUR 1,351.57/month. An Austrian or blocked account is NOT required: a (foreign) savings book or bank account in the applicant's own name that is accessible from Austria is accepted, alternatively a notarised declaration of guarantee (Haftungserklärung), traveller's cheques or a scholarship confirmation.

### Cited Findings
**2026 amounts (official, identical on four official sources):**
- "Proof of sufficient financial means to cover the living costs [as of 2026]: Students under 24 years of age: EUR 722.58/month; Students as of the age of 24: EUR 1,308.39/month; Couples: EUR 2,064.12/month for both; For each child additionally: EUR 201,88/month. You have to prove these funds for twelve months in advance." — [OeAD, Residence Permit – Student](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme)
- BMI brochure "Stand: 1. Jänner 2026": for education permits (pupils, students) the orphan's-pension rates apply: "Antragsteller bis zum 24. Lebensjahr € 722,58; Antragsteller über dem 24. Lebensjahr € 1.308,39"; spouses over 24 both applying as students: € 2.064,12; "Wert der freien Station ... € 386,43"; example: one person needs "€ 15.700,68 (1.308,39x12)" saved per year. — [BMI, Informationsbroschüre über die Unterhaltsberechnung im NAG, 2026](https://www.bmi.gv.at/312/files/broschuere_unterhaltsberechnung_publikation_a4_2026_converted_webbf.pdf)
- MA 35: "Für 2026 ... Einzelpersonen zu Ausbildungszwecken ... bis 24 Jahre: 722,58 Euro; ... ab 24 Jahre: 1.308,39 Euro; 'Freie Station' 2026: 386,43 Euro". — [Stadt Wien, Gesicherter Lebensunterhalt](https://www.wien.gv.at/zusammenleben/einwanderung-aufenthalt-gesicherter-lebensunterhalt)

- Age rule nuance: for the forecast, what counts is "nicht das Alter im Entscheidungszeitpunkt, sondern die Entwicklung in dem Zeitraum, für den der Aufenthaltstitel erteilt werden soll" (VwGH 22.03.2018, Ra 2017/22/0177) — i.e. turning 24 during the permit year matters. — [BMI brochure 2026, ch. 1.2](https://www.bmi.gv.at/312/files/broschuere_unterhaltsberechnung_publikation_a4_2026_converted_webbf.pdf)

**Rent and insurance on top:**
- "If the costs for accommodation exceed EUR 386,43/month [as of 2026], you have to prove additional funds. You also have to prove sufficient funds for the necessary health insurance." — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme)

**Accepted forms of proof:**
- "The funds have to be in a (foreign) savings book or (foreign) bank account that must bear the applicant's name and be accessible from Austria. An alternative possibility would be a declaration of guarantee ('Haftungserklärung') of a person residing in the European Union, the purchase of traveller's cheques or a confirmation of the award of a scholarship." — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme)
- Foreign account is fine if access from Austria is shown: "Das Geld kann auch auf einem Konto im Ausland liegen. Dann müssen Sie aber nachweisen, dass Sie darauf jederzeit von Österreich aus zugreifen können." — [Stadt Wien, Gesicherter Lebensunterhalt](https://www.wien.gv.at/zusammenleben/einwanderung-aufenthalt-gesicherter-lebensunterhalt). BMI: the applicant must show "auf welche Weise er von Österreich aus Zugriff darauf hat bzw. wie er das Geld nach Österreich transferieren kann", e.g. a foreign bank's credit card usable for withdrawals/payments in Europe; the applicant must have a legal claim to the money and it must not come from illegal sources. — [BMI brochure 2026](https://www.bmi.gv.at/312/files/broschuere_unterhaltsberechnung_publikation_a4_2026_converted_webbf.pdf)
- "You can show your financial funds from different sources. For example, you can combine the financial support of your parents with your own savings or income." — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme)
- Origin-of-funds evidence "Upon request of the residence authority" — accepted examples: applicant's declaration of where the funds come from "(e.g., savings, loan, scholarship, sale of an apartment/house, inheritance)"; "Bank statement of the last 6 months (including payments into the account, payment orders, etc.)"; employment proof (contract + last 6 pay slips, or trade licence + last tax assessment); "confirmation of scholarship, sales contract, loan agreement etc."; parents'/sponsors' bank statements of the last 6 months; parents'/sponsor's declaration "that the provided funds are on free disposal of the applicant and where the money comes from"; parents'/sponsors' employment proof. — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme)
- Loans: a credited amount counts only if it is actually available during the permit's validity (VwGH 29.05.2024, Ra 2022/22/0079, cited in the brochure). — [BMI brochure 2026, fn. 54](https://www.bmi.gv.at/312/files/broschuere_unterhaltsberechnung_publikation_a4_2026_converted_webbf.pdf)
- Haftungserklärung: permitted (not mandatory) for students (§ 64(1) NAG last sentence: "Eine Haftungserklärung ist zulässig"); must be certified by an Austrian notary or an Austrian court, valid at least five years; the guarantor must prove capacity to pay; only one declaration is allowed (several guarantors possible); giving an unsustainable declaration is an administrative offence; it covers accommodation and maintenance but the applicant still needs own health insurance. — [oesterreich.gv.at, Haftungserklärung](https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/de/themen/menschen_aus_anderen_staaten/aufenthalt/3/Seite.120230) (updated 10.08.2026); [BMI Haftungserklärung form (V2025-06-26)](https://www.bmi.gv.at/312/60a/files/B/029_2025_Haftungserklaerung_V20250626_bf.pdf); [BMI explanation 08/2026](https://www.bmi.gv.at/312_en/60a/files/b/explanation/19-aufenthaltsbewilligung-student-erklaerung-en-bf-20261006.pdf); [RIS § 64 NAG](https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/i/2005/100/P64/NOR40278006)
- Conditional admission (entrance exam) case: funds and accommodation "only have to be 'shown credibly' for the time being (e.g. by a confirmation of maintenance payments by your parents, a preliminary rental contract or reservation of a room in a student hall of residence)". — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme); [Embassy, Studying in Austria](https://www.bmeia.gv.at/en/austrian-embassy-new-delhi/travels-to-austria/studying-in-austria)
- Renewal: the applicant must show how the funds proven in the first procedure were actually used and prove sufficient funds again. — [BMI brochure 2026](https://www.bmi.gv.at/312/files/broschuere_unterhaltsberechnung_publikation_a4_2026_converted_webbf.pdf)

## 5. Process and timeline from admission letter to entry

### Takeaway
Sequence: admission letter → documents/apostille → e-mail embassy for appointment → personal filing in New Delhi (fee, fingerprints) → file forwarded to the Austrian residence authority → decision within a statutory maximum of 90 days (clock effectively restarts/extends if documents are missing) → embassy notifies → apply for Visa D within 3 months of notification (issued for 4 months) → enter, register address within 3 working days, enrol, take out insurance → collect the card in person within 6 months of notification and within the visa's validity. Official advice is to file at least 3 months, ideally 6 months, before planned entry and to start preparing documents up to 12 months ahead; there is no legal earliest date and no application deadline.

### Cited Findings
- Decision deadline in law (new since 7 Aug 2026): decisions on residence titles are to be taken "unverzüglich, längstens jedoch binnen 90 Tagen"; the possible 30-day extension for complex cases "gilt nicht in Verfahren zur Erteilung einer 'Aufenthaltsbewilligung' gemäß §§ 64 oder 67". — [RIS, § 19a NAG "Entscheidungsfrist", in force 07.08.2026 (BGBl. I Nr. 81/2026)](https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/i/2005/100/P19a/NOR40277978); also "Die Niederlassungsbehörde hat über den Antrag unverzüglich, längstens jedoch binnen 90 Tagen zu entscheiden." — [oesterreich.gv.at](https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/de/themen/menschen_aus_anderen_staaten/aufenthalt/3/2/1/1/Seite.120121)
- OeAD on realistic duration: "The decision period for the 'Residence Permit-Student' is 90 days, but is extended by a further 90 days if additional documents need to be submitted!"; "If all necessary documents have been submitted, the residence authority will process your application within 90 days." — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme)
- When to apply: "It is advisable to submit the application and all necessary documents at least three months before the planned entry ... It is advisable to submit your initial application ... six months in advance, if possible." FAQ: "We recommend that you start preparing the necessary documents 12 months in advance, if possible"; "the waiting time for an appointment to apply for a visa/residence permit may vary depending on the workload of the relevant embassy! Therefore, please book your appointment in advance." — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme); [OeAD FAQ](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/translate-to-english-faq-zu-einreise-und-aufenthalt-fuer-drittstaatsangehoerige-studierende). Embassy: "apply at least three months before the intended entry". — [Embassy, Studying in Austria](https://www.bmeia.gv.at/en/austrian-embassy-new-delhi/travels-to-austria/studying-in-austria)
- No deadline/earliest date: "Die Antragstellung ist an keine Fristen gebunden." — [oesterreich.gv.at](https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/de/themen/menschen_aus_anderen_staaten/aufenthalt/3/2/1/1/Seite.120121); MA 35: "Für die Aufenthaltsbewilligung Student gibt es keine Fristen und Termine. Berücksichtigen Sie aber die Inskriptions- und Anmeldefristen der Hochschule". — [MA 35](https://www.wien.gv.at/amtswege/aufenthaltsbewilligung-student)
- After approval: "the representative authority in your country of residence will inform you accordingly and, upon application, will issue a visa D for you to enter Austria. You have to apply for this visa within three months of receiving the notification ... You can then collect your residence permit at the residence authority in Austria within 6 months of receiving the notification; in any case you have to collect it within the validity period of your visa." — [Embassy, Studying in Austria](https://www.bmeia.gv.at/en/austrian-embassy-new-delhi/travels-to-austria/studying-in-austria). Law: the authority informs the embassy "zwecks Ausstellung eines Visums für die einmalige Einreise (§ 21 iVm § 25 Abs. 1 FPG)"; the notification lapses if the visa is not applied for "binnen drei Monaten", and the procedure is discontinued if the title is not collected "binnen sechs Monaten ab Mitteilung". — [RIS, § 23 NAG](https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/i/2005/100/P23/NOR40198397)
- "The representative authority will issue the Visa D for a validity period of four months." — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme)

- Entrance-exam variant: with a conditional admission ("Bedingter Zulassungsbescheid") apply at least 3 months before the exam date; Visa D (4 months) allows travel to sit the exam; then submit the definitive admission plus concrete funds/accommodation proof to the residence authority and collect the permit within the visa's validity; if the exam is long before the semester, a Visa C for the exam period is possible. — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme); [Embassy, Studying in Austria](https://www.bmeia.gv.at/en/austrian-embassy-new-delhi/travels-to-austria/studying-in-austria)
- Fee must be paid for the process to move: "If you do not pay the fees, you cannot get a residence permit ... If you pay later, your procedure will take longer." — [BMI explanation 08/2026](https://www.bmi.gv.at/312_en/60a/files/b/explanation/19-aufenthaltsbewilligung-student-erklaerung-en-bf-20261006.pdf)

### Inferences
- Entering on a Schengen Visa C to collect the card: no official source provides for it. The law and the Embassy name a Visa D (national visa for single entry) as the instrument for a visa-required national to enter and collect; the only C-visa mention is for sitting an early entrance exam. Treat "enter on a C visa and collect" as UNVERIFIED / not the designed route.
- Backward planning for a winter-semester start (1 October): to have 90 days decision time + ~15 working days for Visa D + appointment lead time, the embassy filing should happen by about May–June (OeAD's "six months" would mean ~April); for a summer-semester start (1 March), by about September–November of the previous year.
- The 6-month collection window and 4-month Visa D validity mean an approval obtained very early can still be used; an applicant should not delay the Visa D application beyond 3 months after notification or the approval lapses.

### Gaps
- What happens if the permit is not ready by the university's enrolment deadline: no immigration source addresses this; it depends on each university's admission period/grace period ("Nachfrist") and whether the admission letter can be re-issued for the next semester — to be taken from the admissions research, not verified here. UNVERIFIED.
- Actual current processing times at MA 35 or other authorities for student first applications are not published on the pages checked.
- Whether the Visa D for collection is filed at the Embassy or at a VFS centre (and in which city) is not stated officially; the embassy's notification letter decides. VFS's "Long-Term Student Visa" category is described only as study "more than 90 days and up to 6 months".

## 6. India-specific rules: CMMPA, document verification, biometrics, interview, German

### Takeaway
The India–Austria Comprehensive Migration and Mobility Partnership Agreement (BGBl. III Nr. 127/2023, in force 1 September 2023) creates no special student track: it restates that Indian students get the normal student residence permit "within the framework of applicable national and European Union law" and commits Austria only to "explore ways and means" to issue permits quickly. No official source shows an APS-style or lawyer-based verification of Indian documents for Austria (India is an Apostille country for Austria); fingerprints are taken at application; no German-language proof is required for the student permit.

### Cited Findings
- CMMPA Art. 3(1): "a residence permit for students, to date, enables Indian nationals to temporary residence for the purpose of study at recognized higher education institutions. The temporary residence may be prolonged ... if progress towards the degree is shown." Art. 3(4): "The Austrian Party, within the framework of applicable national and European Union law, issues residence permits ... to eligible Indian nationals for the purposes of study. The Austrian Party shall explore ways and means to see that the student residence permits are issued as quickly as possible while taking into account the academic exigencies." Art. 3(5): after graduation Indian students "may be granted permission to continue their temporary residence ... for the purpose of seeking employment for up to 12 months". Art. 4(4): "Austria undertakes efforts to step up activities in India in order to enhance and promote possibilities for higher education in Austria." Details are in a "non-binding Explanatory letter". — [Agreement text (Federal Chancellery, Council of Ministers annex)](https://www.bundeskanzleramt.gv.at/dam/jcr:48210014-f019-487b-b077-e50cf4bd6115/48_18_beilage_en.pdf)
- Publication and entry into force: "Abkommen über eine umfassende Partnerschaft für Migration und Mobilität (Indien)", BGBl. III Nr. 127/2023, Inkrafttretensdatum 01.09.2023 (RIS consolidated-law metadata via the official open-data API). — [RIS, BGBl. III Nr. 127/2023](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/III/2023/127)
- Document authentication for India = Apostille by the Indian MEA; no embassy legalisation: see [Embassy New Delhi, Legalisation](https://www.bmeia.gv.at/en/austrian-embassy-new-delhi/service-for-citizens/legalization); Vienna list "Indien: Apostille" — [Stadt Wien Länderliste](https://www.wien.gv.at/zusammenleben/beglaubigungen-ahs-info)
- Lawyer-based document review exists only in the "full diplomatic legalisation" track: "In some countries (e.g., Pakistan) an additional document review procedure will be carried out by trusted lawyers of the Austrian representative authority, which can take up to several months" — India is not in that track. — [OeAD info sheet Legalisation (April 2025)](https://oead.at/fileadmin/Dokumente/oead.at/OeAD_Stamm/Nach_Oesterreich/Einreise_und_Aufenthalt/Datenbank_Einreise_Aufenthalt/Infoblaetter/Englisch/Allgemeine_Info_-_Beglaubigung_en.pdf)
- Contrast (lead): Germany does not accept Indian apostilles and runs its own verification of Indian documents — that German regime does not apply to Austria. — [German Missions in India](https://india.diplo.de/in-en/-/2673230)
- Biometrics: "Bei der Antragstellung hat der Fremde die erforderlichen erkennungsdienstlichen Daten zur Verfügung zu stellen ... andernfalls ist sein Antrag zurückzuweisen" (§ 19(4) NAG); if not technically possible at the embassy, the domestic authority takes them (§ 19(5)). MA 35: "Wenn Sie eine Aufenthaltsbewilligung beantragen, werden von Ihnen Fingerabdrücke abgenommen." For the Visa D: ten fingerprints and photo unless stored in the last 59 months. — [RIS § 19 NAG](https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/i/2005/100/P19/NOR40277976); [MA 35](https://www.wien.gv.at/amtswege/aufenthaltsbewilligung-student); [Embassy, How to apply?](https://www.bmeia.gv.at/en/austrian-embassy-new-delhi/travels-to-austria/entry-and-residence/how-to-apply)
- Interview: no separate interview is prescribed for the residence permit beyond personal filing; for visas "The Embassy may call applicants for an interview if deemed necessary." — [Embassy checklist PDF Aug 2026](https://www.bmeia.gv.at/fileadmin/user_upload/Vertretungen/New_Delhi/ENG/Visa_for_Students_Research_and_Internship_Checklist_updated_AUG_2026.pdf)
- German language: "German before immigration" (A1) applies only to first applications for "Red-White-Red Card Plus", "Settlement Permit" and "Settlement Permit – Special Cases of Gainful Employment" etc.; the student residence permit is not on the list. — [OeAD info sheet "Proof of knowledge of German and Integration Agreement" (Dec 2025)](https://oead.at/fileadmin/Dokumente/oead.at/OeAD_Stamm/Nach_Oesterreich/Einreise_und_Aufenthalt/Datenbank_Einreise_Aufenthalt/Infoblaetter/Englisch/Allgemeine_Info_-_Deutsch_vor_Zuzug_und_Integrationsvereinbarung_en_01.pdf). Law: § 21a(1) NAG requires German only for titles under § 8(1) Z 2, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9 or 10, whereas the "Aufenthaltsbewilligung" is § 8(1) Z 12. — [RIS § 21a NAG](https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/i/2005/100/P21a/NOR40277981); [RIS § 8 NAG](https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/i/2005/100/P8/NOR40277972)
- Embassy warning relevant to Indian applicants: "The use of so-called visa agencies, which demand large sums of money to obtain visas, is explicitly warned against ... Any claims that a large amount of money is required in order to obtain a visa are false." — [Embassy New Delhi, Visa](https://www.bmeia.gv.at/en/austrian-embassy-new-delhi/travels-to-austria/entry-and-residence/visa)

### Inferences
- The CMMPA changes nothing procedural for a student applicant: same permit, same documents, same fees, same embassy route. Its practical relevance is political (commitment to speed, post-study 12-month job-search stay which already exists in § 64(4) NAG).
- Austria has no APS certificate requirement for the residence permit (none appears in any Austrian source read). Universities may impose their own document-authentication rules for admission — outside this note's scope.

### Gaps
- The CMMPA's non-binding Explanatory Letter was not found/read.
- No official statement that literally says "no document verification for Indian applicants"; the conclusion rests on the Apostille rule and the absence of any verification requirement on embassy/OeAD/BMI pages. A press item about "200 Indian students ... through a partnership with VFS Education Services" (WION, April 2026) was seen in search results but not verified and is not an immigration rule.

## 7. Validity and renewal in Austria

### Takeaway
The first card is valid for 12 months (shorter if the passport expires earlier; 2 years only for EU/multilateral mobility programmes or inter-university agreements such as joint masters); renewal is filed in person in Austria before expiry (earliest 3 months before) with proof of study success of at least 16 ECTS or 8 semester hours in the past academic year, continued enrolment, funds, insurance and a credit-bureau extract.

### Cited Findings
- "If all requirements are met, the 'Residence Permit – Student' will be issued for a period of 12 months. If you applied for a shorter term or your travel document is valid for a shorter period of time, your residence permit will be issued for a shorter period." — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme); MA 35: max. 12 months; if the passport is not valid that long the permit is limited to passport validity; 2 years only with a mobility programme or inter-university agreement ("zum Beispiel bei Joint Master-Programmen"). — [MA 35](https://www.wien.gv.at/amtswege/aufenthaltsbewilligung-student); law § 64(6) NAG — [RIS](https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/i/2005/100/P64/NOR40278006)
- Renewal timing: apply "before your residence permit expires; however, at the earliest three months before expiry ... Until a decision about your application for a renewal is taken you are considered to be lawfully resident in Austria"; a late application counts as a first application with no continuous right of residence. — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme); [oesterreich.gv.at](https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/de/themen/menschen_aus_anderen_staaten/aufenthalt/3/2/1/1/Seite.120121)
- Study success: "proof of academic success in the scope of eight semester hours per academic year or 16 ECTS credits per academic year", plus "Fortsetzungsbestätigung" and "Studienbuchblatt"; doctoral students may use a supervisor's confirmation. — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme); MA 35: "mindestens 16 ECTS-Punkten oder 8 Semesterwochenstunden im vergangenen Studienjahr". — [MA 35](https://www.wien.gv.at/amtswege/aufenthaltsbewilligung-student); law: § 64(2) NAG and § 8 NAG-DV (Studienerfolgsnachweis nach § 74 Abs. 6 UG, Studienblatt, Studienbestätigung) — [RIS § 64 NAG](https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/i/2005/100/P64/NOR40278006); [RIS § 8 NAG-DV](https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/ii/2005/451/P8/NOR40278854)
- Hardship clause: renewal possible despite missing study success if the reasons are outside the student's sphere of influence, unavoidable or unforeseeable (§ 64(2) NAG). — [RIS § 64 NAG](https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/i/2005/100/P64/NOR40278006)
- Renewal documents additionally: "current extract from the files of an officially recognised credit reference agency (e.g., KSV 1870)", funds, insurance, regular-expenses declaration; fee again EUR 218. — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme); [MA 35](https://www.wien.gv.at/amtswege/aufenthaltsbewilligung-student)
- Work: employment only with an AMS work permit applied for by the employer; up to 20 hours/week without labour-market test. — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme); [migration.gv.at](https://www.migration.gv.at/en/types-of-immigration/temporary-residence/)
- After graduation: one-time 12-month renewal for job search/business start-up, then switch to Red-White-Red Card, Blue Card EU, etc. (§ 64(4) NAG). — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme); [RIS § 64 NAG](https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/i/2005/100/P64/NOR40278006)
- ÖGK student insurance: confirmation of continued enrolment must be sent to ÖGK by 31 December each year. — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme)
- Travel during pending renewal requires an emergency visa ("Notvignette"). — [MA 35](https://www.wien.gv.at/amtswege/aufenthaltsbewilligung-student)

### Inferences
- An Indian passport should be valid for at least the full 12 months after the planned collection date (ideally the whole programme) to avoid a shortened first card.
- Because the 12 months run from issue, the first renewal falls roughly at the start of the third semester of a 2-year master's; 16 ECTS in the first academic year is a low bar for a full-time master's student (normal load 60 ECTS/year).

### Gaps
- None material for this question.

## 8. Common refusal reasons and pitfalls (per official guidance) + source-access log

### Takeaway
Official sources do not publish refusal statistics or an India-specific refusal list; the pitfalls that official guidance itself flags are: unproven or unexplained origin of funds, money not genuinely available/accessible from Austria or not in the applicant's name, under-calculated funds (rent above EUR 386.43, insurance, the age-24 jump, the 1 January rate change), a police certificate older than 3 months or not apostilled, incomplete files (which stretch the 90-day period), late filing, missing the 3-month Visa D / 6-month collection deadlines, and relying on outdated fee/amount figures.

### Cited Findings
- General grant conditions that lead to refusal if unmet: stay must not conflict with public interests; all-risk health insurance valid in Austria; stay must not lead to a financial burden on a public authority (§ 11(2) Z 1, 3, 4 NAG; students exempt only from Z 2 accommodation title). — [RIS § 11 NAG](https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/i/2005/100/P11/NOR40239020); [RIS § 64 NAG](https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/i/2005/100/P64/NOR40278006)
- Origin and availability of money: the applicant must prove on own initiative that maintenance is secured for the intended stay, "die Herkunft der ... Mittel nachzuweisen", that he has a legal claim to them "und die Mittel nicht aus illegalen Quellen stammen"; money abroad must be demonstrably accessible from Austria. — [BMI brochure 2026](https://www.bmi.gv.at/312/files/broschuere_unterhaltsberechnung_publikation_a4_2026_converted_webbf.pdf). OeAD lists "Bank statement of the last 6 months (including payments into the account, payment orders, etc.)" and sponsor declarations as the evidence that may be requested. — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme). MA 35: "Bei Sparguthaben: Erklärung, woher das Geld kommt" and "Überweisungen von dritten Personen, zum Beispiel Großeltern oder anderen Verwandten, zählen nicht zum Netto-Einkommen." — [Stadt Wien, Gesicherter Lebensunterhalt](https://www.wien.gv.at/zusammenleben/einwanderung-aufenthalt-gesicherter-lebensunterhalt)
- Incomplete applications delay or sink the case: "If you have to submit additional documents, this means that your application is incomplete ... Submit the required documents as soon as possible"; decision period "extended by a further 90 days if additional documents need to be submitted". — [OeAD FAQ](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/translate-to-english-faq-zu-einreise-und-aufenthalt-fuer-drittstaatsangehoerige-studierende); [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme). Embassy (visa checklist): "submission of incomplete or faulty documentation can be a reason for refusal"; "missing documents may be considered non-existent by the authority, which can result in the application being rejected." — [Embassy checklist PDF Aug 2026](https://www.bmeia.gv.at/fileadmin/user_upload/Vertretungen/New_Delhi/ENG/Visa_for_Students_Research_and_Internship_Checklist_updated_AUG_2026.pdf)
- Legalisation lead time: "If legalisations are required, it is advisable to start the process even earlier"; PCC "not older than 3 months at the time of application (legalised and afterwards translated)". — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme)
- Fee is lost on refusal/withdrawal: "The fee will not be refunded, even if the application is refused or withdrawn." — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme)
- Hard deadlines after approval: Visa D must be applied for within 3 months of notification or the notification lapses and the procedure is discontinued; card must be collected within 6 months. — [RIS § 23 NAG](https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/i/2005/100/P23/NOR40198397)
- One purpose, one application: an application showing different purposes, several simultaneous applications, or further applications during a pending procedure are inadmissible (§ 19(2) NAG). — [RIS § 19 NAG](https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/i/2005/100/P19/NOR40277976)
- The Embassy cannot speed things up or give status: "the Consulate cannot give any information on current proceedings nor influence the processing time"; contact the Austrian authority directly. — [Embassy, Settlement and Residence](https://www.bmeia.gv.at/en/austrian-embassy-new-delhi/travels-to-austria/entry-and-residence/settlement-and-residence)
- Stale information trap: the Embassy's own student page still says EUR 120 and a university hand-out still says EUR 672.64/month, versus official 2026 values of EUR 218 and EUR 722.58/1,308.39. — [Embassy, Studying in Austria](https://www.bmeia.gv.at/en/austrian-embassy-new-delhi/travels-to-austria/studying-in-austria); [FH JOANNEUM hand-out](https://cdn3.fh-joanneum.at/media/2025/11/Documents-for-Admission-Visa-India.pdf)

### Inferences
- "Sudden large deposits" are not named verbatim in official guidance, but the 6-month statement and origin-of-funds requirements make an unexplained recent lump sum the obvious weak point; a parental declaration plus the parents' own 6-month statements and income proof is the officially listed way to explain it.
- Students aged 23 at filing should budget at the 24+ rate if they turn 24 during the permit year (BMI forecast rule), and anyone whose permit will be issued in 2027 should budget at 2027 rates.
- Applying less than ~3 months before semester start is the most avoidable failure mode given the 90-day statutory period plus appointment and Visa D time.
