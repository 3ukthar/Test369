## 1. Post-arrival checklist with deadlines (registration, permit card, enrolment, bank, SIM, transport)

### Takeaway
The hard deadlines are: register your address within 3 days of moving in; collect the residence permit card in person before the 4-month Visa D runs out (and within 6 months of the approval notice); and pay the tuition + ÖH fee inside the university's payment period, because the admission letter alone does not make you an admitted student. The card is only handed over against the Meldezettel, the final admission and (if you declared ÖGK insurance) the ÖGK insurance confirmation, so the practical order is: move in → Meldezettel → finish enrolment and pay → ÖGK insurance → residence authority.

### Cited Findings
**Residence registration (Meldezettel)**
- Deadline: "Innerhalb von drei Tagen nach dem Bezug der Unterkunft" (within three days of moving into the accommodation). No fee. [last updated 13.01.2026] — [oesterreich.gv.at, Anmeldung eines Wohnsitzes](https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/de/themen/persoenliche_dokumente_und_bestaetigungen/an__abmeldung_des_wohnsitzes/Seite.1180200)
- OeAD phrases it as "within three working days of arrival in Austria"; studyinaustria.at also says "within three working days of entering the country". The legal rule on the government page counts from moving in, not from entry. — [OeAD, Residence Permit – Student](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme); [studyinaustria.at, Stay](https://studyinaustria.at/en/live-and-work/stay)
- Where: the registration authority for the new address — municipal office (Gemeindeamt), in statutory cities the Magistrat, in Vienna the Magistratisches Bezirksamt. — [oesterreich.gv.at](https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/de/themen/persoenliche_dokumente_und_bestaetigungen/an__abmeldung_des_wohnsitzes/Seite.1180200)
- Documents: completed Meldezettel form signed by the accommodation provider (owner, main tenant, or dorm), one form per person; for foreigners a travel document (passport); the page gives passport and birth certificate as examples of documents proving name, date/place of birth and nationality. — [oesterreich.gv.at](https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/de/themen/persoenliche_dokumente_und_bestaetigungen/an__abmeldung_des_wohnsitzes/Seite.1180200)
- How: in person, by post, or by a messenger; online only with ID Austria/EU Login, and the digital service requires that you were already registered in Austria once before — so a new arrival registers in person. Fax/e-mail not allowed. — [oesterreich.gv.at](https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/de/themen/persoenliche_dokumente_und_bestaetigungen/an__abmeldung_des_wohnsitzes/Seite.1180200)
- You may only register after you actually moved in; a registration without really living there is a punishable "Scheinmeldung". Failure to register is an administrative offence with a fine up to EUR 726 (up to EUR 2,180 for repeat offences). — [oesterreich.gv.at](https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/de/themen/persoenliche_dokumente_und_bestaetigungen/an__abmeldung_des_wohnsitzes/Seite.1180200)
- No Meldezettel needed if you stay in tourist accommodation (hotel, guest house, Airbnb etc.) for a maximum of 2 months; the hotel registers you in its guest register. Any later change of address must be re-registered; deregister when leaving Austria. — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme); [studyinaustria.at](https://studyinaustria.at/en/live-and-work/stay)

**Collecting the residence permit card**
- Sequence for Indian nationals: apply at the Austrian Embassy New Delhi, wait for the decision in India; after a positive decision apply for a Visa D, which is issued "for a validity period of four months"; enter and collect the card in Austria. — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme); [Austrian Embassy New Delhi](https://www.bmeia.gv.at/en/austrian-embassy-new-delhi/travels-to-austria/studying-in-austria/)
- Deadlines: apply for the Visa D within 3 months of the embassy's notification; collect the permit within 6 months of the notification, and in any case within the visa's validity. — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme); [oesterreich.gv.at, Aufenthaltsbewilligung – Student, last updated 03.08.2026](https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/de/themen/menschen_aus_anderen_staaten/aufenthalt/3/2/1/1/Seite.120121)
- A visa cannot be extended in Austria ("Visa C and Visa D cannot be extended in Austria"). — [OeAD info sheet Renewal, April 2025](https://oead.at/fileadmin/Dokumente/oead.at/OeAD_Stamm/Nach_Oesterreich/Einreise_und_Aufenthalt/Datenbank_Einreise_Aufenthalt/Infoblaetter/Englisch/Allgemeine_Info_-_Verlaengerung_von_Aufenthaltstiteln_en.pdf)
- What to bring to collect: "the residence registration ('Meldezettel') and the final admission at the Austrian university. If it was stated prior to entry that a student self-insurance will be taken out upon arrival in Austria, the confirmation of insurance must also be collected from 'Österreichische Gesundheitskasse' and subsequently presented". The FAQ adds "a current bank statement". The authority may ask for more. — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme); [OeAD FAQ](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/translate-to-english-faq-zu-einreise-und-aufenthalt-fuer-drittstaatsangehoerige-studierende)
- Competent authority: the residence authority for your place of residence — district authority (Bezirkshauptmannschaft), in statutory cities the Magistrat, in Vienna MA 35, in Graz the Office of the Styrian Provincial Government. Collection is in person; some authorities require an appointment. — [oesterreich.gv.at, last updated 03.08.2026](https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/de/themen/menschen_aus_anderen_staaten/aufenthalt/3/2/1/1/Seite.120121)
- The first permit is issued for 12 months (shorter if the passport expires earlier); it is a credit-card-format ID, to be carried at all times; it allows up to 90 days in any 180 days in other Schengen states. — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme)
- Fee: "For applications one application fee of EUR 218 applies", payable in full at application, not refunded on refusal. oesterreich.gv.at also says "Antragsgebühr: 218 Euro" [03.08.2026]. **Conflict:** the Austrian Embassy New Delhi page still says "A fee of EUR 120 is payable for the residence permit" (undated; looks outdated). — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme); [oesterreich.gv.at](https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/de/themen/menschen_aus_anderen_staaten/aufenthalt/3/2/1/1/Seite.120121); contradicted by [Embassy New Delhi](https://www.bmeia.gv.at/en/austrian-embassy-new-delhi/travels-to-austria/studying-in-austria/)

**University enrolment, fee payment, student ID** (example: University of Vienna; every institution has its own procedure)
- "Sie haben Ihren Zulassungsbescheid erhalten? Das bedeutet noch nicht, dass Sie zum Studium zugelassen sind." (Receiving the admission letter does not yet mean you are admitted.) The letter is valid for two semesters. — [Uni Wien, master's admission with external degree](https://studieren.univie.ac.at/zulassung/master/extern)
- Steps: complete the admission within the admission period (tell the admissions office via Servicedesk that you will start; the university "reserves the right to call you to an online appointment"), then pay the tuition/ÖH fee; "when the prescribed fee has arrived … you have completed the admission. Then you can order and validate your student ID, the u:card", and download the enrolment confirmation and Studienblatt. — [Uni Wien, Zulassungsverfahren abschließen](https://studieren.univie.ac.at/zulassungsverfahren-abschliessen/ba)
- Pay the exact amount within the payment period, no part payments; if you underpay, the study is closed automatically after the period. Payment periods published on 10 Nov 2026: summer semester 2027: 11 January – 31 March 2027; winter semester 2027/28: 12 July – 31 October 2027. Online-banking transfers can take up to 7 days to arrive; eps transfer needs an Austrian bank account; Visa/Mastercard accepted. — [Uni Wien, Einzahlung des Studien-/ÖH-Beitrags](https://studieren.univie.ac.at/studieren-und-lernen/finanzielles/studien-/oeh-beitrag/einzahlung-meldung-der-fortsetzung)
- Amounts for winter semester 2026/27 — **UNVERIFIED (search snippets of university pages, pages not opened)**: tuition for third-country nationals EUR 726.72 per semester plus ÖH fee EUR 26.20 = EUR 752.92 (Uni Wien total; JKU states ÖH fee EUR 26.20 "Stand WS 2026/27"); payment period for WS 2026/27 at Uni Wien 13 July – 31 October 2026. — [JKU, ÖH- und Studienbeitrag](https://www.jku.at/studium/studierende/dein-studienstart/oeh-und-studienbeitrag/); [Uni Wien](https://studieren.univie.ac.at/studieren-und-lernen/finanzielles/studien-/oeh-beitrag/einzahlung-meldung-der-fortsetzung)

**Bank account**
- Bank Austria: free student account "Für alle bis 30 Jahre" (students aged 18 to 30); welcome offer for first-time openings until 30.11.2026. — [Bank Austria Studentenkonto](https://www.bankaustria.at/privatkunden-konto-und-karten-studentenkonto.jsp)
- Erste Bank / Sparkassen: student account with free account management "bis zum 27. Geburtstag", for "Deviseninländer:innen" who are regular (ordentliche) students of an Austrian university/UAS; EUR 20 bonus for online opening until 26.02.2027. — [Erste Bank und Sparkassen Studentenkonto](https://www.sparkasse.at/sgruppe/privatkunden/konto-karten/studentenkonto)
- The University of Vienna's fastest fee-payment route (eps) "is only possible with an Austrian bank account". — [Uni Wien](https://studieren.univie.ac.at/studieren-und-lernen/finanzielles/studien-/oeh-beitrag/einzahlung-meldung-der-fortsetzung)

**Public transport (Vienna)**
- 2026 prices as stated by WU Vienna: students up to 26 buy the annual "Jahreskarte Jugend" for EUR 294 online/app or EUR 300 at a ticket office; "Students older than 26 should purchase … Jahreskarte WIEN" at EUR 461 online or EUR 467 at a ticket office; the nationwide Klimaticket is another option. — [WU Vienna, Public Transport](https://www.wu.ac.at/en/programs/international-and-exchange-students/international-students/welcome-to-vienna/public-transport)
- The old Vienna "Semesterticket" was replaced in 2026 by the youth annual pass for everyone under 26 — **UNVERIFIED on wienerlinien.at** (site blocked by CAPTCHA); stated by university pages and a Wiener Linien factsheet found via search. — [Akademie der bildenden Künste, offers and discounts](https://www.akbild.ac.at/en/studies/general-study-information/faqs/offers-and-discounts-for-students); [WU Vienna](https://www.wu.ac.at/en/programs/international-and-exchange-students/international-students/welcome-to-vienna/public-transport)

### Inferences
- Because an Indian master's student with work experience is often 26 or older, the cheap youth pass (under 26) and Erste Bank's free account (under 27) may not apply; Bank Austria's limit (30) and the full-price Vienna annual pass (EUR 461/467 in 2026) are the relevant figures for that age group.
- With a 4-month Visa D and a residence authority that wants Meldezettel + final admission + ÖGK confirmation, arriving only days before the visa expires is risky; arriving at the start of the visa validity leaves room for the appointment at MA 35/BH.
- Open the Austrian bank account early: it is needed for the ÖGK direct debit and makes fee payment same-day.

### Gaps
- Exact documents banks require from a third-country student (typically passport, Meldezettel, enrolment confirmation, residence permit): **UNVERIFIED** — not stated on the bank pages opened.
- SIM card: no official source found. That prepaid SIMs must be registered with photo ID is **UNVERIFIED**.
- Wiener Linien's own page (prices, eligibility, announced 2027 price increases) could not be opened; transport offers in Graz, Linz, Innsbruck, Salzburg were not researched.
- MA 35's own student pages (appointment booking, Vienna-specific checklists) were not found at the old URLs; rely on OeAD + oesterreich.gv.at and the appointment system of the local authority.
- Whether a given university insists on showing original documents in person at enrolment differs by institution (Uni Wien: possible online appointment); check the admission letter.

## 2. Health insurance (ÖGK student self-insurance, private alternatives, acceptance for the permit)

### Takeaway
ÖGK student self-insurance costs EUR 78.84 per month in 2026 (2027 amount not published as of 10 Nov 2026), starts the day after application with no waiting period for benefits, and is the standard way to meet the "all-risk health insurance" requirement. It has eligibility limits tied to the Student Support Act (income ceiling, study duration, changes of programme, a prior completed degree combined with employment) but no age limit is listed. Private policies are accepted only with specific all-risk wording; ordinary travel insurance is not enough for the permit.

### Cited Findings
- "Die Selbstversicherung kostet 2026 monatlich EUR 78,84." / "78.84 euros (as of 2026)". — [ÖGK, Selbstversicherung für Studierende](https://www.oegk.at/cdscontent/?contentid=10007.868713); [ÖGK leaflet "Self-insurance for students" (EN PDF)](https://www.oegk.at/cdscontent/load?contentid=10008.751795); same figure "[as of 2026]" on [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme)
- Who can take it: regular (ordentliche) students at Austrian universities, universities of applied sciences etc., participants of university preparation courses; not compulsorily insured elsewhere; habitual residence in Austria. — [ÖGK](https://www.oegk.at/cdscontent/?contentid=10007.868713)
- Exclusions (ÖGK page): changed studies more than twice or after the third enrolled semester; total study duration exceeded by more than four semesters plus one tolerance semester (Student Support Act); income above the "Zuverdienstgrenze" of the Student Support Act, "adjusted annually for inflation". — [ÖGK](https://www.oegk.at/cdscontent/?contentid=10007.868713)
- Exclusions (ÖGK leaflet): income above the maximum in § 49(3) StudFG per calendar year; and "if you have already completed a higher education programme within the meaning of the StudFG 1992 prior to your new studies" — but the preferential insurance remains possible "if you are self-insured and do not pursue self-employment or employment during your university studies. Income from gainful employment up to 551.10 euros per month (marginal earnings threshold 2026) is not taken into account." "A master's programme following a bachelor's programme does not count as a second degree programme provided the field of study remains the same." — [ÖGK leaflet (EN PDF)](https://www.oegk.at/cdscontent/load?contentid=10008.751795)
- The Student Support Act earnings ceiling ("Zuverdienstgrenze") for calendar year 2026 is EUR 17,677 (ordinance of 23 July 2026). — [BGBl. II Nr. 221/2026, copy on stipendium.at](https://www.stipendium.at/fileadmin/Download/Dokumente/BGBLA_2026_II_221.pdf)
- No age limit appears in the ÖGK conditions or leaflet. — [ÖGK](https://www.oegk.at/cdscontent/?contentid=10007.868713)
- How to apply: written application to the ÖGK of the province where you live while studying (online via "Meine ÖGK" needs ID Austria; otherwise the paper form). Documents: completed signed form, photo ID / passport copy, Meldezettel, current Studienblatt / confirmation of study periods, current enrolment or continuation confirmation, proof of previous insurance periods in the last 12 months. — [ÖGK](https://www.oegk.at/cdscontent/?contentid=10007.868713); [ÖGK leaflet](https://www.oegk.at/cdscontent/load?contentid=10008.751795)
- Start and waiting period: insured "mit dem Tag nach der Antragstellung" (from the day after the application); "You are entitled to all ÖGK benefits in kind from the start of self-insurance." No sick pay or maternity pay. — [ÖGK](https://www.oegk.at/cdscontent/?contentid=10007.868713); [ÖGK leaflet](https://www.oegk.at/cdscontent/load?contentid=10008.751795)
- Minimum term 6 months before you can cancel (unless compulsory insurance through a job above the marginal threshold starts or you move abroad). If you quit voluntarily or two monthly contributions are unpaid, the insurance ends and there is a 6-month block before you can re-apply. Contributions are due on the 1st of the month and must arrive within 15 days; direct debit recommended. — [ÖGK](https://www.oegk.at/cdscontent/?contentid=10007.868713)
- Ongoing duty: send the continuation/enrolment confirmation for every semester; OeAD: by 31 December each year a valid continuation confirmation covering the academic year must be submitted to ÖGK. Report changes (job with insurance, address) within one week. — [ÖGK leaflet](https://www.oegk.at/cdscontent/load?contentid=10008.751795); [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme)
- It ends when the requirements cease, and at the latest at the end of the third calendar month after the end of the academic year in which you were last enrolled / after the last exam date. — [ÖGK](https://www.oegk.at/cdscontent/?contentid=10007.868713)
- Dependants (spouse, registered partner, children) can be co-insured; spouses are free of charge only under certain conditions. — [ÖGK leaflet](https://www.oegk.at/cdscontent/load?contentid=10008.751795)
- If the student conditions are not met, ordinary voluntary self-insurance is possible instead. [last updated 23.01.2026] — [oesterreich.gv.at, Checkliste Studierende – Sozialversicherung](https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/de/themen/bildung_und_ausbildung/hochschulen/universitaet/1/1/Seite.160223)
- For the permit: degree students "fulfil the requirement of a 'health insurance covering all risks' if they take out student insurance with the ÖGK after entering Austria and enrolling"; a written statement that you will do so can be attached to the application. For entry you need travel health insurance (cover "well over EUR 30,000"), which is "only sufficient for the period until" the all-risk insurance is taken out. — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme)
- Private alternative: allowed "(e.g. via insurance agency www.feelsafe.at or other insurance providers)", but it must "essentially" match the statutory insurance, have no "waiting periods, cost caps or exclude certain risks", "must pay for medical treatment in Austria directly", and the insurer must give "an explicit written statement" confirming this. — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme)
- Vienna MA 35: a travel health insurance taken out for the entry visa "ist … nicht ausreichend" for the residence title application. MA 35 publishes a list of checked private insurances; any other policy must contain MA 35's exact "allgemeine Zusatzerklärung" (cover as ÖGK, no waiting or end periods, caps and deductibles only as at ÖGK). — [Stadt Wien / MA 35, accepted private health insurances](https://www.wien.gv.at/verwaltung/einwanderung/ahs-info/aufenthalt-krankenversicherungen.html)
- On that MA 35 list: Care Concept AG "Care Austria" and "Care Austria Education" need no additional declaration (but end automatically when a permanent residence title is granted); all other Care Concept products "besonders Care Economy, Care Au Pair, Care Expatriate, Care College, Care Student" need the additional declaration; Expat & Co "Student Insurance" needs the declaration; some listed policies are only concluded for one year. — [Stadt Wien / MA 35](https://www.wien.gv.at/verwaltung/einwanderung/ahs-info/aufenthalt-krankenversicherungen.html)
- MA 35 also notes: self-insurance with ÖGK is fine if there is no waiting period; "if you have a waiting period, you must bridge it with private insurance." — [Stadt Wien / MA 35](https://www.wien.gv.at/verwaltung/einwanderung/ahs-info/aufenthalt-krankenversicherungen.html)

### Inferences
- An Indian master's student already holds a completed degree. Read literally, the ÖGK leaflet then makes the cheap student rate depend on not working above the marginal threshold (EUR 551.10/month in 2026). In practice a job above that threshold brings compulsory insurance through the employer anyway, so the self-insurance would end; but a freelance (Werkvertrag) income above the threshold could cost the student rate. Whether a foreign bachelor's degree counts as a completed programme "within the meaning of the StudFG" is not stated — confirm with ÖGK when applying.
- If the 2027 contribution follows the general revaluation factor for 2027 (1.046, PRELIMINARY per KPMG), it would be roughly EUR 82.5; this is my arithmetic, not a published figure.
- Because ÖGK cover only starts the day after application and needs Meldezettel + enrolment confirmation, the travel insurance must cover at least the first weeks in Austria.

### Gaps
- 2027 ÖGK student contribution: not published on oegk.at on 10 Nov 2026.
- Cost and waiting period of ordinary (non-student) ÖGK self-insurance: **UNVERIFIED** (not found on an official page).
- FeelSafe price: about EUR 77.75/month "as of 2026" appears only on third-party pages (home4students, a broker site) — **UNVERIFIED**; feelsafe.at English page returned 404. Which FeelSafe tariff maps to which Care Concept product on the MA 35 list was not verified.
- Authorities outside Vienna may apply their own practice on private policies; only MA 35's list was checked.
- The § 49(3) StudFG amount referred to by the ÖGK leaflet was not read in the statute; the EUR 17,677 figure is the ordinance's "Zuverdienstgrenze gemäß § 29 … StudFG" for 2026.

## 3. Housing (how to find it, rents, deposits, timing, scams)

### Takeaway
Austrian universities do not allocate rooms; you must book a dorm or find a flat-share yourself, months ahead. The Student Social Survey 2025 puts average student housing cost at EUR 586/month (Vienna EUR 596), dorm rooms from non-profit providers start around EUR 400–530 in Vienna, and OeAD student housing in Vienna was already fully booked for winter semester 2026.

### Cited Findings
- "Austria does not have a tradition of on-campus accommodation … Austrian universities or UAS do not automatically allocate a room when you register for your course. Rather, you have to search for accommodation yourself." Options listed: dormitories, OeAD student housing, shared flats (WG), private rentals. Quicklinks: oeadstudenthousing.at, home4students.at, oeh.ac.at, stuwo.at, oejab.at, viennabase.at. — [studyinaustria.at, Accommodation](https://studyinaustria.at/en/live-and-work/accomodation)
- Official monthly budget guideline: accommodation approx. EUR 450–700, food approx. EUR 300, studies and personal needs approx. EUR 500, total "approx. 1,300 Euros"; source given as Statistik Austria / Studierenden-Sozialerhebung 2025 (IHS, 2026). — [studyinaustria.at, Living costs](https://studyinaustria.at/en/live-and-work/living-costs)
- Student Social Survey 2025 (IHS for the science ministry): students not living with parents spend on average EUR 586/month on housing (+7% vs 2023, +51% vs 2015); highest averages at small university locations (EUR 630), Klagenfurt (EUR 604) and Vienna (EUR 596); dorm costs rose 61% since 2015; about a third of dorm residents pay up to EUR 400/month and 15% pay over EUR 600; average total student costs EUR 1,294/month (2023: EUR 1,224). — [BMFWF, Medieninfo SOLA 2025](https://www.bmfwf.gv.at/dam/jcr:378db906-500d-4dec-885d-894f9f2d7295/Medieninfo_SOLA_2025.pdf)
- home4students (non-profit dorm operator) all-inclusive price list for academic year 2025/26 (dated 01.2025): Vienna single rooms EUR 395–535/month, place in a twin room EUR 289–325; Graz singles EUR 434–505; Innsbruck EUR 455–505; Salzburg EUR 505; Klagenfurt EUR 369–413. "The earlier you register via the application form, the greater the chance of getting a place in your preferred dorm." — [home4students Preisliste 2025/26](https://home4students.at/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/home4students-Preisliste_2025_26-DE.pdf)
- OeAD student housing, listing viewed 10 Nov 2026: Vienna offers "from" EUR 340 to EUR 1,290/month (26 houses, median "from" price about EUR 551); Innsbruck from EUR 314–488; Linz from EUR 249–258; Klagenfurt from EUR 290–406; Leoben from EUR 394. — [OeAD student housing, Vienna](https://www.oeadstudenthousing.at/en/accommodation/vienna/)
- "VIENNA: fully booked WS/2026" — applications for Vienna for winter semester 2026 were closed due to high demand; bookings open again for stays starting December 2026; alternative offered: The Social Hub at EUR 718–740/month with promo code. — [OeAD student housing news](https://www.oeadstudenthousing.at/en/news/applications-for-the-winter-semester-2026-now-closed/)

## 4. Working while studying (third-country students)

### Takeaway
A work permit (Beschäftigungsbewilligung) is still required for any employed job, including marginal ones; the employer applies to the AMS before the job starts. Up to 20 hours per week it is granted without a labour-market test, for bachelor's and master's students alike; above 20 hours a labour-market test applies. Work must not undermine study as the purpose of stay. A student job helps with running costs but cannot be relied on to finance living, and cannot be used for the first permit.

### Cited Findings
- "Studierende aus Drittstaaten unterliegen dem Ausländerbeschäftigungsgesetz (AuslBG) und benötigen für eine Beschäftigung in Österreich (auch für eine geringfügige) eine Beschäftigungsbewilligung. Diese muss von der Arbeitgeberin/dem Arbeitgeber beim Arbeitsmarktservice beantragt werden. Für eine Beschäftigung bis zu 20 Wochenstunden können Beschäftigungsbewilligungen ohne Arbeitsmarktprüfung erteilt werden. Eine Beschäftigung über 20 Wochenstunden ist zwar nicht ausgeschlossen, allerdings kann diese nur genehmigt werden, wenn die Stelle nicht mit … vorgemerkten inländischen oder integrierten ausländischen Arbeitskräften besetzt werden kann (Arbeitsmarktprüfung)." [last updated 01.01.2026, responsible: labour ministry] — [oesterreich.gv.at, Studieren und Arbeiten für Drittstaatsangehörige](https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/de/themen/bildung_und_ausbildung/hochschulen/universitaet/1/1/Seite.160224)
- Same rule on OeAD: permit for "up to 20 hours/week. This involves small-scale employment ('geringfügige Beschäftigung') as well. This permit will be granted without a labour market check." The employer applies "before the employee takes up the job". No distinction between bachelor's and master's level is made. — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme); [OeAD info sheet Working, April 2025](https://oead.at/fileadmin/Dokumente/oead.at/OeAD_Stamm/Nach_Oesterreich/Einreise_und_Aufenthalt/Datenbank_Einreise_Aufenthalt/Infoblaetter/Englisch/Allgemeine_Info_-_Arbeiten_en.pdf)
- Statute: work permits may be issued to "Schülern und Studenten (Abs. 3 Z 5) für eine Beschäftigung, die 20 Wochenstunden nicht überschreitet" without the labour-market test; Abs. 3 Z 5 covers holders of a student permit under "§ 64 Abs. 1 und 4 NAG". — [§ 4 AuslBG (jusline reproduction, stand 10.11.2026)](https://www.jusline.at/gesetz/auslbg/paragraf/4)
- AMS: apply at the AMS office for the place of work (Vienna: Service AusländerInnenbeschäftigung); documents: application, valid residence permit, current proof of studies, passport, Meldezettel; "Die Arbeitszeit darf nicht höher als 20 Wochenstunden sein" — otherwise a labour-market test (Ersatzkraftverfahren). [page updated 3 October 2022] — [AMS, Beschäftigung ausländischer Schüler_innen und Student_innen](https://www.ams.at/unternehmen/service-zur-personalsuche/beschaeftigung-auslaendischer-arbeitskraefte/beschaeftigung-auslaendischer-schuelerinnen-und-schueler--studen)
- Time: the AMS must decide on work-permit applications "binnen sechs Wochen" (within six weeks); if the decision is not delivered in time, the employer may start employing the person (with exceptions). — [§ 20a AuslBG (jusline)](https://www.jusline.at/gesetz/auslbg/paragraf/20a); [§ 20b AuslBG (jusline)](https://www.jusline.at/gesetz/auslbg/paragraf/20b)
- Study must stay the main purpose: "Die Ausübung einer Erwerbstätigkeit richtet sich nach dem AuslBG. Diese Erwerbstätigkeit darf das Erfordernis des Studiums als ausschließlichen Aufenthaltszweck nicht beeinträchtigen." — [§ 64 (3) NAG (jusline, in force since 07.08.2026)](https://www.jusline.at/gesetz/nag/paragraf/64)
- Sanction warning: "If you break them, you risk a heavy fine or even the loss of your residence permit." — [OeAD info sheet Working, April 2025](https://oead.at/fileadmin/Dokumente/oead.at/OeAD_Stamm/Nach_Oesterreich/Einreise_und_Aufenthalt/Datenbank_Einreise_Aufenthalt/Infoblaetter/Englisch/Allgemeine_Info_-_Arbeiten_en.pdf)
- **Conflict:** the OeAD FAQ says "In the summer and winter months, more hours can usually be worked." Neither the statute nor the ministry page contains a holiday exception; above 20 hours a labour-market test is required. Treat the FAQ sentence as unreliable. — [OeAD FAQ](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/translate-to-english-faq-zu-einreise-und-aufenthalt-fuer-drittstaatsangehoerige-studierende); contradicted by [oesterreich.gv.at](https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/de/themen/bildung_und_ausbildung/hochschulen/universitaet/1/1/Seite.160224)
- Marginal-employment threshold (Geringfügigkeitsgrenze): EUR 551.10 per month in 2026. — [ÖGK leaflet "Self-insurance for students"](https://www.oegk.at/cdscontent/load?contentid=10008.751795)
- 2027 (PRELIMINARY, "noch nicht offiziell kundgemacht"): the threshold stays at EUR 551.10 under the Budgetbegleitgesetz 2027/2028; revaluation factor 2027 = 1.046; monthly maximum contribution base rises from EUR 6,930 (2026) to EUR 7,410 (2027). — [KPMG Austria, Sozialversicherungswerte 2027 (October 2026)](https://kpmg.com/at/de/media/newsletter/tax-news/2026/10/tn-kmu-sozialversicherungswerte-2027.html)
- Self-employment / freelance: work under a genuine contract for work (Werkvertrag) "requires no work permit", but social insurance, tax and trade law apply and you must handle them yourself; "It is not the name of the contract that is relevant but the type of work that is actually carried out" — fixed hours, fixed workplace and instructions make it employment needing a permit. OeAD advises asking AK, AMS or WKO before starting. — [OeAD info sheet Working, April 2025](https://oead.at/fileadmin/Dokumente/oead.at/OeAD_Stamm/Nach_Oesterreich/Einreise_und_Aufenthalt/Datenbank_Einreise_Aufenthalt/Infoblaetter/Englisch/Allgemeine_Info_-_Arbeiten_en.pdf)
- No permit needed for activities in EU exchange/research programmes or in academic teaching and research. — [OeAD info sheet Working](https://oead.at/fileadmin/Dokumente/oead.at/OeAD_Stamm/Nach_Oesterreich/Einreise_und_Aufenthalt/Datenbank_Einreise_Aufenthalt/Infoblaetter/Englisch/Allgemeine_Info_-_Arbeiten_en.pdf)
- Internships: internships required by the Austrian curriculum (Berufspraktikum) and unpaid traineeships (Volontariat, up to three months per calendar year) need no work permit, but the employer must notify the AMS at the latest three weeks before the start and the AMS issues a confirmation. — [oesterreich.gv.at, 01.01.2026](https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/de/themen/bildung_und_ausbildung/hochschulen/universitaet/1/1/Seite.160224); [§ 3 (5) AuslBG (jusline)](https://www.jusline.at/gesetz/auslbg/paragraf/3); [OeAD info sheet Working](https://oead.at/fileadmin/Dokumente/oead.at/OeAD_Stamm/Nach_Oesterreich/Einreise_und_Aufenthalt/Datenbank_Einreise_Aufenthalt/Infoblaetter/Englisch/Allgemeine_Info_-_Arbeiten_en.pdf). The business portal still says "two weeks" [page dated 1 January 2025] — outdated versus the statute: [usp.gv.at](https://www.usp.gv.at/themen/mitarbeiter-und-gesundheit/einstellung-mitarbeiter-und-arten-der-beschaeftigung/weitere-informationen-auslaendische-beschaeftigte/berechtigungen-zur-beschaeftigung-fuer-arbeitgeber/beschaeftigungsbewilligung-schueler-studierende-studienabsolventen.html)
- What students actually earn (all students in Austria, not only internationals), Student Social Survey 2025: 68% work during the semester, on average 20.2 hours/week; average earned income of working students EUR 1,179/month; average total budget EUR 1,662/month, average costs EUR 1,294/month; 57% of working students have difficulty combining work and study; study effort drops noticeably from 11 hours of work per week. — [BMFWF, Medieninfo SOLA 2025](https://www.bmfwf.gv.at/dam/jcr:378db906-500d-4dec-885d-894f9f2d7295/Medieninfo_SOLA_2025.pdf)
- Employer reluctance: in a WKÖ/Central European University survey of 378 international master's graduates (class of 2022/23; 2.9% Indian), "59 %" said they had received job rejections because employers did not want to apply for a work permit (extra administration or delay). — [WKÖ newsletter, 30 July 2025](https://www.wko.at/oe/news/2025-07-30-spik)
- First permit funds (2026 amounts): students under 24: EUR 722.58/month; from age 24: EUR 1,308.39/month; "You have to prove these funds for twelve months in advance" in a savings book or bank account in the applicant's name accessible from Austria (alternatives: declaration of guarantee by an EU resident, scholarship); the authority may ask for the origin of funds. — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme)

### Inferences
- Twelve months × EUR 1,308.39 = EUR 15,700.68 for a student aged 24 or over (EUR 8,670.96 under 24), 2026 values, before adding rent above EUR 386.43/month and insurance. These figures normally rise each January; 2027 values were not published.
- Future Austrian wages cannot be counted for the first permit: the money must already be in an account for 12 months ahead, and a work permit can only be requested once a valid residence permit exists (AMS document list). No official page says this in one sentence; it follows from the two rules above.
- A marginal job yields at most EUR 551.10/month (2026 and, preliminarily, 2027) — under half of the EUR 1,294 average student cost. A 20-hour job at survey-average pay (about EUR 1,179) would roughly cover a frugal budget, but depends on finding an employer willing to file for the permit and, usually, on German.
- The 20-hour rule also applies during the 12-month job-search extension, because § 4 AuslBG refers to student permits under § 64 (1) and (4) NAG.
- Pay slips from a permitted job can be shown as proof of means at renewal (oesterreich.gv.at lists pay slips and employment contracts among general proofs of secured subsistence).

- Typical hourly wages / collective-agreement minimums for student jobs in 2026 — not verified on an official page (the trade-union PDF could not be downloaded).

## 5. Residence permit renewal

### Takeaway
File the renewal in person in Austria before the card expires, at the earliest three months before; a late application is treated as a new first application. You must show 16 ECTS (or 8 semester hours) of study success per academic year, continued enrolment, funds, insurance and a credit-agency extract; the fee is EUR 218.

### Cited Findings
- Apply "at the competent residence authority in Austria (in Vienna: at the competent branch office of MA35) before your residence permit expires; however, at the earliest three months before expiry". "Until a decision … is taken you are considered to be lawfully resident in Austria, even if your current residence permit expires in the meantime." The new card is issued only after the old one expires. — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme)
- In person; "Danach gilt der Antrag als Erstantrag und Sie haben kein durchgehendes Aufenthaltsrecht" (after expiry the application counts as a first application). [03.08.2026] — [oesterreich.gv.at](https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/de/themen/menschen_aus_anderen_staaten/aufenthalt/3/2/1/1/Seite.120121)
- Late filing can only be treated as a renewal if an unforeseen/unavoidable event caused the delay, the applicant bears at most minor fault, and the application is filed within two weeks after the obstacle ends (§ 24 (2) NAG); otherwise it generally has to be filed abroad. — [OeAD info sheet Renewal, April 2025](https://oead.at/fileadmin/Dokumente/oead.at/OeAD_Stamm/Nach_Oesterreich/Einreise_und_Aufenthalt/Datenbank_Einreise_Aufenthalt/Infoblaetter/Englisch/Allgemeine_Info_-_Verlaengerung_von_Aufenthaltstiteln_en.pdf)
- **Conflict:** the OeAD FAQ says the renewal "must be submitted at least three months in advance of the expiry". The OeAD main page, the OeAD info sheet ("maximum 3 months before") and oesterreich.gv.at all say the opposite: before expiry, no earlier than three months before. — [OeAD FAQ](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/translate-to-english-faq-zu-einreise-und-aufenthalt-fuer-drittstaatsangehoerige-studierende); contradicted by [oesterreich.gv.at](https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/de/themen/menschen_aus_anderen_staaten/aufenthalt/3/2/1/1/Seite.120121)
- Study success: "proof of academic success in the scope of eight semester hours per academic year or 16 ECTS credits per academic year", plus continuation confirmation (Fortsetzungsbestätigung) and record of studies (Studienbuchblatt). With two parallel programmes the success of the main programme counts; after a change of programme the authority must be informed and may ask for the previous programme's results. — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme)
- Statute: renewal for study is only permitted with proof of study success; it can still be renewed without it if the reasons were outside the student's sphere of influence, unavoidable or unforeseeable. — [§ 64 (2) NAG (jusline)](https://www.jusline.at/gesetz/nag/paragraf/64)
- Other renewal documents: passport, photo, proof of funds (same 2026 amounts: EUR 722.58 under 24 / EUR 1,308.39 from 24, twelve months in advance, extra funds if rent exceeds EUR 386.43), accommodation, all-risk health insurance, declaration of regular expenses, and "current extract from the files of an officially recognised credit reference agency (e.g., KSV 1870 Information GmbH)". — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme)
- Fee: EUR 218 per application. — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme)
- Travelling while the renewal is pending and the card has expired requires a "Notvignette" (emergency visa) in the passport; valid 3 months, once per renewal procedure, fee-based. — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme); [OeAD info sheet Renewal](https://oead.at/fileadmin/Dokumente/oead.at/OeAD_Stamm/Nach_Oesterreich/Einreise_und_Aufenthalt/Datenbank_Einreise_Aufenthalt/Infoblaetter/Englisch/Allgemeine_Info_-_Verlaengerung_von_Aufenthaltstiteln_en.pdf)

### Inferences
- A first permit issued in, say, September 2026 for 12 months must be renewed between June and September 2027; exam results for 16 ECTS should be on the transcript by then, so a weak first semester has to be made up in the second.
- The 2026 funds figures apply to applications decided in 2026; a renewal filed in 2027 will be measured against 2027 amounts not yet published.

### Gaps
- 2027 reference amounts for funds (ASVG rates) — not published as of 10 Nov 2026.
- Cost of the KSV extract and of the Notvignette — not checked.
- Whether MA 35 accepts renewal applications online — not verified; the government page says in person.

## 6. After graduation: job-search year, Red-White-Red Card for graduates, RWR Card plus, EU Blue Card, permanent residence, citizenship

### Takeaway
After finishing the degree you can renew the student permit once for 12 months to look for work or start a business, then switch in Austria to a Red-White-Red Card for graduates: no points, no labour-market test, and — since 1 October 2022 — no fixed minimum salary; the pay must match the locally customary salary of comparable Austrian graduates. After 21 months of qualifying employment within 24 months you can get the RWR Card plus (free labour-market access); long-term residence needs 5 years of settlement with student years counted at half, plus B1 German.

### Cited Findings
**12-month extension (§ 64 (4) NAG)**
- Statute: third-country nationals who successfully completed a degree programme and aim for a title under §§ 41 (RWR Card), 42 (EU Blue Card) or 43c (settlement permit – researcher) can have the student permit extended "einmalig zum Zweck der Arbeitssuche oder der Unternehmensgründung für die Dauer von zwölf Monaten", in a renewal procedure (§ 24 (1)), provided the general requirements "mit Ausnahme des § 11 Abs. 2 Z 2" (i.e. except proof of accommodation) continue to be met. — [§ 64 (4) NAG (jusline, in force since 07.08.2026)](https://www.jusline.at/gesetz/nag/paragraf/64)
- Government wording: once only, 12 months, general requirements (except proof of locally customary accommodation) must be met; document needed: written proof of successful completion of studies; the application must be filed in time before the student permit expires. [03.08.2026 / 07.08.2026] — [oesterreich.gv.at, Aufenthaltsbewilligung – Student](https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/de/themen/menschen_aus_anderen_staaten/aufenthalt/3/2/1/1/Seite.120121); [oesterreich.gv.at, RWR-Karte für Studienabsolventen](https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/de/themen/menschen_aus_anderen_staaten/aufenthalt/3/2/2/Seite.120229)
- Conditions named by migration.gv.at: "general requirements under Austrian settlement and residence law (such as sufficient means of subsistence, health insurance coverage etc.)". — [migration.gv.at, Graduates](https://www.migration.gv.at/en/types-of-immigration/permanent-immigration/graduates/)
- From this status a change of purpose is only allowed to §§ 41, 42, 43c or 47 (2) NAG. — [§ 64 (5) NAG (jusline)](https://www.jusline.at/gesetz/nag/paragraf/64)
- A direct switch from the student permit to any RWR Card variant, without the search year, is also possible. — [oesterreich.gv.at, 07.08.2026](https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/de/themen/menschen_aus_anderen_staaten/aufenthalt/3/2/2/Seite.120229)

**Red-White-Red Card for graduates**
- Who: graduates of an Austrian university, university of applied sciences or accredited private university with a completed bachelor's, master's, doctoral/PhD programme (or diploma programme from the second stage). — [migration.gv.at, Graduates](https://www.migration.gv.at/en/types-of-immigration/permanent-immigration/graduates/)
- Conditions: an employment offer "which matches your level of qualification"; "you get paid the locally customary gross minimum salary which comparable Austrian graduates (junior employees) would receive"; health insurance. "For graduates, there is no points system." No labour-market test. — [migration.gv.at, Graduates](https://www.migration.gv.at/en/types-of-immigration/permanent-immigration/graduates/)
- Statute: "ein monatliches Bruttoentgelt …, das mindestens dem ortsüblichen Entgelt inländischer Studienabsolventen mit einer vergleichbaren Tätigkeit und Berufserfahrung entspricht … Bei Studienabsolventen gemäß Z 2 entfällt die Arbeitsmarktprüfung im Einzelfall." [in force since 01.10.2022] — [§ 12b AuslBG (jusline)](https://www.jusline.at/gesetz/auslbg/paragraf/12b)
- No numeric threshold for 2026 or 2027: the Austrian Business Agency states that the minimum-salary requirement for graduates "ist mit 1. Oktober 2022 entfallen. Die Entlohnung muss nach den kollektivvertraglichen Regelungen geschehen bzw. nach betriebsüblicher Überbezahlung." — [ABA Work in Austria blog, 9 January 2023](https://www.workinaustria.com/ueber-uns/blog/neue-services-der-aba-fuer-internationale-studierende)
- General requirements apply except proof of accommodation and of sufficient means. — [oesterreich.gv.at, 07.08.2026](https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/de/themen/menschen_aus_anderen_staaten/aufenthalt/3/2/2/Seite.120229)
- Procedure: usually a change-of-purpose application filed in person at the residence authority in Austria (the employer may file instead); the authority forwards it to the AMS, which checks degree and pay; decision "innerhalb von acht Wochen"; apply at least eight weeks before the job starts; as a rule you may work only after receiving the card. Documents: passport, photo, degree certificate, employer's declaration (Arbeitgebererklärung), health insurance. Fee EUR 218. — [oesterreich.gv.at, 07.08.2026](https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/de/themen/menschen_aus_anderen_staaten/aufenthalt/3/2/2/Seite.120229); [§ 41 (3) NAG (jusline)](https://www.jusline.at/gesetz/nag/paragraf/41)
- Timing warning: "the application should be filed at the latest on the day the student permit expires. Only then can the periods acquired as a student be credited at half towards 'Daueraufenthalt – EU'." — [oesterreich.gv.at, 07.08.2026](https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/de/themen/menschen_aus_anderen_staaten/aufenthalt/3/2/2/Seite.120229)
- Validity and rights: issued "in der Regel für bis zu zwei Jahre" (statute: two years, or contract length plus three months if shorter); tied to the named employer; subsidiary self-employment allowed. On a change of employer the new job may be started provisionally after a 45-day waiting period from a complete application (rule introduced with the August 2026 amendment). Family members can get an RWR Card plus. — [oesterreich.gv.at](https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/de/themen/menschen_aus_anderen_staaten/aufenthalt/3/2/2/Seite.120229); [migration.gv.at, Graduates](https://www.migration.gv.at/en/types-of-immigration/permanent-immigration/graduates/); [§ 41 NAG (jusline)](https://www.jusline.at/gesetz/nag/paragraf/41)
- August 2026 amendment (BGBl. I Nr. 81/2026, implementing Directive (EU) 2024/1233): employer-change rule with provisional start after 45 days, and a combined permit may not be withdrawn for unemployment within the first six months. — [Government bill 443 d.B. XXVIII. GP, Vorblatt](https://www.parlament.gv.at/dokument/XXVIII/I/443/imfname_1746965.pdf)

**Comparison thresholds**
- RWR Card "other key workers" (points system, labour-market test): minimum EUR 3,465 gross per month in 2026 (statute: 50% of the monthly maximum contribution base, plus special payments). — [migration.gv.at, Other key workers](https://www.migration.gv.at/en/types-of-immigration/permanent-immigration/other-key-workers/); [§ 12b Z 1 AuslBG (jusline)](https://www.jusline.at/gesetz/auslbg/paragraf/12b)
- EU Blue Card 2026: degree of at least three years, binding job offer matching the education, and "at least the average gross annual income of full-time employees (in 2026: at least € 55,678; annual salary plus special payments)"; labour-market test applies; no points; issued for 24 months; fee EUR 218. — [migration.gv.at, EU Blue Card](https://www.migration.gv.at/en/types-of-immigration/permanent-immigration/eu-blue-card/)

**RWR Card plus**
- Available to RWR Card holders "if you were employed in accordance with the eligibility requirements … for at least 21 months during the preceding 24 months" (same for Blue Card holders); gives fixed-term settlement with unlimited labour-market access, employed or self-employed, any employer. — [migration.gv.at, RWR Card plus](https://www.migration.gv.at/en/types-of-immigration/permanent-immigration/redwhiteredcardplus/)
- German: for the switch to RWR Card plus an A2 integration certificate is "in der Regel" required, but proof of general university entrance qualification (e.g. a degree from a recognised university) can replace it. — [ABA Work in Austria blog, 30 June 2025](https://www.workinaustria.com/blog/deutschkenntnisse-in-oesterreich-was-internationale-fachkraefte-wissen-sollten)

**Permanent residence ("Daueraufenthalt – EU")**
- Requirements: five years of uninterrupted actual settlement in Austria and completion of Module 2 of the Integration Agreement; gives unlimited settlement and unrestricted labour-market access; card renewed every five years. Fee EUR 275. [last updated 07.08.2026] — [oesterreich.gv.at, Daueraufenthalt – EU](https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/de/themen/menschen_aus_anderen_staaten/aufenthalt/3/2/Seite.120402)
- Student time: "Zeiten des Aufenthalts auf Grund einer Aufenthaltsbewilligung (zum Beispiel als Studentin/Student) … werden zur Hälfte angerechnet, wenn der Antragsteller jetzt zur Niederlassung berechtigt ist." Statute: the "unmittelbar vorangehenden" (immediately preceding) lawful stay on a residence permit counts at half. — [oesterreich.gv.at](https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/de/themen/menschen_aus_anderen_staaten/aufenthalt/3/2/Seite.120402); [§ 45 (2) NAG (jusline)](https://www.jusline.at/gesetz/nag/paragraf/45)
- The five-year period is broken by absences from Austria of more than ten months in total or more than six months in a row. The title lapses after more than 12 consecutive months outside the EEA. — [oesterreich.gv.at](https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/de/themen/menschen_aus_anderen_staaten/aufenthalt/3/2/Seite.120402)
- Module 2 means German at B1 level. — [ABA Work in Austria blog, 30 June 2025](https://www.workinaustria.com/blog/deutschkenntnisse-in-oesterreich-was-internationale-fachkraefte-wissen-sollten)

**Citizenship (brief)**
- General requirements: as a rule ten years of lawful, uninterrupted residence, at least five of them settled; no criminal record; secured livelihood (own regular income averaged over 36 months of the last six years); German via Module 2 and a civics test; "grundsätzlich Verlust der bisherigen Staatsangehörigkeit" (as a rule, loss of the previous nationality). [citizenship requirements page updated 29.01.2026] — [migration.gv.at, Staatsbürgerschaft](https://www.migration.gv.at/de/leben-und-arbeiten-in-oesterreich/rahmenbedingungen-der-integration/staatsbuergerschaft/); [oesterreich.gv.at, Allgemeine Einbürgerungsvoraussetzungen](https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/de/themen/menschen_aus_anderen_staaten/staatsbuergerschaft/1/Seite.260421)

### Inferences
- "Salary threshold for the graduate RWR Card" is therefore a case-by-case AMS assessment against the applicable collective agreement plus customary overpayment for the job, not a number that changes each January. Sites quoting a fixed 2026 figure for graduates are describing other RWR categories or the pre-October-2022 rule.
- Preliminary 2027 key-worker threshold: 50% of EUR 7,410 = EUR 3,705 gross/month (my arithmetic from KPMG's preliminary 2027 contribution base). The 2027 Blue Card figure was not published.
- Illustrative timeline to permanent residence: 2 years on a student permit + 1 job-search year (also a student "Aufenthaltsbewilligung") = 3 years counted at half = 1.5 years credit, leaving about 3.5 years on RWR Card / RWR Card plus — provided the switch is filed before the student permit expires and stays abroad remain under the limits. This is a reading of § 45 (2) NAG, not an official worked example.
- During the search year the funds and insurance requirements still apply (only accommodation proof is waived), so about EUR 15,700 (2026 rate, age 24+) or equivalent income/guarantee must be shown again; and ÖGK student self-insurance ends about three months after the last enrolled academic year, so different insurance is needed.
- For an Indian citizen, Austrian naturalisation normally means giving up Indian citizenship; India's own rules on this were not researched here (**UNVERIFIED**).

### Gaps
- How strictly authorities calculate funds for the search year and which insurance they accept in that year — no official detail found.
- Number of graduate RWR Cards issued per year and approval rates — not found.
- Blue Card 2027 threshold; any 2027 changes to fees — not published.

## 7. Job-market reality for English-speaking graduates

### Takeaway
Austria's own data show that most non-European graduates leave: only about one in three stayed 12 months after graduating (class of 2020/21), and the main reported reason is difficulty finding a job, with German below C1 and employer reluctance over permits as the chief obstacles. Unemployment stood at 7.3% in October 2026; shortages are real but concentrated in vocational occupations rather than English-language graduate roles.

### Cited Findings
- Statistics Austria figures as reported by the Austrian Economic Chamber: of about 16,000 international graduates in 2020/21, about 46% left Austria within 12 months; among non-Europeans "blieb … nur jede/r Dritte" (only one in three stayed); 83% of graduates from European third countries stayed. By field, more than 50% left from business/law, STEM (broad) and health, versus 30% in computer science and 38% in engineering. — [WKÖ newsletter, 30 July 2025](https://www.wko.at/oe/news/2025-07-30-spik)
- WKÖ/CEU survey (378 master's graduates of 2022/23 from 68 countries, 2.9% from India; not a representative sample): 75% of those who left named difficulty finding a job as the main reason; one third had no German before starting; only 12% were at C1 at survey time; "Meist werden akademische Bewerber erst ab diesem Niveau am Arbeitsmarkt interessant" (academic applicants usually only become interesting at that level); 44% lacked time to learn German alongside studies, 37% could not afford a course or certificate; 78% of those who could not find a job in Austria were third-country nationals. — [WKÖ newsletter, 30 July 2025](https://www.wko.at/oe/news/2025-07-30-spik)
- Labour market, end of October 2026: 314,499 registered unemployed (+1.3% year on year), national unemployment rate 7.3%; 389,941 including training participants; 124,905 of the unemployed are foreign nationals; Vienna 121,892 unemployed; 73,733 immediately available vacancies (−3.4%). AMS: "the economic upturn is not yet strong enough to reduce unemployment in Austria." — [AMS, Übersicht über den Arbeitsmarkt, Oktober 2026](https://www.ams.at/content/dam/download/arbeitsmarktdaten/%C3%B6sterreich/berichte-auswertungen/001_uebersicht_aktuell.pdf)
- Shortages (employer survey, n = 2,500, 2025): 78% of firms report labour/skills shortages; hardest-hit sectors are hospitality (69%) and food production (64%); hardest occupations to fill: cooks, retail/sales, drivers; 80% of firms have their greatest recruiting difficulty in positions requiring an apprenticeship qualification. — [WKÖ newsletter (Arbeitskräfteradar 2025), 30 July 2025](https://www.wko.at/oe/news/2025-07-30-spik)
- Students in "study-adequate" jobs: 37% of all students (54% of working students); the share is "particularly high" in computer science. — [BMFWF, Medieninfo SOLA 2025](https://www.bmfwf.gv.at/dam/jcr:378db906-500d-4dec-885d-894f9f2d7295/Medieninfo_SOLA_2025.pdf)
- German is not a legal requirement for the first RWR Card in most categories, but is needed later (A2 for RWR Card plus unless exempt; B1 mandatory for permanent residence). — [ABA Work in Austria blog, 30 June 2025](https://www.workinaustria.com/blog/deutschkenntnisse-in-oesterreich-was-internationale-fachkraefte-wissen-sollten)
- Support: ABA runs a "Talent Hub" job platform ("many also in English") and a free immigration advice service for international students and graduates. — [ABA Work in Austria blog, 9 January 2023](https://www.workinaustria.com/ueber-uns/blog/neue-services-der-aba-fuer-internationale-studierende)
- A legal shortage-occupation list exists for 2026 (Fachkräfteverordnung 2026, BGBl. II 316/2025, valid 1 Jan – 31 Dec 2026) and is published on the migration portal. — [migration.gv.at, bundesweite Mangelberufe](https://www.migration.gv.at/de/formen-der-zuwanderung/dauerhafte-zuwanderung/bundesweite-mangelberufe/) (page located via search, list content not opened — **UNVERIFIED** details)

### Inferences
- For a non-European master's graduate the base rate of staying is roughly one in three, better in computer science and engineering. The legal route is generous; the bottleneck is getting a degree-level job offer, which in most fields needs near-fluent German.
- Getting study-related work experience during the degree and reaching B2–C1 German are the two factors the survey links most directly to staying; both compete for time with the 16-ECTS requirement.
- The WKÖ is an employers' lobby arguing for easier retention, so its framing favours "barriers" explanations; the Statistics Austria leaving rates it quotes are the harder data.

### Gaps
- No field-specific or India-specific employment data for graduates was found; no data on how many English-only graduate jobs exist.
- Unemployment by education level (graduates) for 2026 was not extracted from AMS tables.
- The original Statistics Austria publication behind the 46% / one-in-three figures was not opened.
- Number of occupations on the 2026 shortage list and which graduate-level occupations it contains — not verified.

## 8. Bringing a spouse or children

### Takeaway
Family members (spouse or registered partner aged at least 21, unmarried minor children) can apply for a "Residence Permit – Family Community", normally at the embassy in India, but the couple must show EUR 2,064.12 per month plus EUR 201.88 per child (2026) for twelve months in advance, plus adequate housing and insurance.

### Cited Findings
- "Family members of students holding a 'Residence Permit – Student' can apply for a 'Residence Permit – Family Community'. Family members are spouses, partners in a civil union and unmarried minors. Spouses and partners in a civil union must be at least 21 years old at the time of application." They apply as a rule at the Austrian representation in their country of residence. — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme)
- Funds [as of 2026]: "Couples: EUR 2,064.12/month for both; For each child additionally: EUR 201.88/month", proven "for twelve months in advance" in a savings book or bank account accessible from Austria (alternative: scholarship confirmation); additional funds if accommodation costs exceed EUR 386.43/month; plus funds for health insurance. — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme)
- Other documents: passport, photo, accommodation for at least 3 months, all-risk health insurance, legalised and translated police clearance (not older than 3 months) and proof of family status (marriage/birth certificates, legalised then translated); at renewal a credit-agency extract. Fee EUR 218 per application (free for the first permit of a child born in Austria until age two). — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme)
- Health insurance: dependants can be co-insured under ÖGK student self-insurance; spouses are free of charge only under certain conditions. — [ÖGK leaflet](https://www.oegk.at/cdscontent/load?contentid=10008.751795)
- Housing: OeAD student housing "accommodations are not suitable for families"; only a few units for couples. — [OeAD housing FAQ](https://www.oeadstudenthousing.at/en/faq/167/how-can-i-book-family-accommodation/)
- Work by family members: the statute provides that for "Aufenthaltsbewilligung 'Familiengemeinschaft'" applications the AMS procedure applies "sofern dieser Aufenthaltstitel Zugang zum Arbeitsmarkt gewähren soll", with the AMS checking the requirements of § 4 (1) AuslBG except item 1. — [§ 20d AuslBG (jusline, in force since 07.08.2026)](https://www.jusline.at/gesetz/auslbg/paragraf/20d)
- Later: family members of RWR Card holders can receive an RWR Card plus (unrestricted labour-market access). — [migration.gv.at, RWR Card plus](https://www.migration.gv.at/en/types-of-immigration/permanent-immigration/redwhiteredcardplus/)

### Inferences
- Twelve months × EUR 2,064.12 = EUR 24,769.44 for a couple (2026), plus EUR 2,422.56 per child, plus rent above EUR 386.43/month and insurance — roughly EUR 9,000 more per year than the student alone (age 24+).
- A spouse on the family permit should not plan on immediate free access to work during the study phase; access depends on an AMS-checked procedure tied to a specific job. The practical details were not confirmed on an official explanatory page.

### Gaps
- Official plain-language guidance on work rights of a student's spouse — not found; only the statute cross-reference above.
- Processing time for family permits and whether a quota applies — not checked.
- Whether the spouse must show German before arrival for this permit type — not verified (OeAD's "German language skills" info sheet was not opened).

## 9. Official warnings and frequently reported pitfalls for Indian students

### Takeaway
No country-specific Austrian warning about Indian students was found; the Embassy of India in Vienna advises normal caution and asks students to register on the MADAD portal. The documented pitfalls are procedural: long processing and legalisation lead times, the short Visa D window, missed renewal deadlines, the 16-ECTS rule, unauthorised or mislabelled work, insurance gaps, sold-out housing, and the job-market/German barrier after graduation.

### Cited Findings
- Embassy of India, Vienna: "General travel advisory for students: Need to exercise normal caution. Take care of your belongings, identity papers and mobile in Metro, bus and crowded places. Avoid going to unknown places at night." Students studying in or being admitted to Austria "are requested to get themselves registered at the Madad portal of the Government of India". Officer for student matters: Deputy Chief of Mission, pol.vienna@mea.gov.in. [site last updated 16 October 2026] — [Embassy of India Vienna, Information for Indian students](https://eoivienna.gov.in/pages/NTcw)
- Application lead time: decision period for the student permit is 90 days, "extended by a further 90 days if additional documents need to be submitted"; OeAD advises applying six months ahead and starting document preparation 12 months ahead; embassy appointment waiting times vary; "Depending on the issuing country, it may take several months to legalise the documents". — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme); [OeAD FAQ](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/translate-to-english-faq-zu-einreise-und-aufenthalt-fuer-drittstaatsangehoerige-studierende)
- Austrian Embassy New Delhi: the application must be submitted in person in New Delhi; "All documents have to be legalised and documents in languages other than German have to be submitted together with a certified German translation." Police clearance must be no older than 3 months, legalised and then translated. — [Austrian Embassy New Delhi](https://www.bmeia.gv.at/en/austrian-embassy-new-delhi/travels-to-austria/studying-in-austria/); [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme)
- A tourist/other visa cannot be used to enter and then apply in Austria: "It is not permitted to circumvent the provisions of aliens law". — [OeAD FAQ](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/translate-to-english-faq-zu-einreise-und-aufenthalt-fuer-drittstaatsangehoerige-studierende)
- "Completeness of documents: Missing documents can lead to significant delays." Origin of funds may have to be proven (6 months of bank statements, sponsor's declaration and proof of income). — [OeAD](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/entry-and-residence/residence-permit-student-no-mobility-programme)
- Bureaucratic delay is itself a reported problem: surveyed international graduates named "langwierige Aufenthaltsverfahren bei den Aufenthaltsbehörden wie der MA 35" (lengthy residence procedures at authorities such as MA 35) as the main hurdle during studies. — [WKÖ newsletter, 30 July 2025](https://www.wko.at/oe/news/2025-07-30-spik)
- Work: breaking the employment rules risks "a heavy fine or even the loss of your residence permit"; a "Werkvertrag" that is really employment needs a permit regardless of its label. — [OeAD info sheet Working, April 2025](https://oead.at/fileadmin/Dokumente/oead.at/OeAD_Stamm/Nach_Oesterreich/Einreise_und_Aufenthalt/Datenbank_Einreise_Aufenthalt/Infoblaetter/Englisch/Allgemeine_Info_-_Arbeiten_en.pdf)
- Registration: registering an address where you do not live is punishable; not registering can be fined up to EUR 726. — [oesterreich.gv.at](https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/de/themen/persoenliche_dokumente_und_bestaetigungen/an__abmeldung_des_wohnsitzes/Seite.1180200)
- Insurance: two unpaid ÖGK contributions end the insurance and trigger a 6-month re-entry block; travel insurance is not accepted for the residence title. — [ÖGK](https://www.oegk.at/cdscontent/?contentid=10007.868713); [Stadt Wien / MA 35](https://www.wien.gv.at/verwaltung/einwanderung/ahs-info/aufenthalt-krankenversicherungen.html)
- Housing: OeAD Vienna was fully booked for winter semester 2026 before the semester began. — [OeAD student housing news](https://www.oeadstudenthousing.at/en/news/applications-for-the-winter-semester-2026-now-closed/)
- Official pages contradict each other in places (fee EUR 218 vs EUR 120; renewal "at least" vs "at most" three months before; "more hours in summer and winter"; internship notice two vs three weeks) — see sections 1, 4 and 5 for the sources on each side.

### Inferences
- The most consequential mistakes are date mistakes: letting the Visa D or the permit card expire converts a simple in-country step into a fresh application from India.
- Where OeAD summary pages and the statute/ministry pages differ, the statute and oesterreich.gv.at (dated August 2026) should be followed.
- The embassy fee figure (EUR 120) suggests the New Delhi page lags behind; bring enough to pay EUR 218 and confirm with the embassy/VFS before the appointment.
