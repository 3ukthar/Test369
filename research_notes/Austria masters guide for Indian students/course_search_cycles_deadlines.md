# Finding master's programmes in Austria; admission cycles and non-EU application deadlines (state as of 10 November 2026, for Summer 2027 and Winter 2027/28 intakes)

Reading conventions used throughout:
- "SS 2027" = summer semester 2027 (starts 1 March 2027). "WS 2027/28" = winter semester 2027/28 (starts 1 October 2027).
- **VERIFIED** = read on the institution's own page on 10 Nov 2026 (URL given; the academic year printed on the page is quoted).
- **GENERIC RULE** = the official page states a recurring day-month rule without a year (e.g. "winter semester: by 15 May"); I apply it to 2027 and say so.
- **PREVIOUS CYCLE** = only the 2026/27 dates are published; expect similar, re-check.
- **UNVERIFIED** = seen only in a search snippet or third-party page; not confirmed on the official page.
- Status column is relative to today, 10 Nov 2026.

## 1. Where and how do you search for master's programmes in Austria (especially English-taught)? How many exist and in which fields?

### Takeaway
There is one official national database (studienwahl.at, run for the ministry/OeAD, with a language filter and a dedicated "programmes in English" entry point), one official UAS-only portal (fachhochschulen.ac.at, with graduation/language/mode filters), and then each university's own catalogue. OeAD says about 600 programmes at about 70 institutions are taught in English (all levels); the UAS portal's live filter shows 94 English-language UAS master's out of 244 UAS master's.

### Cited Findings
- OeAD/Study in Austria points applicants to studienwahl.at/en: "over 2,500 degree programmes from more than 77 esteemed institutions"; English-taught ones at studienwahl.at/programmes-in-english — [Study in Austria – Find your programme](https://studyinaustria.at/en/plan-your-studies/find-your-programme)
- "Around 600 programmes are currently available that are taught in English" at "70 educational institutions" (figure covers all degree levels, not only master's) — [Study in Austria – Study programmes in English](https://studyinaustria.at/en/study/find-your-programme-1-1)
- studienwahl.at covers "all degree programmes of the Austrian universities, private universities, universities of applied sciences and university colleges of teacher education"; besides full-text search it filters by subject area, type of degree programme (bachelor's, master's, joint degree) and language ("it is possible to search systematically for English-taught degree programmes") — [OeAD – Options](https://oead.at/en/to-austria/studying/options)
- OeAD does not process applications; it tells applicants to contact the admissions office of the chosen institution and to verify programme details and entry prerequisites on the institution's own site — [Study in Austria – Application and admission](https://studyinaustria.at/en/plan-your-studies/application-and-admission)
- UAS-only portal ("Austrian Study Guide") filters: study area (Business, Engineering, Health, Life Sciences, Media & Design, Safety & Security, Social Affairs), graduation (Bachelor / Master / Course), institution, organisational form (Co-op / Extra-occupational / Full-time / Part-time), language (English / German), federal state — [fachhochschulen.ac.at – Courses of studies](https://www.fachhochschulen.ac.at/en/course-of-studies)
- Live count on 10 Nov 2026: filter "Master degree programme" = 244 matches; "Master degree programme" + "English" = 94 matches — [fachhochschulen.ac.at filtered search](https://www.fachhochschulen.ac.at/en/course-of-studies?tx_solr%5Bfilter%5D%5B0%5D=graduation%3AMaster+degree+programme&tx_solr%5Bfilter%5D%5B1%5D=language%3AEnglish)
- OeAD's UAS overview: "At present 640 UAS degree programmes ... are offered by 21 institutions. About 80 degree programmes are offered entirely in English." (all levels; "entirely in English" is narrower than the portal's language tag, which is why the portal shows 94 master's) — [Study in Austria – University of Applied Sciences](https://studyinaustria.at/en/study/institutions/university-of-applied-sciences)
- University of Vienna: "more than 120 master's programmes"; its catalogue lists master's alphabetically and split by "with selection procedure" / "without selection procedure"; each programme links to the Master Access Guide (MAG), which states eligible bachelor's degrees, language level, qualitative admission requirements and required documents — [Uni Wien – Master's programmes](https://studieren.univie.ac.at/en/find-your-degree-programme/masters-programmes); [Uni Wien – MAG](https://studieren.univie.ac.at/en/applying-for-a-programme/admission-info/mag)
- TU Wien lists 26 English-taught master's (incl. double degrees/Erasmus Mundus): Aeronautical Engineering; Biomedical Engineering; Building Sciences and Environment (selection procedure); Business Informatics; Cartography (Erasmus Mundus); Computational Science and Engineering; Data Science; Digital Civil Engineering Science (selection procedure); DD Logic and Computation; DD IT Security; Embedded Computing Systems; Financial and Actuarial Mathematics; Geodesy and Geoinformation; Green Chemistry (selection procedure); Information and Communication Engineering; Interdisciplinary Mathematics; Logic and Artificial Intelligence; Manufacturing and Robotics; Media and Human-Centered Computing; Medical Informatics; Software Engineering; Statistics-Probability-Mathematics in Economics; Quantum Information Science and Technology; Technical Chemistry; Technical Physics; Visual Computing — [TU Wien – English-taught Master's Programmes](https://www.tuwien.at/en/studies/studies/master-programmes/english-taught-masters-programmes)
- WU Vienna: "9 English-taught master's programs" (e.g. Business Communication, Digital Economy, Economics, International Management/CEMS, Marketing, Quantitative Finance, Socio-Ecological Economics and Policy, Supply Chain Management), all 4 semesters/120 ECTS, full-time — [WU – Master's programs](https://www.wu.ac.at/en/programs/masters-programs)
- TU Graz has a separate "Master's programmes taught in English" list on its master's overview page — [TU Graz – Overview master's degree programmes](https://www.tugraz.at/en/studying-and-teaching/degree-and-certificate-programmes/masters-degree-programmes/overview-masters-degree-programmes)
- UAS Technikum Wien: "offers several Master's degree programs in English ... The required language level is B2" — [FH Technikum Wien – Degree Programs in English](https://www.technikum-wien.at/en/degree-programs-in-english/)
- Erasmus Mundus joint master's (some with Austrian partners, e.g. TU Wien's Cartography) are listed in the EU's catalogue — [EACEA – Erasmus Mundus Catalogue](https://www.eacea.ec.europa.eu/scholarships/erasmus-mundus-catalogue_en)
- Typical English level asked: Uni Wien "for most master's programmes instructed in English you need proof of English language proficiency at level B2" (C1 for English studies master's) — [Uni Wien – English language proficiency](https://studieren.univie.ac.at/en/applying-for-a-programme/language-proficiency/english-language-proficiency); TU Wien "Competence in English Level B2" for English-taught master's — [TU Wien – Other (inter-)national Bachelor's degree](https://www.tuwien.at/en/studies/admission/masters-programmes/other-inter-national-bachelors-degree); Innsbruck B2 at time of application — [Uni Innsbruck – Master's Programme](https://www.uibk.ac.at/studium/anmeldung-zulassung/masterstudium/index.html.en)

### Inferences
- Practical search workflow: (1) studienwahl.at/programmes-in-english → set degree type "Master" → filter by subject; (2) for applied/part-time options repeat on fachhochschulen.ac.at with Master + English; (3) open the university's own programme page and, for Uni Wien/Klagenfurt, the Master Access Guide entry, because "eligible bachelor's degree" rules decide admission at public universities; (4) note for each programme: selection procedure yes/no, intake semester(s), non-EU deadline.
- English-taught master's cluster in engineering/computer science (TU Wien, TU Graz, JKU, UAS), business/economics (WU, Uni Wien, MCI, IMC Krems, FH), natural/life sciences (NAWI Graz, Uni Wien, BOKU). Bachelor's programmes are almost all in German, so the English offer is overwhelmingly at master's level (Uni Graz: "all of the Bachelor's/Diploma courses are [German]") — basis: [Uni Graz – Internationals](https://www.uni-graz.at/en/studies/admission-and-deadlines/internationals/).

## 2. How do public universities, Universities of Applied Sciences (FH/UAS) and private universities differ for a master's applicant?

### Takeaway
Public universities mostly admit by checking whether your bachelor's is "equivalent/eligible" (no cap on places, no interview) except for a growing list of master's with selection procedures; UAS have limited places and always run an entrance procedure (documents + interview/test), offer part-time formats and several application rounds; private universities set their own fees and procedures.

### Cited Findings
- System size: "23 Universities, 21 Universities of applied sciences, 19 private universities and higher education institutions, 14 Teacher education colleges"; international students are 30% of the student population — [Study in Austria – Higher education institutions](https://studyinaustria.at/en/study/institutions)
- Public universities: research and teaching "an inseparable entity"; comprehensive universities in Vienna, Graz, Innsbruck, Klagenfurt, Linz, Salzburg plus technical/medical/arts universities — [Study in Austria – Universities](https://studyinaustria.at/en/study/institutions/universities)
- UAS: "professionally-oriented higher education ... mandatory career-oriented practical training units or job-based internships. The number of students per year and programme is limited, and for this reason most of the programmes have an entrance procedure"; "UAS offer full-time and extra-occupational study programmes" — [Study in Austria – University of Applied Sciences](https://studyinaustria.at/en/study/institutions/university-of-applied-sciences)
- UAS entrance procedure: "all applicants have to undergo an entrance procedure ... Usually you have to submit written documents, pass written or oral tests and take part in an interview"; master's requires "at least a 6 semester specialised relevant Bachelor's degree (min 180 ECTS)" at both sectors — [Study in Austria – Application and admission](https://studyinaustria.at/en/plan-your-studies/application-and-admission)
- Master's = at least 120 ECTS (2 years) after a bachelor's of at least 180 ECTS — [Study in Austria – Find your programme](https://studyinaustria.at/en/plan-your-studies/find-your-programme)
- Public-university "equivalence" model, TU Wien: application needed "as the equivalence to the relevant bachelor's programme offered at TU Wien has to be evaluated by the respective Dean of Academic Affairs"; differences are compensated by supplementary exams; "If the identified differences in subject matter amount to more than 30 ECTS credits, admission is unfortunately not possible"; "a maximum of 2 applications for admission per person" — [TU Wien – Other (inter-)national Bachelor's degree](https://www.tuwien.at/en/studies/admission/masters-programmes/other-inter-national-bachelors-degree)
- TU Wien: "At present, only the master's programmes 'Building Science and Environment' and 'Green Chemistry' have a limited number of places ... All other master's programmes offered at TU Wien are without selection procedure" — [TU Wien – Selection or assessment procedure](https://www.tuwien.at/en/studies/admission/masters-programmes/selection-or-assessment-procedure)
- Private universities: accredited, periodically re-accredited; examples Modul University Vienna, Webster Vienna Private University, Central European University (CEU), Sigmund Freud University — [Study in Austria – Private Universities](https://studyinaustria.at/en/study/institutions/private-universities)
- UAS interview is standard, FH OÖ: "An interview is part of the admission process for all Hagenberg Master's degree programmes" — [FH OÖ – Admission procedure Hagenberg](https://fh-ooe.at/en/application/admission-procedure-hagenberg); MCI: "all applicants will be invited to an online admission interview" and "the highest ranked applicants will be admitted after each round" (latter per search snippet) — [MCI – Application & Admission](https://www.mci.edu/en/study-at-mci/application-admission)
- UAS fees vary by institution for third-country nationals (examples read on official pages): FH Joanneum €726.72/semester (third country) — [FH Joanneum – International applicants master's](https://www.fh-joanneum.at/en/international/international-degree-seeking-students/admissions/international-applicants-masters-degree-programme/); CU Carinthia (FH Kärnten) Applied Data Science "Tuition for non-EU/EEA citizens: €500 + €26.20 ÖH fee (per semester)" — [CU Carinthia – Applied Data Science](https://www.cu-hochschule-kaernten.at/en/study/master/applied-data-science); FH Salzburg "tuition fees of 363€ per semester" plus a deposit for third-country applicants — [FH Salzburg – International Students FAQs](https://www.fh-salzburg.ac.at/en/prospective-students/about-to-start-studying/international-applicants/international-students-faqs); MCI "Non-EU students have to pay 8,250 euros tuition per semester", first-year €16,500 due within two weeks of acceptance — [MCI – Application & Admission](https://www.mci.edu/en/study-at-mci/application-admission)
- Public-university application is free at JKU ("There is no fee to submit an application for admission") and agencies are barred: "Applications submitted by an agency or placement organization are unlawful and will not be processed" — [JKU – Master's degree programs](https://www.jku.at/en/degree-programs/prospective-students/register-to-enroll/masters-degree-programs); TU Graz likewise rejects agency submissions under § 60(6) Universities Act — [TU Graz – Master's without admission procedure, international degree](https://www.tugraz.at/en/studying-and-teaching/studying-at-tu-graz/prospective-students/admission/masters-programme-without-admission-procedure-with-an-international-degree)
- UAS application deposits/fees for non-EU applicants: FH Joanneum €363.36 deposit + €10 processing fee (deposit credited/refundable) — [FH Joanneum](https://www.fh-joanneum.at/en/international/international-degree-seeking-students/admissions/international-applicants-masters-degree-programme/); IMC Krems €300 registration fee, credited to first tuition — [IMC Krems – Online application](https://www.imc.ac.at/en/study/application/online-application/); USTP St. Pölten deposit for evaluation of third-country documents — [USTP – International degree seeking students](https://www.ustp.at/en/international/international-degree-seeking-students)
- Part-time/extra-occupational example: CU Carinthia Applied Data Science runs "approx. 2 Saturdays/month & 5 working days per semester"; winter intake only — [CU Carinthia – Applied Data Science](https://www.cu-hochschule-kaernten.at/en/study/master/applied-data-science). FH Salzburg FAQ flags that full-time vs part-time can affect the visa — [FH Salzburg FAQs](https://www.fh-salzburg.ac.at/en/prospective-students/about-to-start-studying/international-applicants/international-students-faqs)

### Inferences
- For an Indian applicant with work experience: public universities reward a closely matching bachelor's curriculum (ECTS-by-subject comparison) and ignore work experience; UAS and WU-style selection procedures weigh CV, motivation letter and interview, so work experience counts there.
- UAS acceptance letters arrive on a predictable schedule after each round (interview dates are published), which makes residence-permit planning easier than the open-ended processing at public universities.

### Gaps
- Public-university tuition for third-country students and private-university fees were not researched here (other workstream); only incidental fee facts are cited.
- Whether a residence permit is granted for part-time/extra-occupational UAS programmes was not verified.

## 3. Semester dates, "general admission period", grace period (Nachfrist), and why non-EU deadlines are much earlier

### Takeaway
The academic year runs 1 October–30 September (winter semester from 1 Oct, summer semester from 1 Mar). The statutory general admission period ends 5 September / 5 February with a grace period to 31 October / 31 March (master's admission usually possible throughout the grace period), but that is only the *enrolment* window: third-country nationals must file the *application* in a separate, far earlier window that each university sets itself.

### Cited Findings
- "The academic year in Austria, as a rule, begins on October 1st and ends on September 30th"; winter semester from 1 October, summer semester 1 March–30 September — [Study in Austria – Find your programme](https://studyinaustria.at/en/plan-your-studies/find-your-programme)
- TU Wien: WS 2026/27 = 1 Oct 2026–28 Feb 2027; SS 2027 = 1 Mar–30 Sep 2027 — [TU Wien – Academic Calendar](https://www.tuwien.at/en/studies/admission/academic-calendar). Leoben: study year 2027/28 winter semester 01.10.2027–28.02.2028, summer semester 01.03.2028–30.09.2028 — [Montanuniversität Leoben – Dates & deadlines](https://ssc.unileoben.ac.at/en/dates-deadlines)
- OeAD general rule: "At universities the closing dates for application for the winter semester are September 5, and for the summer semester February 5. As it may take longer to review foreign documents ... submit your application as early as possible"; programmes with entrance exams can close "up to 6 months before the beginning of the semester"; UAS and private universities "have different application deadlines" — [Study in Austria – Application and admission](https://studyinaustria.at/en/plan-your-studies/application-and-admission)
- Grace/extension period illustrated (Innsbruck, study year 2026/27): general admission period bachelor/diploma 13.07.–05.09.2026 and 11.01.–05.02.2027; exceptions (Nachfrist) 06.09.–31.10.2026 and 06.02.–31.03.2027; master's admission period 13.07.–31.10.2026 and 11.01.–31.03.2027; but "Bewerbungsfrist für Drittstaatsangehörige und Staatenlose" 01.04.–15.05.2026 (WS) and 01.09.–15.10.2026 (SS) — [Uni Innsbruck – Bewerbungs- und Zulassungsfristen](https://www.uibk.ac.at/de/studienabteilung/zulassungsfristen/)
- Uni Wien separates "Application period" (when you apply) from "Admission period" (when you complete enrolment after getting the letter): "If you do not submit your application in time, you will not be able to complete the admission procedure" — [Uni Wien – Deadlines](https://studieren.univie.ac.at/en/applying-for-a-programme/deadlines)
- TU Wien: general admission period master's SS 2027 = 7 Jan–31 Mar 2027, but "Non-EU/EEA-citizen must submit the application for admission in complete form (with all documents and certifications) within this deadline. Otherwise, admission for the applied semester is not possible": SS 2027 = 16 Jul 2026–15 Jan 2027 — [TU Wien – Academic Calendar](https://www.tuwien.at/en/studies/admission/academic-calendar)
- JKU: non-EU citizens "must submit your application six months before the date you wish to start your studies" — [JKU – Master's degree programs](https://www.jku.at/en/degree-programs/prospective-students/register-to-enroll/masters-degree-programs)
- Uni Graz: deadlines "depend on the nationality of the applicants", not on where the documents were issued; applications filed in the wrong window are cancelled and the person blocked "from reapplying for 2 semesters" — [Uni Graz – Submission deadlines international students](https://internationale-studierende.uni-graz.at/en/submission-deadlines/)
- Some institutions key the early deadline to where the *degree* was obtained, not citizenship: Leoben ("For degrees from Third-country Applicants") — [Leoben](https://ssc.unileoben.ac.at/en/dates-deadlines); FH Technikum Wien ("*applies to the country in which the school or university degree was obtained") — [FH Technikum Wien – Application deadlines](https://www.technikum-wien.at/en/student-guide/admission-deadlines/); FH OÖ keys it to needing a visa — [FH OÖ – Application](https://fh-ooe.at/en/application-admission/application)
- BOKU and Salzburg: master's admission "can also take place outside of the admission period" / "is generally possible throughout the year" — but third-country applicants still have a fixed application cut-off — [BOKU – Admission](https://boku.ac.at/en/studienservices/themen/zulassung); [Uni Salzburg – Important dates and deadlines](https://www.plus.ac.at/studium/important-dates-and-deadlines/?lang=en)

### Inferences
- An Indian applicant should ignore "5 September / 31 October" style dates quoted on aggregator sites; those are enrolment periods. The binding date is the "non-EU / third-country application" line, typically 3–8 months before semester start.
- Indian citizens with Indian degrees fall under the early deadline under every definition used (citizenship, country of degree, visa requirement).

### Gaps
- I did not read the Universities Act (UG § 61) text itself; the 5 Sept/5 Feb + grace period structure is cited from university and OeAD pages.

## 6. How long do admission decisions take for non-EU applicants, and how does that interact with the residence permit?

### Takeaway
Public universities quote anything from "several weeks" to "10–12 weeks on average" to "several months" and refuse to give status updates; the residence permit then needs a personal application at the embassy, a 90-day decision period (extendable by another 90), and OeAD advises filing three to six months before entry. End to end, plan on roughly six to nine months from complete application to arrival.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- The competence check is a new hard prerequisite on the critical path for Uni Wien, TU Graz, JKU (and probably others); slot availability at VFS centres in India could be the binding constraint for the 16 Nov–14 Dec (Uni Graz) or 15 Dec (TU Graz) windows.

## 8. Given today is 10 Nov 2026: which intake is realistic, and what can be done now? (backward-planned timeline)

### Takeaway
Winter semester 2027/28 (start 1 October 2027) is the realistic target for someone starting from zero; a March 2027 start is still formally open at eight public universities but is very unlikely to work because the apostille, VFS competence check, university processing and the residence permit cannot all complete in under four months. The first hard deadline for the October 2027 intake is already 15 December 2026 (TU Graz/NAWI Graz restricted master's).

### Inferences
(All of this section is my synthesis from the dated facts above; none of it is a quoted official recommendation.)

**Is March 2027 feasible?** Formally yes at the eight universities listed; practically no for a from-zero applicant: (a) apostille of degree and transcripts in India plus the VFS competence check must precede the application; (b) decisions take weeks to months (TU Wien 10–12 weeks → an application posted in December yields a letter around March); (c) the residence permit then takes up to 90 days, while enrolment closes 31 March (30 April at TU Graz). Only someone who already holds apostilled documents, an English certificate, a competence-check slot this month, and applies in the first days of the window to a fast-deciding university has an outside chance, and would likely arrive late in the semester. Universities themselves say late applications roll to the next semester.

**Month-by-month plan to a 1 October 2027 start**
- **November 2026 (now):** Shortlist 6–10 programmes via studienwahl.at/programmes-in-english, fachhochschulen.ac.at and university catalogues; for each record selection procedure yes/no and the non-EU deadline from section 4. Check eligibility against the India rules (three-year-plus bachelor's, 60% threshold, university "Section"). Check passport validity through at least late 2028. Book an English test if no B2 proof exists (IELTS/TOEFL; FH Joanneum cites TOEFL 87 / PTE 59 as B2 equivalents). Start the apostille chain for degree certificate and transcripts (state attestation → MEA apostille). Book the VFS competence check for the first-choice university. Gather syllabus/course descriptions (TU Wien and TU Graz ask for them). Draft CV and motivation letters.
- **By 15 Dec 2026:** TU Graz/NAWI Graz restricted English master's (registration 15 Oct–15 Dec 2026) — requires the competence check first. Optionally Uni Graz/TU Wien/Uni Wien summer applications only if documents are already complete.
- **1 Dec 2026 onward:** IMC Krems portal opens; Salzburg EU Studies call opens.
- **By 8 Jan 2027:** WU priority deadline II (last for CEMS).
- **By 25 Jan 2027:** FH Joanneum (only deadline for applicants from India); pay deposit €363.36 + €10.
- **By 1 Feb / 7 Feb / 28 Feb 2027:** USTP St. Pölten; MCI round 2; FH Salzburg AISP/HCI; Klagenfurt International Management window I (11 Jan–28 Feb).
- **6 Feb – 31 Mar 2027:** JKU Linz window. **23 Feb – 30 Apr 2027:** Leoben window. **1 Mar – 6 Apr 2027:** Uni Wien selection-procedure master's. **By 8 Mar:** WU priority III. **By 31 Mar:** FH Technikum Wien. **By 1 Apr:** FH Salzburg. File TU Wien (paper, by post) and BOKU (from 1 Mar) in this period rather than at their July cut-offs.
- **April–May 2027:** Klagenfurt (1 Apr–30 Jun); Innsbruck (by 15 May); MCI round 3 (11 Apr); IMC (e.g. 15 Apr); FH OÖ (31 May); UAS interviews; re-check Uni Graz and Salzburg pages for their newly published WS 2027/28 dates (previous cycle: 11 May–8 Jun and 10 Aug).
- **31 May – 28 Jun 2027:** Uni Wien non-restricted master's window (apply on day one).
- **As soon as an admission letter arrives (target: by June 2027):** book the embassy/VFS appointment for the "Residence Permit – Student"; obtain police clearance (max three months old), proof of funds for 12 months, accommodation proof; pay any UAS tuition deposit.
- **July–September 2027:** permit decision (up to 90 days), Visa D, travel; housing.
- **1 October 2027:** semester starts; enrol in person by 31 October 2027 at the latest (Uni Wien admission period 12 Jul–31 Oct 2027).

### Gaps
